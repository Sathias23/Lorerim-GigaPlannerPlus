# Legends Of Aetherium

You follow an expedition team into **Itharzel**, "a lost Dwemer city in the rift", and recover lost Aetherium armor, weapons and crafting materials [3]. It is a single quest, **Legends Of Aetherium**. It runs through an excavation mine into a large chamber with a central tower and ends with a fight against the **Aetherial Colossus** for the **Aetherial Armory** [1].

## Starting in LoreRim
- **Hiring Notice** in any of four inns: **The Bee and Barb** (Riften), **The Frozen Hearth** (Winterhold), **Candlehearth Hall** (Windhelm) or **The Bannered Mare** (Whiterun) [2][3]. The notice is signed by **Amandia Screvius**. It offers "150 gold per day for guards and 50 per day for workers" and says the party may already have left for "the excavation site south of Riften near Crystaldrift Cave" [1].
- **Direct start:** travel to the **Itharzel Excavation Site** map marker, about 0.7 cells southeast of Crystaldrift Cave (derived from coordinates) [1][7]. The mod page says the site is "south of Riften and Crystaldrift Cave" [3].
- **Winterhold notice:** the mod page warns that a Winterhold city overhaul can make the Frozen Hearth notice unreachable [3]. LoreRim ships **The Great City of Winterhold** plus `TGC Winterhold - Legends of Aetherium Patch.esp`, which repositions the notice reference inside The Frozen Hearth [6]. If you still can't find it, use another inn or go to the site directly [3].
- **Level:** the author recommends 10–15+ [5]. LoreRim's Requiem patch makes every encounter top tier (see LoreRim notes), so expect it to be harder than the mod page suggests. That last point is an inference [4].

## Quests
### Legends Of Aetherium
- **Giver / trigger:** the Hiring Notice, or arriving at the site (`aaaLOAQuest`) [1].
- **Where:** Itharzel Excavation Site → Itharzel Excavation Mine → Itharzel Grand Chamber → the central tower → Itharzel Throne → Aetherial Armory [1].
- **Steps (objective text):** [1]
  1. Travel to the Itharzel excavation site. Optional: find Amandia Screvius.
  2. Explore the excavation site and go down into the mine to find the crew.
  3. Investigate the central tower in the large chamber.
  4. Find a way to reach the top of the tower. It is barred, and three levers around its base won't move until you investigate "the three other dwemer structures in the chamber". These are presumably the Manufactorium, Chironasium and Siphonasium cells.
  5. Enter the throne room.
  6. Defeat the Aetherial Colossus, a Dwemer centurion guarding an armory.
  7. Find the Aetherial Armoury.
- **Rewards:** the Aetherial Armory's contents (below) [1][3].

## Locations
- **Itharzel Excavation Site:** exterior map marker, south of Riften [1][3].
- **Interior cells:** Itharzel Excavation Mine, Itharzel Grand Chamber, Itharzel Manufactorium, Itharzel Chironasium, Itharzel Mage's Quaters (sic), Itharzel Siphonasium, Itharzel Throne and Aetherial Armory [1].
- **Enemies:** Aetherial Spiders, Aetherial Spheres, Aetherial Centurions, an Aetherial Fire Forge Master, the Aetherial Mage and the Aetherial Colossus [1].

## Rewards & notable items
- **Armor and weapons:** an Aetherial Armour set (helmet, armor, gauntlets, boots), Aetherial Sword, Bow, Greatsword and Warhammer, Aetherial Mage Armor (robes and hood), **Aetherium Ingot**, **Raw Aetherium** and the **Dwarven Pickaxe** [1][3].
- **Tiers:** in the base mod, items come in four tiers by level: generic (0–19), **Adorned** (20–29), **Enriched** (30–39) and **Ascended** (40+). They can be "improved to higher levelled versions at the forge" [3].
- **Spell tomes:** Conjure Aetherial Spider and Conjure Aetherial Sphere [1].

## LoreRim notes
- **Requiem unleveling (LoreRim patch):** `Legends of Aetherium - Requiem Patch.esp` cuts every leveled list in the mod to one entry [4].
  - Loot lists always give the **Ascended** item: Ascended Aetherial Sword, Bow, Greatsword, Warhammer, Helmet, Armor, Gauntlets and Boots [1][4].
  - Creature lists always spawn the **Master** variants: Aetherial Sphere Master, Aetherial Spider Master and Aetherial Centurion Master [1][4].
  - The patch also overrides the mod's armor, weapons, NPCs, enchantments, conjuration spells and tempering recipes. These are presumably Requiem balance changes; the individual values were not checked [4].
- **Magic Redone patch:** adds the Dwarven Pickaxe to the vanilla `MineOreToolsList`, so it works as a mining tool [4].
- **Asset patches:** Metallurgy retextures the Aetherium Ingot and Ore [6]. Ancient Dwemer Metal recolors the Dwemer textures [6]. A Lux lighting patch also ships [6].
