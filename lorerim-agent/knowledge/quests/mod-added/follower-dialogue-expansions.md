# Follower Dialogue Expansion (FDE) series

The FDE series is a set of separate mods, one per vanilla character, by a single author; each adds hundreds to thousands of AI-voiced lines (quest awareness, location/weather awareness, approval systems, romance lines) and some add personal quests or make the character recruitable for the first time [3][4][5][6][7]. LoreRim ships 24 of them in the "Gameplay - Followers" separator plus Olfina Gray-Mane in "Gameplay - Lines Expansions & Dialogue", along with cross-follower banter patches for Auri, Inigo, Lucien and Remiel [1][9]. Only five of the shipped plugins (Erik, Faralda, Jenassa, Mjoll, Fura) carry journal quests [1][2]. The LoreRim site describes this generally ("many followers that already existed in game have been expanded upon with additional quest commentary and new dialogue") but does not name FDE [8].

## Quests
### With Love, Eilonwy, Part 1 (Faralda)
- **Giver:** Faralda, Professor of Destruction [1].
- **Steps** [1]: 1. Bring Faralda's family letter to Vekel the Man (Ragged Flagon, Riften). 2. Talk to Faralda. 3. Continue traveling with Faralda. 4. Return to the College of Winterhold with Faralda. 5. Talk to Faralda. 6. Continue traveling with Faralda.
- **Outcome:** Faralda receives a reply from her aunt Eilonwy [1].

### With Love, Eilonwy, Part 2 (Faralda)
- **Steps** [1]: 1. Talk to Faralda. 2. Meet Faralda's aunt at Lost Prospect Mine. 3. (Optional) Attack Merandil now. 4. Kill Merandil. 5. Talk to Faralda. 6. Meet Faralda back at the College of Winterhold.
- **Choices & outcomes:** Faralda's brother Merandil turns out to be Thalmor trying to force her back to Alinor; kill him, or let him take her back to Alinor (stage 120 — she leaves) [1].
- FDE Faralda also overrides the vanilla College radiant "Research Thief" (FreeformWinterholdCollegeA), presumably her research-notes recruitment step [2][5].

### Erik the Shield Brother (Erik the Slayer)
- **Steps** [1]: 1. Speak to Aela about Erik joining the Companions. 2. Clear a (radiant) location with Erik as his trial. 3. Talk to the quest giver. 4. Officially make Erik a Companion.
- **Outcome:** Erik joins the Companions [1][4].

### Aerin the Man (Mjoll the Lioness)
- **Steps** [1]: 1. Clear a (radiant) dungeon with Aerin and Mjoll. 2. Watch Aerin's confession to Mjoll. 3. Talk to Aerin / Talk to Mjoll.
- **Outcome:** Aerin proves himself and wins Mjoll's love; alternate stages record Mjoll's or Aerin's death [1].

### Shadows of the Past (Jenassa romance)
- **Steps** [1]: 1. Travel to the tower where Jenassa met her first love. 2. Kill the bandits or let the beasts finish the bandits. 3. Talk to Jenassa at the tower. 4. Talk to Jenassa at Markarth.
- **Outcome:** "Jenassa has allowed herself to heal from the wounds of the past." [1] The mod page calls this her romance quest [6].

### Blood Ties (Fura Bloodmouth)
- **Steps** [1]: 1. Bring Fura to her mother (in Solitude). 2. Talk to Fura. 3. Stay with Fura. 4. Kill Angeline, as a true Volkihar.
- **Outcomes:** reunite Fura and her mother; the journal records the mother's death, either by you "as a true Volkihar" or otherwise [1].

### Bloodmouth (Fura Bloodmouth)
- **Steps** [1]: 1. Use Vampire's Seduction to tell Legate Cipius (Whiterun) to go to Cold Rock Pass. 2. Meet Fura at Cold Rock Pass. 3. Witness the confrontation. 4. Let the Death Hounds kill Cipius or try to save Cipius.
- **Choices & outcomes:** let Fura have her revenge, or persuade her to let Cipius go [1].
- FDE Fura also contains a non-start-enabled QUST "Kindred Judgment" (EditorID `DLC1VQ08Test`) duplicating vanilla Dawnguard objectives with an alternate "reshape the prophecy" journal line; whether it is ever player-facing is unverified [1][2].

## LoreRim notes
- All listed FDE plugins are enabled in the Default profile; none is mastered by a LoreRim-made patch [9].
- FDE Olfina overrides vanilla Whiterun scenes (Olfina/Jon scenes 1–3, Bannered Mare convo) [2]; the author lists it as incompatible with "IDE Whiterun" [3].
- FDE Lydia is incompatible with "IFD Lydia" per its author [3]. Jenassa can be reverted to her older Dark Brotherhood personality via console (`set aaJenassaDBEnabled to 1` then `addtofaction IvarsteadKlimmekFaction` on her) [6].
- LoreRim also installs "(SHAPE YOUR QUEEN) Follower Dialogue Expansion - Elisif the Fair" (v1.0.8.0) and an "FDE Mjoll Auri patch"; they are not covered by this file [9].
- The author's own spreadsheet of per-follower features is linked from the mod pages (not fetched) [3].
