# The Gray Cowl of Nocturnal (10th Anniversary)

MannyGT's DLC-sized quest mod, which the author estimates at 8–15+ hours, is built for thieves and assassins [2]. You track down the Gray Cowl of Nocturnal, last worn by the Champion of Cyrodiil at the end of the Third Era [2][3]. The quests run through Skyrim, an island in the Coldharbour realm, and a hidden, isolated region of Hammerfell's Alik'r Desert, each a separate worldspace [1][2]. LoreRim ships the 10th-anniversary rebuild (`Gray Fox Cowl.esm`, v1.4.0.0) plus a set of patches and add-ons [1][16].

**Don't confuse it with the Creation Club "The Gray Cowl Returns!".** LoreRim also loads that CC (`ccbgssse020-graycowl.esl`). Its quest is named "The Gray Cowl of Nocturnal" and is a separate Riften/Solitude questline about the Gray Fox [14][15]. Everything below is about the MannyGT mod. Its main quest is **"The Call of Gray Cowl of Nocturnal"** [1].

## Starting in LoreRim
- **LoreRim gate:** you must first complete the Thieves Guild questline, then steal an item [3]. The install backs this up. `Gray Fox Cowl - Under New Management Start.esp` overrides the mod's start trigger, a Story Manager node named `manny_GF_Steal`. Its only condition becomes `GetStage TGLeadership == 200`, where TGLeadership is the vanilla "Under New Management" quest [4][6]. Stage 200 of Under New Management is a quest-finishing stage ("I've become the Guild Master of the Thieves Guild") [6].
- **The mod's default trigger** is any steal or pickpocket at player level 10 or higher [2]. The original node carries a level ≥ 10 check (`GetLevel >= 10`) plus event-data checks. The LoreRim override replaces that whole condition list, so the level check is not in the active record [4]. In practice the Thieves Guild completion is the binding gate (medium confidence on the in-game effect).
- The same patch rewrites one dialogue line. "Yes, it's happened after I've stolen something." becomes "Yes, it happened after I restored the Thieves Guild." [4]
- The Under New Management start file comes from the Nexus mod "There is no (CC) Gray Cowl". That mod's author says the file turns Gray Cowl into an endgame quest for the Thieves Guild [5]. LoreRim does not ship that mod's main CC-disabling plugin: the CC Gray Cowl esl is still in the load order [5][14][16].
- **What happens when it fires:** you get a vision (journal: "I had a strange vision in my mind. Also I know where to go now.") and the objective "Reach the place" [1]. A secondary wiki says the vision points to Seviana Umbranox's house, north-west of the Nordic ruin Valthume (low confidence; unverified) [13].
- Author's advice: good Sneak (most important) and Pickpocket. Some side-quest bosses are tough [2].

## Quests
### The Call of Gray Cowl of Nocturnal
- **Giver / trigger:** a theft after the LoreRim gate, which brings the vision [1][4].
- **Where:** Skyrim (Seviana's House, Hall of The Initiation), then Coldharbour, then the Alik'r Desert (Ben Erai, Al Shedim, Lair of the Ancestral Cheetahs, Oasis of Mora Sul, Mausoleum) [1].
- **Steps (plugin objectives):** 1) Reach the place; 2) Find the Hall of the Initiation; 3) Reach the other side; 4) Get the four keys; 5) Follow Arenar; 6) Speak with Arenar; 7) Speak with Syloria; 8) Find a way to enter the Mausoleum; 9) Get into position and shoot the Arrow; 10) Return to Seviana [1].
- **Story beats (journal):** you escape Coldharbour, then meet Arenar and follow him to his village in the desert [1]. Lady Syloria (NPC "Syloria Peleu" of the Fortress of Ben Erai) sends you after two items [1][7]:
  - the key to the Mora Sul entrance, in **Al Shedim**, north of Ben Erai;
  - the **Alliance of Ancestral Cheetahs** amulet, at the far north of the region [1].
- **Endgame (journal):** the Mausoleum of the Champion of Cyrodiil is in the **Oasis of Mora Sul**, on the region's east side. Inside, stand on the small rug near the bow and fire the **Arrow of Extrication** (in-game item name) at the bluish disc opposite. A door then opens on the right side of the Mausoleum [1][7]. Take the Cowl and return to Seviana through the magic portal [1].
- A secondary wiki says the Hall of the Initiation sits behind the "Eye of Cyrodiil", south-west of Bloodlet Throne (low confidence; unverified) [13].
- **Rewards:** the Gray Cowl of Nocturnal (final journal entry: "I've got the legendary Gray Cowl of Nocturnal.") [1]. See Rewards below.

### Reward of Coldharbour
- **Trigger:** part of the main quest's Coldharbour section (daedric-type quest) [1].
- **Steps:** Escape from Coldharbour; (Optional) Do not kill the Guardians of Coldharbour [1]. Journal: "I must escape from Coldharbour and maybe I'll do it silently." [1]

### The Curse of Sadraaka
- **Where:** the Forgotten City in the Alik'r Desert, which is full of Alik'r Warrior ghosts [1].
- **Steps:** Find information about the Forgotten City; Kill Sadraaka and free the Forgotten City from the curse [1].
- **Reward hint:** the plugin defines a "Spell Tome: Conjure Forgotten Draugr" (spell "Conjure Sadraaka the Forgotten Draugr"). Its link to this quest is inferred from the name (unverified) [7].

### Time for the Farewells
- **Steps:** Escort Seviana and Luvien to the docks of Solitude [1]. This epilogue matters for the Hammerfell Quests Bundle: its Solstheim quest unlocks only after the Solitude Docks ending (see [hammerfell-quests-bundle.md](hammerfell-quests-bundle.md)).

### Bury Valen Dreth
- **Steps:** Bury the rests of Valen Dreth at Falkreath (misc quest) [1].

### The Experiment
- **Giver:** Ilrav Baenius (the journal also spells him "Irlav") [1][7].
- **Steps:** Place the artifact near Markarth, Falkreath, Windhelm and Dawnstar; Report to Ilrav the experiment results [1]. The journal warns: "something went wrong with the experiment... I was almost killed!" [1]
- **Reward:** the amulet **Irlav's Penitence** (record `manny_GF_Amulet_IrlavExperimentReward`). Its enchantment combines resist fire, frost and shock with fortify conjuration [7].

### Memories of the Umbranox
- **Steps:** Find all the journals (a counter), then deliver them to Seviana [1]. There are six volumes of "Caio's Journal" [7]. Per the author, the 4th can only be found in chests inside destroyed houses in Coldharbour [2].
- **Outcomes:** deliver all for **1000 gold** or **a powerful spell tome**, or keep them. Missing any journal fails the quest (stage 200: "I surely missed some journals of Caio Umbranox.") [1]. So collect every journal before you leave Coldharbour.

### Claim Shanta Filibb
- **Trigger:** the notice note "Clear out Shanta Filibb" (see the notice-board note below), or being attacked by unknown bandits [1][10].
- **Steps:** Kill all the unknown attackers; Kill all the bandits (at Shanta Filibb); Check for a reward (on the Ben Erai notice board); Get your reward from Crassius; Buy furnitures for your new home [1].
- **Reward:** the player home **Shanta Filibb** [1].

### Ben Erai notices and Missives (repeatable)
- The mod puts five pre-set notes on a Ben Erai notice board:
  - Desert Wolf Pelts
  - Praetorian Dynamo Cores
  - "I want my amulet back!" (Metilius)
  - Duneripper Blood Phials
  - Clear Shanta Filibb [7][10]
- **In LoreRim**, `GrayCowlMissivesNoNoticeBoard.esp` removes that board and moves the notes to the Missives board in Ben Erai, renamed:
  - "Missive: Collect Desert Wolf Pelts"
  - "Missive: Collect Praetorian Dynamo Cores"
  - "Missive: Retrieve Stolen Amulet"
  - "Missive: Collect Duneripper Blood"
  - "Missive: Clear out Shanta Filibb" [10]
- `Missives - Gray Cowl Patch.esp` adds that Missive Board to Ben Erai [9]. It generates the standard radiant Missives jobs for the Alik'r Basin, for example "Deliver a Letter Near/Medium/Distant", "Gather Rare Ore", "Collect Some Weak Soul Gems", "Kill Some Bandits", "Kill a Dragon" and "Retrieve From a Ruin" (27 templates) [9]. `MissivesGrayCowlMap.esp` adds a map to the Alik'r board [12]. For the general Missives system, see [missives.md](missives.md).

### Unjournaled content
The plugin also has background quests with no journal text. Among them are a Pit arena (`manny_GF_ThePit`, "The Pit Opponents fight"), a Sayara quest, a Yokuda controller, Sheogorath scenes and an Irlav favour [1]. The author describes **The Pit** as a place "where you can bet or fight", and mentions desert shrines with unique blessings [2]. The plugin's blessing spells:
- Blessing of Warm Dunes
- Blessing of Mighty Redguard
- Blessing of Magnificient Sprint (spelled this way in the plugin)
- Blessing of Sun Healer
- Blessing of Light Skin
- Blessing of Night Healer [7]

## Locations

## Rewards & notable items
All item names are from plugin records.
- **Gray Cowl of Nocturnal** (`manny_GF_Armor_GrayCowl`). Its enchantment record has Fortify Sneak (25), a Fortify Carry Weight effect (200, as defined in the ESM) and "Gray Cowl Detect Life" [7]. LoreRim's Requiem patch re-balances the armor record [7].
- **Bounty while cowled** (Bounty fix mod): wearing the Cowl gives you a Gray Fox bounty of 500 (non-violent) in every hold. Crimes committed while wearing it add to that bounty. You can pay it off or go to jail. Being seen equipping or unequipping the Cowl merges your own bounty with the Gray Fox's [11]. This replaces the original mod's bounty behaviour [11].

## LoreRim notes
- **Start gate:** Thieves Guild completion ("Under New Management"), enforced by `Gray Fox Cowl - Under New Management Start.esp` [3][4]. The LoreRim site and the install agree.
- **Requiem balance:** LoreRim's own `Gray Cowl of Nocturnal - Requiem Patch.esp` (in "LoreRim - xEdit64 Output") overrides the mod's weapons, armor, spells, spell tomes, containers, factions and crafting recipes for Requiem [7].
- **Artifact sacrifice (LoreRim script):** LoreRim's artifact-sacrifice script treats the mod's Gray Cowl as a Nocturnal artifact and its Umbra as a Clavicus Vile artifact. When the item is removed via the forge hook (`OnForgeItemRemoved`), worship of that deity is blocked, and any active worship of it ends [8].
- **Two Gray Cowls exist in LoreRim:** this mod's and the CC "The Gray Cowl Returns!" (quest "The Gray Cowl of Nocturnal", Riften graveyard start). LoreRim gives the CC NPCs voiced narration [14][15]. `SKYBLIVION - Umbra` sits in this mod's separator but only replaces the model of the *Creation Club* Umbra found in Champion's Rest [12].
- **Cosmetic and compatibility add-ons** [12]:
  - NPCs PLUS: face improvements for 26 NPCs, no plugin
  - Retexture plus Parallax Gen
  - NAT III weather patch
  - FLM "Immersive interaction" ini
  - Moveable Lanterns and Signs
  - Daedric Shrines AIO patch
  - DynDOLOD TexGen root-block fix
- **Bounty fix** ships scripts only (no plugin) [11][16].
- **Author warnings:** uninstalling mid-save is unsupported. Updating a save from the old SE version mid-quest can CTD or teleport you to the wrong location [2].
