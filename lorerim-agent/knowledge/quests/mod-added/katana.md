# Katana - Journey in the Shadows (Katana, Megara, Shale)

The mod adds three human-voiced companions with 8k+ dialogue lines: **Katana**, "a rogue shrouded in mystery"; **Megara**, a healer; and **Shale**, a spellsword [1]. Its quests are a personal arc about Katana and her pursuer River (**Chasing the Current**, then **The Ravens' Lament**), a wedding quest (**Serenata**) and several light-hearted side quests spread across the major towns [2][3]. All three companions are essential, start at level 10 and then level with you, use their own follower system, can cook, and each has a summon spell and a map-tracker item [3].

## Starting in LoreRim
- **Where:** "Head over to the Winking Skeever. Katana's upstairs, probably eating." [1][3] LoreRim ships the mod's *Great City of Solitude* compatibility plugin for that location [4].
- **Start:** on a first playthrough choose "So, what brings you to Skyrim?" [3]. Katana has an affinity system, "which is why she isn't initially the warmest" [3].
- **Skips:** since v3.0 you can skip Chasing the Current if you pass Katana's test, and the test can also skip all of the mod's quests so you get to Shale directly [3].
- **Recruiting the other two:** Megara can be recruited at the Drunken Huntsman (Whiterun) after Chasing the Current, or by passing Katana's test. Shale can be recruited at the Bee and Barb (Riften) after The Ravens' Lament, or by passing the test that skips all quests [3].
- **LoreRim gates:** I found no LoreRim delayed start or level gate. The LoreRim site gives only the Winking Skeever start [1]. LoreRim's LoreCut patch **removes the player's "I'm not from this world." intro option** [4][5].
- **Open Cities:** the author calls it "INCOMPATIBLE-ish" unless you skip all quests with the quiz. LoreRim's Default profile doesn't contain Open Cities, so this doesn't apply [3][4].

## Quests
### Chasing the Current (main, part 1)
- **Giver / trigger:** Katana, once she's ready to share her plan. "Talk to Katana when you have a moment" [2].
- **Steps** [2]:
  1. Head to the Drunken Huntsman, sit at a table and wait to be approached.
  2. Defeat Lucky Irnsvar at Silent Moons Camp near Whiterun.
  3. Sit at a table in the Silver-Blood Inn (Markarth) and wait for Megara. Read Lucky Irnsvar's letter.
  4. Defeat Galtun Bold-Thief at Kolskeggr Mine near Markarth.
  5. Meet Megara at the Bee and Barb in Riften and read Galtun Bold-Thief's Note.
  6. Defeat Runir Wulfhart at Broken Helm Hollow near Riften.
  7. Head to the Frozen Hearth (Winterhold) for the thief's location. Someone seems to be watching you; talk to Katana.
  8. Go to a house near Fort Fellhammer and confront River. Talk to Katana.
- **Outcome:** "River got away, but it seems Katana's made peace with it." The quest ends with a celebratory drink at the Drunken Huntsman, where Megara can be recruited [2][3].
- **Author warning:** don't read the bandits' letters until they are an active objective, or the quest breaks (Nexus article quoted in search results; low confidence) [6].
- **Unlocks for Katana:** a combat shade near bleedout, lockpicking in select places, her own off-screen bounties, scouting, and Shadow Mode (you go invisible and your movement is muffled) [3].

### The Ravens' Lament (main, part 2)
- **Steps** [2]: talk to Katana about the Bee and Barb → go there with Katana and Megara → search for traces of River and take River's note → choose between Vilemyr Inn (Katana's guess) and Fallowstone Cave (Megara's guess) → find River and Shale → defeat River's summons → follow Megara, then Shale, on foot → "Defeat River and her friends" (hagravens) → head to the Bee and Barb and talk with Katana, Megara and Shale.
- **Outcome:** "River's gone… We saved Shale, the shade saved us all, and Katana's free of River." Shale is now recruitable [2][3].

### Serenata (Katana & Megara's wedding)
- **Trigger:** "With Katana and Shale, talk to Megara in a city during the day" [2]. The author's notes say new dialogue unlocks after The Ravens' Lament and Serenata [5].
- **Steps** [2]: journey with Shale for about a day → a courier delivers the "Note from Katana and Megara" → read it with Shale → use the **Scroll to Secret Place** → find Katana and Megara → "Enjoy the ceremony" (Chadryn officiates) → mingle and tell Megara when you're ready to leave → adventure with Shale during the honeymoon → talk to Megara on their return.

### The Sweetest Carrot (Megara)
- Head to Chillfurrow Farm, steal the carrot with Megara, go to the Bannered Mare, give Megara time, then check in. You can refuse to help (stage 69) [2]. The author recommends meeting Chadryn first [5].

### Honey Nuts and the Chin of Fate
- After "dealing with Chadryn": head to Solitude with Katana and Megara, stop by Honningbrew Meadery, look for "a special plant near Riften", then walk to the bridge in Riverwood [2].

### Sugar and Spice (Katana)
- Head to Kilerth's Rest with Katana, "get comfortable and close your eyes", then go to the coast by Katla's Farm with her [2].

### The Khat's Eye
- Relax at Moorside Inn (Morthal) with Katana and Megara. Look near Movarth's Lair for Al'Tharo's spectacles, defeat the Thalmor spies, and return the spectacles [2].

### Megara's Radiant Quests
- Repeatable bounties: "Kill the leader of the bandits at <BanditLocation>", then collect the reward from Megara [2][3].

### Meeting Katana / Companion Quests
- *Meeting Katana* logs "Katana recruited." The *Katana / Megara / Shale Companion Quest* entries are "<Name> is waiting for you / Find <Name>" trackers for a dismissed companion [2].

## Locations
- **Kilerth's Rest** (`AK69RiverHideoutLocation`) — River's hideout. Its record ID, together with Chasing the Current's "house near Fort Fellhammer", suggests this is the house where you confront River (inference, medium). Sugar and Spice later returns there [2].
- **The Garden** (`AK69CozyLocation`) — a new location in Katana.esp. Its purpose isn't documented in the sources I found. It may be the "secret place" the Serenata scroll takes you to (unverified) [2].
- **River's Camp** (`AK69RiverCampLocation`) — a location added by the LoreRim LoreCut patch [4]. The Ravens' Lament mentions "getting her away from River's camp" [2].
- Vanilla venues used: Winking Skeever, Drunken Huntsman, Silver-Blood Inn, Bee and Barb, Frozen Hearth, Vilemyr Inn, Fallowstone Cave, Moorside Inn, Bannered Mare [2]. LoreRim ships the mod's patches for JK's Drunken Huntsman, JK's Bee and Barb, COTN Winterhold/Morthal, Great City of Solitude/Winterhold and Great Town of Ivarstead [4].

## Rewards & notable items
- Companion features that unlock after Chasing the Current (see above). Megara heals party members individually, cures diseases for garlic, and runs a combat potion-buff system ("Megara's Potion Buffs for Combat" book). Shale taunts enemies and carries a magical storage chest [3][4].
- Summon spells: **Summon Katana / Summon Megara / Summon Shale** [2].
- Notable weapons in the LoreRim patch: *Koroshi*, *Tsukikage*, *Staff of Adrana* and *Katana's Bow* (replacing *Katana's Rifle*) [4].

## LoreRim notes
The **Katana - Raven Replacer - LoreCut - Reqtificated** patch (hosted on Nexus page 132964, which the Katana author lists under "Lorerim") does the following [3][5]:
- "This has already been Reqtificated" (Requiem integration).
- Forwards the Raven Replacer appearance.
- Changes Katana's rifle to an ebony bow and removes lines about bullets or the rifle.
- Removes the player's option to say they're from another world.
- Companions no longer thank you for heals.
- Disables Megara's ingredient pick-up and nerfs her potion buffs.
- Removes the option to tell Shale you like him, which disables his flirt route (record `AK69ShaleFlirtTopic` "I think I like you." is overridden) [4].
- Katana's lockpicking is only Adept.
- Megara keeps vanilla torch behaviour.

Comments about Irin and Adrana are not removed [5]. The patch overrides 170 dialogue INFOs, 26 NPCs and many spells and armours [4].
