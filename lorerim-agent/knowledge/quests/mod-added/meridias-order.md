# Meridia's Order

Meridia's Order is an expansive questline by TheOscar0. It adds a new faction, followers, bosses, locations and artifacts, and has "over 15 new quests, most of which have different ways to complete them" [2]. You help **High Priestess Alara** and her paladins stop **The Malevolent**, a necromancer group led by **Dark Master Fiya**, who wants to raise **Lamae Bal**, the First Vampire [1]. The plot takes you through infiltrating a rival cult (the Dark Dawn), a traitor in the Order, and a Coldharbour pocket realm. Many quests let you choose between stealth, persuasion and violence [1][2]. The LoreRim site lists it on its New Quests page [3].

## Starting in LoreRim
- **Prerequisite:** finish the vanilla Daedric quest **The Break of Dawn** (`DA09`) [2][3]. The mod page says that quest unlocks at level 12 and starts from Meridia's Beacon or by travelling to her statue near Solitude [2]. In LoreRim, `DA09` is overridden by USSEP, Requiem and *Wintersun and Daedric Shrines replacer*, so its start conditions may differ from vanilla (see related) [5].
- **Then either:**
  - wait in any major city until a paladin of the Order approaches you [2]; the journal names her: "I have been approached by **Paladin Marith** of Meridia's Order" [1]. Or:
  - go to **Meridia's Sanctum**, "near Rimerock Burrow, far west of Solitude", and speak with **High Priestess Alara** [2].
- **LoreRim recommendation:** level **25+**. "Beware, because Lorerim is based around Requiem, the undead are no joke" [3].
- No LoreRim plugin overrides the mod's quest records (`NMeridia*`) [6].

## Quests
Quest names, objectives and outcomes come from the plugin's journal records [1]. They are listed in story order.

### Meridia's Order
- **Steps:** 1) Speak with High Priestess Alara at Meridia's Sanctum. 2) Kill the Necromancer in Solitude. 3) Return to Alara [1].
- **Notes:** the necromancer is **Malfyur the Unliving** (NPC record [8]). A secondary wiki for a different modlist says he can summon a Bone Colossus, that killing him gives a 1,000-gold bounty, and that his ghost attacks back at the Sanctum [7]. Those details are low confidence.

### Our Lady
- Follow Alara to **Meridia's Shrine**. Her attempt to commune with Meridia fails, and necromancers attack. Kill them, then talk to Alara about their plans [1].

### Prisoner
- Alara wants the staff **Summoner's Demise**, held by the Wood Elf **Findar** [1].
- Steps: ask Order members about Findar. Marith sends you to Findar's mother in a shack outside Solitude. Find Findar in the **Dragonsreach Dungeon** in Whiterun and help him escape, or bail him out [1].
- If Findar dies, his mother points you on. Either way, you retrieve the staff from **Golvunter**, a ruin just outside Riften, and return to Alara [1].
- **Reward:** you keep *Summoner's Demise* for the next task [1].

### Infiltration
- **Arch-Paladin Laracin** sends you to **Deep Moon Lookout** to join a necromancer group called the **Dark Dawn**. The recruiter **Dralos** orders you to kill **Adelaide** [1].
- **Choices:** kill her, reason with them, or kill the necromancers and get the information from Adelaide instead [1].
- Then gain access to the **Dark Dawn Sanctuary** and learn the plan by searching documents, winning members' favour, or fighting through. The plan is to raise the First Vampire created by Molag Bal [1].
- When you return, the Sanctum has been attacked by **The Malevolent**, and **Acolyte Kariss** is dead [1].

### The Dark Dawn (side branch of Infiltration)
- Gain the favour of the Sanctuary Master **Reeshi** [1]:
  - kill or warn a **Markarth Court Member**;
  - deliver a sealed letter to a necromancer courier (you may open it);
  - receive a key to Reeshi's chamber holding the evidence [1].
- If you turn on the Dark Dawn, you "may have to fight [your] way through the sanctuary" [1].

### Fort Justice
- Initiate **Velteth** sends you to **Fort Justice**, an abandoned outpost in the Rift. There you confront **Fiya** and wipe out her forces [1].
- Attend Kariss's funeral, which is ambushed. Kill the necromancers, then meet Alara and the others at Fort Justice [1].

### A New Day
- At Fort Justice, Alara gives you a letter addressed to "the Champion". Meet its author at **Old Hroldan Inn** in the Reach: **Vaegra** of the Necromancer Union [1].
- After an attack, travel to Vaegra's home. She warns that a Dark Elf has infiltrated the Order [1].
- **Choice:** confront **Garin**, whom Alara suspects. Garin names **Velteth**, who confesses to being a Malevolent plant and is executed [1].
- **Meridia** appears and speaks with you [1].

### The Way In
- To enter Fiya's pocket realm, you need someone who has been there. Recruit Marith and search the **College of Winterhold**, then the road near **Dawnstar**, for the defector **Orthelos** [1].
- Bring him to Garin, whose ritual lets you enter **Coldharbour** [1].

### The Pocket Realm
- Inside Fiya's pocket realm of Coldharbour, destroy the realm, escape through Garin, and report to Alara [1].
- You can engage Fiya, work with her servants, or attack everything [1].

### A Fate Worse Than Death (realm branch)
- "Play her games" with Fiya [1]. Objectives include "Kill Marith or Alara" and "Kill **Cleans-Many-Boots**", or you can investigate another way [1].
- The branch ends with Fiya fleeing once the realm is damaged [1].

### The Jester, In The Bedroom, With The Knife (realm branch)
- **The Jester** asks you to turn **Alzacar** against Fiya and to kill her advisor **Kelrog** [1].
- Alzacar reveals that the realm is destroyed by killing a conjured dog named **Molag** in the **Master Bedroom** [1].

### The Ritual (finale)
- Interrupt Fiya's ritual at her hideaway. You are pulled into **Lamae Bal**'s crypt and must destroy Lamae Bal to escape. Then speak with Alara and Meridia [1].
- **Reward:** "Meridia has granted me use of the Ring of Khajiiti", and Alara welcomes you to stay with the Order [1]. See LoreRim notes for the ring's in-game name.

### Member side quests
Each of these has a "rejection stage" and makes the member available to you afterwards [1].

| Quest | Giver | What happens | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stendarr's Mercy** | Arch-Paladin Laracin | Assassinate the Vigilant leader of **Stendarr's Beacon**, or convince Laracin to let you try a truce | If you help, Laracin "offered his services". You can also refuse [1] |
| **Repentance Upheld** | Initiate Garin Anathri | Confront **Rosette**, leader of his old cult, in Windhelm | Garin offers his services [1] |
| **Champion's Choice** | Paladin Asger | A noble in Riften (**Arandar**) accuses **Orfoljt** in the Ratway of being a warlock; decide whom to believe | Asger offers his services, or asks you to keep your distance [1] |
| **Finding Yourself** | Acolyte Una | Escort her to Alara, Meridia's Shrine and the Sanctum; kill a vampire | She becomes a trader [1] |
| **A Parting Gift** | Initiate Valrar | Recover **Valrar's Family Ring** from his ex-partner without harming them | He becomes a trader and disease healer and gives a free daily potion. He shuns you if you killed the ex, or leaves the Order if you persuade him to [1] |

## Rewards & notable items
Item names come from the plugin; LoreRim names are given where they differ [8].
- **Ring of Khajiiti** (finale reward). The plugin has two rings: `NMeridiaRingofKhajiiti` "Ring of Khajiiti" and `NMeridiaRingofKhajiitiEnhanced` "Mighty Ring of Khajiiti". **LoreRim renames them** to "Lesser Ring of Khajiiti" and "Ring of Khajiiti" respectively (`Aetherium Forge - Perk Potions - TGC.esp`) [4]. Which record the finale grants is unverified.
- **Mighty Dawnbreaker** (weapon record; how you get it is unverified) and **Summoner's Demise** (staff) [8].
- Apparel [8]:
  - Jester's Clothes ("Prices are 12% better, and damage with One-Handed weapons is 12% better"), Boots, Gloves and Hat
  - Dark Master Robes, Boots and Gloves
  - Blessed Robes and Blessed Hood
  - Dark Dawn Robes, Malevolent Robes and Necromancer Union Robes
  - Ring of Golvunter, Idina's Ring and Sentimental Ring
- **Followers and services:** Laracin, Garin and Asger "offer their services". Una and Valrar become traders. Valrar also heals diseases and gives a daily potion [1]. The mod page advertises new followers [2].

## LoreRim notes
- **Artifact sacrifice (LoreRim-specific):** LoreRim's Aetherium Forge recipe `AetheriumForge_AE_Khajiit` turns the enhanced ring (`NMeridiaRingofKhajiitiEnhanced`) into **Insight of Aetherius**, a perk potion [4].
- LoreRim's *Convert artifacts to perk points* script (`LoreRim Artifact Sacrifice.esp`) counts that ring, alongside Dawnbreaker, as a relic of **Meridia** [4]. Destroying it ends Wintersun worship of Meridia if you follow her ("You have destroyed a sacred relic of your god… You are forsaken."). It also blocks worshipping her later ("Your actions have already betrayed this deity…") [4]. See mod-added/lorerim-specific-quests.md.
- **Requiem balance:** Requiem (Reqtificator) and Synthesis outputs override **Mighty Dawnbreaker**. `LoreRim - Armor Merges.esp` overrides the Dark Master set and both Khajiiti rings, and `LoreRim - ISC Patches.esp` overrides the quest rings [6].
- **Compatibility patches enabled in LoreRim** [5][6]:
  - **Northern Roads**: fixes floating objects around the sanctuary and the navmesh [5]
  - **Great Village of Old Hroldan**: moves an NPC and markers into the Old Hroldan Inn cell "so … the quest works properly" [5]
  - **The Great City of Winterhold** (`TGC Winterhold - Meridias Order patch.esp`)
  - **COTN Dawnstar** (`COTN Dawnstar - Meridias Order patch.esp`)
  - **Ivy's Stendarr's Beacon Overhaul** (`Ivy Stendarrs Beacon Overhaul - Meridia's Order Patch.esp`)
  - Lux and Lux Orbis lighting patches and an occlusion add-on
- The patch author recommends adding these patches only on a new game [5].
- `Meridia.esp` itself overrides two generic vanilla dialogue quests (`DialogueGenericCommanded`, `DialogueFavorGeneric`) but no vanilla story quest [1].
- **Vigilant overlap:** *Stendarr's Mercy* targets the Vigilants at Stendarr's Beacon [1]. LoreRim also ships the Vigilant mod (see related). How the two interact is not documented in the sources read.
