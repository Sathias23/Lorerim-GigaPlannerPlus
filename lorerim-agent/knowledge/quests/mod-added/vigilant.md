# VIGILANT

VIGILANT is a large quest mod by Vicn. You join the Vigilants of Stendarr and hunt the Daedra, witches and vampires troubling Skyrim, until Molag Bal takes a personal interest in you. [3][9] The mod page splits the story into four episodes and adds an epilogue: The Summoner (a tutorial), Bloody Matron (a small dungeon), Child of Oblivion (a horror hide-and-seek), Oblivion (a new world with no journal entries), and the Anatomancer epilogue. [3] Vigilant.esm adds 49 player-facing quests and about 185 new locations, plus 11 worldspaces including Coldharbour, the White Wasteland and the Elder Field. [2] LoreRim added Vigilant in V4. [1]

## Starting in LoreRim
- **Quest giver:** Altano, a Vigilant, recruits you at the Windpeak Inn in Dawnstar. [1][3] The first quest is **Vigilant of Stendarr**. [2]
- **Gate actually shipped (`Vigilant - Delayed Start.esp`, "Option 2"):** the Story Manager node `VigilantDelayedStart` starts the recruitment only when all three of these are true: [4][5][14]
  1. Your level is at least `zzzVigilantMinLevel`, which is **25** in LoreRim's copy.
  2. **The House of Horrors** (`DA10`) is completed.
  3. **Kindred Judgment** (`DLC1VQ08`) is completed. The mod page says either Dawnguard side counts. [5]
- Until then, Altano and Orlando are not in the Windpeak Inn. [5]
- The patch only works on a new game, or when it is installed together with Vigilant. [5]
- **Upstream gates that push this later in LoreRim:**
  - The House of Horrors is itself delayed to **level 35** by "Delayed Quest Starts - House of Horrors" (global `ANDR_HouseOfHorrorsLevelReq` = 35). After that, ask Kleppr or Frabbi in Markarth "Anything noteworthy happening?". [10]
  - Dawnguard is held back until "Laid to Rest" is done (Sensible Quest Prerequisites). [11]
  - In practice, recruitment comes at level 35 or later. That figure is derived from these gates. [4][10]
- **Contradiction:** LoreRim's New Lands page says you must finish "the main quest, Dawnguard and Molag's daedric quest (House of Horrors)". [1] The installed Delayed Start plugin has no main-quest condition. [4] An agent should say that the site lists the main quest but the shipped plugin only checks level, House of Horrors and Kindred Judgment.
- **No skipping in LoreRim:** in the base mod, Orlando the Knowledgeable and the debug-room paintings let you skip to Act 4. [3] LoreRim's Immersion Tweaks disables Orlando and empties the **Spinner's Needle** skip quests. LoreRim also removes the Debug Room key from the Owl Statue. [6][7]
- **Level advice:** the Delayed Start author calls 25 the "absolute earliest" and recommends about 40, because Act 4 is endgame. [5]

## Quests
Quest names and objectives come from the Vigilant.esm records. [2] The Act I order follows the Elder Scrolls Mods Wiki, which calls Old Regrets "Old Guilts". [12] The plugin's EditorID numbering (`zzzAoMMq00`–`zzzAoMMq10`, Vigilant of Stendarr through The Landing) gives the same order. [2]

### Act 1 — The Summoner
#### Vigilant of Stendarr
- **Giver:** Altano at the Windpeak Inn, Dawnstar. [1][3]
- **Steps:** Join the Vigilants of Stendarr. Follow Altano or meet him in the Temple of Stendarr. Talk to Altano, then to Thorondir, then to Altano again. [2]
- **Where:** the Temple of Stendarr in Stuhn Ravine. The fan wiki places the ravine just south of Nightcaller Temple. [12]

#### Bloodsucker
- Talk to Altano. Follow him to (or meet him in) Whiterun's Hall of the Dead. Find and defeat the vampire, then report back. [2]

#### He Who Cannot Be Touched
- Talk to Altano in the Bannered Mare, defeat the Daedra, and report back. [2]

#### Lazy Afternoon
- Talk to Altano in the Bannered Mare. Follow him to (or meet him in) Candlehearth Hall, defeat the Daedra, and report back. [2]

#### The Eye of Madness
- Talk to Altano in Candlehearth Hall and investigate rumors about the Eye of Madness. [2]
- Optionally, ask the cultist of Meridia for help. [2]
- Kill Balor and report to Altano. [2]

#### Dine and Dash
- Meet Altano in Candlehearth Hall, then at Stendarr's Beacon, then in the Bee and Barb. [2]
- Defeat the Daedra and talk to Keerava. [2]
- **Pay 1000 gold to Keerava.** You can optionally borrow the gold from Altano. [2]
- Report to Jacob. [2]

#### Thus Spoke Khajiit
- Meet Altano in the Ragged Flagon and find Jo'vanni. You can optionally ask the cultist of Meridia for help. [2]
- The quest moves into Jo'vanni's Dream Theater. You take Campaner'Ra's pelt from Mar'so and bring it back to Jo'vanni, then report to Altano. [2]
- **Alternate resolution:** a "Mar'so Suicide" bad-end record exists for this quest. [2][9]

#### Old Regrets
- Talk to Jacob and defeat the Summoner's Mercenary. [2]
- Watch over Jacob, then defeat Bal. [2]
- Give the Mace of Molag Bal to Altano. [2]

#### No Mercy
- Hunt down the witches, which the translator's notes call the Ivarstead witches. [2][9]
- Optionally, negotiate with Altano or defeat him. [2]
- Defeat the Ugly One. [2]
- **Choice:** a merciful alternate resolution leads to **Art of Mercy**. [9]

#### Art of Mercy
- This is the alternate resolution of the Ivarstead witches. [9]
- Talk to Carene, stop her, then talk to her again. Killing Carene is optional. [2]
- Leave, then report to Thorondir. [2]

#### The Endless Fall
- Talk to Altano and defeat the Daedra. [2]
- Look for survivors, chase Altano, and **defeat Molag Bal**. [2]

#### The Landing
- Talk to Altano and destroy the Mace of Molag Bal, then return to the Temple of Stendarr. [2]
- Journal: "Molag Bal left his curse on me. If even a spot of further corruption touches my soul, I will be dragged into his realm." [2]

### Act 2 — Bloody Matron
Act 2 starts automatically after Act 1. [3] It is about prisoners disappearing from the Windhelm dungeons. [3]

#### Empty Cells
- Talk to the courier, then the steward, and investigate the Windhelm dungeons. [2]
- Ask about the Maiden Statue and find the Windhelm Report in the Temple of Stendarr. [2]
- Eliminate the vampires under Windhelm and report back. [2]

#### Remnants
- Defeat a series of vampires. [2]
- You can give **Stendarr's Mercy** to one of them instead of fighting. [2]

#### The Blood Matron
- Defeat Lamae Bal, or free her from the curse of Molag Bal. [2]
- **Locations:** Windhelm Dungeon, Old Windhelm, Asylum, Blood Mist Castle, Matron's Chamber and Lamae's Dream. [2]

### Act 3 — Child of Oblivion
Act 3 starts automatically after Act 2. [3] You rescue the owners of a noble mansion. [3]

#### Child of Oblivion
- Talk to Gwyneth and travel to the Bruiant Mansion. [2]
- Solve the incident there. A guide record tracks breaking the five Blood Curses: Shivers, Corruption, Froth, Chains and Jealousy. [2]
- Defeat Julius and escape. [2]
- **Key choice:** "Die in flames as a martyr or accept Molag Bal's corruption in exchange for help." [2] The Landing has already warned that more corruption will drag you into Molag Bal's realm. [2]

#### Successor
- Talk to Gwyneth, investigate the Keeper's whereabouts, and report back. [2]

### Act 4 — Oblivion (Coldharbour)
Act 4 begins immediately after Act 3. You are trapped in Oblivion and must cross the wasteland to find a way out. The mod page calls this act "Non-Journal". [3] Its journal entries are cryptic "memory" quests, for example:
- **The Grand Inquisitor:** "Unfulfilled dreams drown in silence". [2]
- **The Mad King**, **Knight of Hounds**, **Johan the Fool**, **Adabal**, **Remains of the Miracle**, **Temptation of Marukh**, **The Nameless Bard**, **Beyond the Shores of Madness**, **Broken Horns** and **Memory Guide**. [2]
- **Feral Soul-Shriven:** a repeatable "Defeat <enemy>" quest. [2]
- **Maggot:** "Take back your name from <owner>". [2]

**Regions** include Malatar District, the Waterfront District, Mitta Village, the Arena, the Inquisition Court District, Sancremor Tower, Malada, Fort Welkynd, Jhunal's Library, the Old Forest, the Whale Graveyard and the Abyss of Molag Bal. [2]

**Endings:** the mod page refers to a "True Ending". After it, the Tree of Life gives access to the **Elder Field**. After Episode 4, the Red Stone lets you re-enter Coldharbour. [3] Both of those keys come from debug-room items, and LoreRim removes the debug-room key, so they may not be reachable this way in LoreRim (unverified). [3][7] Ending conditions such as karma are not covered here (unverified).

### Epilogue and side quests
#### Sacred Anatomancer
- After you escape Oblivion, the Librarian passes on a rumor about the Anatomancer. [3]
- Find the Anatomancer and prove yourself. [2]
- **Evil path:** help him, snatch a "two-legged sheep", and put the sack into the grinder. [2]
- **Good path:** kill him and divine the future from his guts. [2]

#### Legacy of Belharza
- Talk to the Minotaur and meet the Chief of Minotaurs at the Hidden Minotaur Village. [2][9]

### Radiant quests
- **Bounty board** (in front of the Temple of Stendarr; the mod page counts 8): [3]
  - **Bounty: Piper**, **Bounty: Chick Trader** (kill the trader, or buy an orphan and take them to the Temple), **Bounty: Summoner** and **Bounty: Witch**. [2]
  - **Bounty: Vampire**, **Bounty: Daedric Relic** (a relic of Meridia), **Bounty: Horn** (the lost Horn of Stendarr) and **Bounty: Book** (an erotic book). Relic-type bounties go into the Collection Chest. [2]
- **Temple dungeon radiants** (the mod page counts 5): **Radiance in the Spawn**, **Radiance in the Duplicate**, **Radiance in the Dead**, **Radiance in the Witch Hunter** and **Radiance in the Vampire**. Each is "Slay <target> in <location>". [2][3]
- **Dispatching Vigils:** set a flag on the map in the Temple to send Vigils to a city. [3] The helper records cover Windhelm, Dawnstar, Markarth, Falkreath, Winterhold and Morthal. [2] Vigils defend citizens, but they attack you if you commit crimes. [3]

## Locations
- **Temple of Stendarr / Stuhn Ravine** — the Vigilant hub, in its own Stuhn Ravine worldspace. It includes the Dungeon and Stendarr's Beacon Underground. [2] The fan wiki places the entrance south of Nightcaller Temple. [12]
- **Windhelm Dungeon, Old Windhelm, Blood Mist Castle, Lamae's Dream** — Act 2. [2]
- **Bruiant Estate / Bruiant Mansion** (with the North and South Wings, Basement and Hidden Room) and the Blood Curse realms — Act 3. [2]
- **Coldharbour** and the White Wasteland, Arena, Old Forest and Whale Graveyard worldspaces — Act 4. [2]
- **Elder Field** — the worldspace for the True Ending. [2][3]
- **Witch's Pond / Pond House** — Act 1 witches. [2] **Jo'vanni's Dream Theater** — Thus Spoke Khajiit. [2] **Hidden Minotaur Village** — Legacy of Belharza. [2]

## Rewards & notable items
- The mod adds many unique armor sets, weapons, jewelry and creatures. [3]
- **Anvil of Zenithar:** crafts the unique gear. Recipes unlock as you complete objectives, beat bosses and reach new areas. [3] Anvils are found in the Temple, the Beacon basement, the Waterfront District blacksmith, the Colosseum and Jhunal's Library. [3]
  - Immersion Tweaks replaces the Anvil of Zenithar with a vanilla anvil. [6]
  - LoreRim's patch loads later and restores the "Anvil of Zenithar" name and keyword, with 576 recipes tied to it. In LoreRim the anvil therefore still works (high confidence on the records). [7]
- **Devotion and Radiance:** these are spent to buy stats. LoreRim's Immersion Tweaks raises the cost from 3 to **30** (`zzzAoMgRate` = 30). [6]
- **Conjuration tomes:** "Piece of Bal", "Core of Order" and "Meridia's Beacon" tomes summon Vigilant bosses. They are kept and Requiem-adjusted in LoreRim's patch. The "No Boss Summons" plugin is **not** installed. [7][13]

## LoreRim notes
- **Boss difficulty:** LoreRim's MCM preset sets Difficulty Level to **50** and Increase Attack Power to **10**; the mod's defaults are 0 and 0. [8] The mod page says each point of the health multiplier adds +10% boss health and each attack point adds +20% damage. Bosses are much tougher than default, but exact multipliers are not verified. [3]
- **Immersion Tweaks (All in One):** [6]
  - Removes the Altar of Nocturnal at the start of Act 4 (it sold area-skip keys).
  - Blocks the door to Bandit Kanra.
  - Removes the Debug Room keys, Orlando's skips and the shared chests.
- **Requiem integration:** `Lorerim - Vigilant Patch.esp` rebalances Vigilant NPCs, races, gear, spells and recipes against Requiem. `LoreRim - Vigilant Boss.esp` adds stagger and combat perks to the Molag Bal boss. Neither plugin edits any quest. [7]
- **Combat feel:**
  - MCO-revised boss scripts remove Vicn's scripted killmove "special attacks". Only the scripts are installed; the Rebalanced Rewards plugin is not. [13]
  - Boss movesets come through Payload Interpreter configs. [13]
  - TrueHUD shows boss bars. [13]
- **NG+:** clear flags are saved to `ElderScroll_Global.json`. [3] LoreRim ships that file with every counter at 0, so new profiles start without NG+ unlocks. [8]
- **Visual and compatibility add-ons:** two NPC overhauls (NPC Overhaul and NPCs Refined), retextures, an extended soundtrack, Auri's Vigilant commentary (`018AuriVIGILANTpatch.esp`), and a Nature of the Wild Lands fix for a buried lute and tombstone in the True Ending world. [13]
- **Voice gaps:** some characters are unvoiced, including Anton the Brilliant, the Anatomancer and the Minotaurs. Turn on subtitles. [9]
- **Not in LoreRim:** Vicn's related mods DAc0da, GLENMORIL and Unslaad are not in the install, so Delayed Start options that need them are moot. [5]
