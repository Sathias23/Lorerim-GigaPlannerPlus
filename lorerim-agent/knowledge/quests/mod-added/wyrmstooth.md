# Wyrmstooth

Wyrmstooth is a fully voice-acted, DLC-sized quest mod by Jonx0r. A dragon has been disrupting trade routes in Skyrim, and you follow it to the island of Wyrmstooth, north of Solitude across the Sea of Ghosts [2][3]. The island has its own worldspace, and below it is a second worldspace called Dimfrost. The mod adds about 40 named locations [2]. You can also buy and upgrade a player fort (Fort Valus) and learn a new Shout, and the mod adds new weapons, shields, spells and an original soundtrack [3][4]. LoreRim ships it with a Requiem balance patch, an extra start prerequisite, a Settings Loader for the MCM, and a range of compatibility and visual patches [1][5][6][7][11].

## Starting in LoreRim

**Gates.** The opening quest **Wyrmstooth** starts in the background once every condition below is met:
1. **"Rise in the East" is complete.** This is the vanilla East Empire Company quest in Windhelm, given by Orthus Endario [9][10]. The LoreRim site lists it as a LoreRim-specific extra requirement [1]. The **Sensible Wyrmstooth Prerequisite** mod (`Rise of Wyrmstooth.esp`) enforces it: it overrides Wyrmstooth's starter quest `WTWyrmstoothStarter`, and its script requires `MS10` (Rise in the East) at stage ≥ 100 [5]. UESP lists stage 100 as the stage that finishes the quest [10]. The check applies to **every** MCM option, including "Immediately", so changing the MCM cannot skip it [5].
2. **A Main Quest milestone.** The MCM default is "After 'Way of the Voice'" (option 4, which checks `MQ105` stage ≥ 160) [5][7]. In the MCM you can pick any milestone from "Unbound" to "Dragonslayer", or "Immediately" [7].
3. **A minimum player level.** The mod's default is 10 [3][4]. In LoreRim this setting is contested (see LoreRim notes): `Requiem - Wyrmstooth.esp` sets the `WTStartLevel` global to **20** [6]. However, LoreRim's **Wyrmstooth - Settings Loader** writes `iWTGeneralRequirementsMinimalLevel=10` to that global when the MCM first initialises [7]. The effective value is therefore most likely **10** (medium confidence; this is inferred from the scripts and not observed in game). Check MCM → Wyrmstooth → "Minimum player level".

**How it starts.** The imperial courier Theodyn Bienne tracks you down, starting from the Bannered Mare in Whiterun [3][4]. Since 1.19.1 he can also appear in the nearest town you visit once you meet the requirements [4]. Alternatively, go straight to Lurius Liore at the Bannered Mare [4]. The MCM has a "Notify on start" toggle, which is off in LoreRim's settings [7].

The LoreRim site's text matches the mod defaults (level 10, Way of the Voice) and adds the Rise in the East requirement [1]. It does not mention the Requiem patch's level 20.

## Quests

### Wyrmstooth (`WTDragonHunt`, the intro; called "Dragon Hunt" before 1.19)
- **Giver / trigger:** A courier, Theodyn Bienne, sends you to Lurius Liore [2][4].
- **Where:** The Bannered Mare (Whiterun), then the road to Falkreath, then Ancient's Ascent, south-east of Falkreath [2].
- **Steps:** (10) Speak to Lurius Liore at The Bannered Mare. (20) Meet the mercenaries on the road to Falkreath and speak to Athir. (100) Find and defeat the dragon at Ancient's Ascent. (110) Speak to Athir; the dragon Vulthurkrah has fled to Wyrmstooth. (120) Return to Lurius Liore [2].
- **Notes:** If the mercenaries are not on the road, check the Deadman's Drink in Falkreath [4]. Finishing this quest starts Barrow of the Wyrm [4].

### Barrow of the Wyrm (`WTBarrowOfTheWyrm`, the main quest)
- **Giver:** Lurius Liore [2].
- **Steps:** (10) Meet Lurius at the Solitude docks; his ship, the Red Wave, takes you to Wyrmstooth. (50) Follow him to the Imperial mining settlement **Stonehollow**, which the dragon has destroyed. (60/90) Go to Wyrmstooth Barrow and search the locked entrance for a clue. (100) A bandit's journal names the sword **Goreduster** as the key; retrieve it and slot it into the pedestal. (110–120) Explore the barrow and follow the old mage **Alberthor**. (135) Alberthor transfers your mind into a Draugr; as the Draugr, find the switch that opens the gate. (180) Find a way down to the dragon's den in **Dimfrost**. (230) Vulthurkrah escapes; defend Stonehollow from him. (260) Receive your reward from Lurius [2].
- **Choices:** Since 1.19 you can leave the mercenaries behind and do the dungeon alone [4].
- **After:** Stonehollow is rebuilt (`WTRebuild` starts once this quest finishes) [4]. Athir, Daenlit and Shargam can be hired after Vulthurkrah is defeated [4]. A Debt Unpaid unlocks [4].

### Reclaiming the Past (`WTPlayerHome`, misc)
- (10) Ask Lurius Liore about Fort Valus. (20) Buy the deed and speak to him again about upgrades and hiring a crew [2]. The quest starts when you enter any building at Fort Valus [4]. A third-party guide gives the deed price as 30,000 gold [13], and a playthrough blog reports roughly 10,000 more for all upgrades [14]. Both prices are unverified against the plugin.

### Side quests (Wyrmstooth.esp) [2]
- **Retrieving Embersunder** (Athir): get the sword Embersunder from the forsworn camp at Hag's End.
- **Repaying a Debt** (Shargam): kill Ulfgar, who is staying at the Silverblood Inn in Markarth.
- **Robbed Blind** (Daenlit): steal an enchanted necklace from Erikur, a Solitude merchant. If Erikur is dead, the Nighthunter's Locket is placed in his coffin instead [4].
- **Stickler in the Mud** (Elmera): gather 30 spiddal sticks. They grow near an old Oblivion gate on the island [4].
- **Unwanted Guests** (Alberthor): kill the Thalmor spies on the island.
- **Wrap Me Up** (Alberthor): bring 8 wisp wrappings, which drop from wisp mothers or can be bought from alchemists.
- **A Debt Unpaid** (Signy): steal Erikur's debt ledger. Only available after Barrow of the Wyrm [4].
- **A Howl Load of Trouble:** start from a note by the tracker Dunyick. Find his rust-coloured wolf Faelor in the mountains near the mining settlement. Dunyick then dies of his injuries; kill the bandits who attacked him. At the end you can send Faelor back to Dunyick's camp or **take her as a follower**.
- **Someone with Backbone:** start from a book on Wyrmstooth's history. Investigate the Tomb of Vulom and follow a disembodied voice. Find Vulom's bones in the Nordic ruins across the island and return them; Vulom then reveals himself as a lich. You can refuse at the start (stage 15).
- **A Priceless Commodity:** start from a note by Bolmar at Gronndal Grove, where miners have dug into a spriggan sanctuary. **Choice:** kill the Spriggan Matriarch for Bolmar, or side with her and kill the miners who escaped. You can also betray her later (stage 45).
- **The Naked Nord** (added in 1.20): start from a note about a paralysed naked Nord, Gjalrunn. The witch Glendora stole his golden pants, and warlocks then took them to Krakevisa for a ritual. **Choices:** return the pants to Gjalrunn or give them to Glendora (for a reward), or lie and keep them. Get or refuse the paralysis antidote, which costs a daedra heart traded to Glendora. You can also kill Gjalrunn at his request. Several outcomes make him seek revenge later.
- **Blind Robber's Cache:** Fjeimir's letter points to Rolgar, who took the key and went to Dimfrost. Take the key from his corpse and open the cache.
- **Bounties (misc, single objective):** Animal Bounty (the bear south of Fort Valus), Bandit Bounty (the Marauder Clanlord at Cragwater Cavern), Vampire Bounty (the Master Vampire at Bloodfrost Burrow), Warlock Bounty (the warlock at Krakevisa). **Noticeboard Pointer** sends you to the notice board outside the tradehouse, which posts notes about side quests as the story progresses [2][4].
- **Unmarked:** a trail of journals that starts at the dockmaster's house and leads to **Frostwind Folly** [4].

### Missives board (Missives - Wyrmstooth Patch)
This patch adds a Missives board just outside the inn at Stonehollow [8]. It requires the Wyrmstooth main quest to be completed and the town fully rebuilt [8]. It adds radiant jobs such as Deliver a Letter/Weapon/Potion, Gather ingredients/ore/soul gems, Kill Some Things, Kill Some Marauders, Kill a Dragon, and Retrieve From a Hideout/Ruin/Wilderness [8]. Stonehollow has few NPCs, so few jobs may appear [8]. See [missives.md](missives.md).

## Locations
- **Wyrmstooth** (worldspace `WyrmstoothWorld`): the island north of Solitude [2][3]. Travel is by the Red Wave from the Solitude docks. Holmar ferries you back and forth [4], and map markers link Skyrim and Wyrmstooth [4].
- **Dimfrost** (worldspace): the underground realm below Wyrmstooth Barrow. It holds Vulthurkrah's den, including the Luminatory, and three respawning Dwemer dungeons [2][4].
- **Stonehollow:** the Imperial mining settlement and hub. It is rebuilt after the main quest [2][4].
- **Fort Valus:** the purchasable, upgradeable player fort [2][3].
- **Wyrmstooth Barrow:** the main dungeon, which the mod's page calls one of the largest in Tamriel [2][3].
- **Others (plugin LCTN records):** Fort Moonwatch, Cragwater Cavern (the marauder town; since 1.20.3 entering it turns the marauders hostile), Krakevisa, Gronndal Grove, Bloodfrost Burrow, Tomb of Vulom, Blind Robber's Bluff, Kazmalgur (an orc stronghold), Chillwater Mill, Frostvein Mine, Frostwind Folly, Haetar's Cave, Twinpeak Tower, The Hermit (an inn), Herman's Hideout, Stendarr's Outpost, Bloodstone Camp, Hag's Perch, Waylayer's Watch, Belonir's Borg, Abandoned Lighthouse, a Dark Brotherhood Sanctuary, and several farms and houses [2][4].
- **Unmarked Locations Pack - Wyrmstooth Addon** adds more than 15 unmarked spots with loot, notes and encounters [12].

Full area detail is in [../areas/wyrmstooth-island.md](../areas/wyrmstooth-island.md).

## Rewards & notable items
- **Shout:** "Fiik Lo Sah" (Phantom Form), learned at 3 new word walls [4]. One source says Fort Valus has the first word and Haetar's Cave the last (unverified; low confidence, from a search snippet of a fan wiki/walkthrough not listed in Sources).
- **Suleyk's mask:** the dragon priest mask is a playable item with a unique effect and can be tempered [4].
- **Named gear (per the changelog):** Goreduster, Embersunder, Chaoswielder, the Bloodletter dagger, the Gauntlets of Dentistry, and the Nighthunter's Locket [2][4].
- **Followers:** the mercenaries Athir, Daenlit and Shargam, plus Elmera, who joins at the barrow crypt during the main quest; Faelor the wolf is also available [4][13].
- **Fort Valus:** the player home, with hireable staff (cook, blacksmith, gardener, guards) [4].
- Lurius pays you at the end of Barrow of the Wyrm. The amount was not found [2].

## LoreRim notes
- **Extra prerequisite:** Sensible Wyrmstooth Prerequisite requires Rise in the East for every start option [1][5].
- **Requiem balance:** Requiem - Wyrmstooth (Updated) rebalances the dragon, the dragon priest, werewolves, ingredients, ores and ingots, armour and weapons. It replaces or removes many Wyrmstooth spells with Requiem equivalents, renames NPCs (for example, "Marauder Berserker" becomes "Marauder"), makes the new drinks Survival-aware, and raises the start level from 10 to 20 [6].
- **The level contradiction:** the Requiem patch's GLOB `WTStartLevel` is 20.0, while `Wyrmstooth.esp` uses 10.0 [6][2]. The Settings Loader's `settings.ini` contains `iWTGeneralRequirementsMinimalLevel=10`, and its script runs `LoadSettings()` → `WTStartLevel.SetValue(...)` in `OnConfigInit` [7]. No `MCM/Settings/Wyrmstooth.ini` override was found in the install. The likely effective gate is 10, but this has not been tested in game.
- **Boss health scaling is on:** the Settings Loader sets `bWTGeneralMiscellaneousScaleBossHealth=1` with a multiplier of 12 [7]. The author says this was not meant to be on by default; the plugin global is 0 [4]. While it is on, the health of Vulthurkrah, Vulom, Valstadt, Malentis, Suleyk and The Valedrynn scales up if your current weapon could kill them in 4 hits or fewer [4].
- **Difficulty:** Wyrmstooth Barrow and Dimfrost have a minimum encounter level of 24 [4]. A third-party guide recommends level 25 or higher [13].
- **Asset and compatibility patches (no quest content):**
  - The Cause / Rare Curios patches: spiddal sticks use The Cause's assets, and Elmera's quest data is updated to match [11].
  - Crates and EECO building navmesh patches [11].
  - Daedric Shrines, Metallurgy (Wyrmstone/Brimstone ore), Vulom skeleton HD, Icy Mesh tweak, Dragons SE (Vulthurkrah model), Dovah Names, Enigma dragon voices, and texture, ENB, banner and alcohol-mesh replacers [11].
- **Other Wyrmstooth-related mods enabled elsewhere in the load order** (not in this separator): Stonehollow Overhaul for Wyrmstooth, Ferries - Wyrmstooth Addon, GTS - Wyrmstooth Adoption, Lux/Lux Orbis patches, and a paper map [11].
- **Known issue:** if Lurius does not travel to the Red Wave at the start of Barrow of the Wyrm, sleep, and he is teleported to the ship's interior [3][4].
