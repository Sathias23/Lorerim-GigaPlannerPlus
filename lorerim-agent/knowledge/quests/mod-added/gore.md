# Gore - A Companion Mod

Gore is "a fully voiced, lore-friendly, standalone follower with 4,300+ lines" [1]. The author describes him as "a child-soldier grown adult, cast off by the family he never truly had", whose story "is told across two main quests, a few side quests, and a conclusion that I am currently working on" [3]. He fights with a greatsword and heavy armour, starts with Iron-tier gear, and adds an optional survival-lite camp, a healing flask and an optional "Alternate Death System" [3]. The author gives a content warning: his personal quests touch on heavy personal themes, and they are optional [3].

## Starting in LoreRim
- **Where:** "Go to Peak's Shade Tower and find Gore" [1]. The tower is outside Falkreath [3].
- **Start:** *Raven's Flight* begins with a rumour: "Imperial Patrols near Falkreath looking for something... or someone." Head to Peak's Shade Tower, speak with the Nord whose leg is trapped, free him and help him [2].
- **LoreRim gates:** I found no LoreRim delayed start or level gate [1]. The author recommends recruiting him at a low level [3].
- **Load-order note:** the Nexus page says "Load Gore.esm as high as you possibly can", but LoreRim ships it as **`GORE.esp`** [2][3].

## Quests
Recommended order from the author's walkthrough [3], with plugin objectives [2]:

### Raven's Flight
- Head to Peak's Shade Tower outside Falkreath → speak with the Nord → help free his leg → see what help he needs. Ending: "Gore is back up on his feet. Now it's time to get him to Falkreath" [2].
- **Branch:** you can kill him instead. The journal reads "I have killed the Nord in the trap. Glory to the Aldmeri Dominion." (objective "Gore is dead!") [2].

### Wanted
- "Defeat the Thalmor Envoy" (or talk to them), speak with Gore, then "Take Gore to an inn after losing the pursuers" so he'll open up about his name [2]. Afterwards, tell him to follow, step outside and tell him you're thirsty [3].

### The Drunken Hunted
- Head to the Drunken Huntsman in Whiterun, tell Gore he can relax, and exhaust his dialogue ("I feel like I'm getting closer with Gore") [2][3].

### Blood Ties
- "Get to know Gore better" (relax at another inn and exhaust his dialogue), then head to Dawnstar with Gore. You find **Pate** and then Pate's family (wife **June**, children **Reese** and **Dorothy**). "Intervene..." before Gore "does anything crazy" [2][3].
- **Consequences** depend on your choices [2][3]:
  - **To Free a Raven** — Gore turns himself in. Wait, visit him, speak to the guard about paying for his release, then take him to the Windpeak Inn.
  - **Come, the Crows** — speak to Gore and head to an inn far from Dawnstar.
  - **The Apple from the Tree** — Gore wants to leave the party. If you won't allow it, the objective becomes "Kill Gore" ("I have killed Gore for trying to betray me").
- The author says this epilogue stage is where good decisions can open the **romance** [3].

### Where We Stand (interlude)
- Ask him, while he's relaxed, if there's anywhere he wants to see in Skyrim. He takes you to a spot near Ivarstead [2][3].

### Nest Left Empty (interlude)
- He brings this one up himself. Head to the waterfall marked on your map, survive the ambush, and speak to Gore [2][3]. Afterwards, tell him to relax and "talk about the ravens some more". The author calls this dialogue tree "CRUCIAL in deciding which ending you will get" [3].

### Later steps (not separate journal quests)
- If romancing: ask him on a date ("Click on the boat!"), ask about marriage at his camp, then speak to him one last time relaxed at an inn [3]. The third and final quest "has not been made yet" [3].

### Side quests
- **Count Fleet** — ask him about life in the Ravens while he's relaxed, then get him a horse at the Whiterun Stables. The horse is *Count Fleet* (spell *Summon Count Fleet*). Getting the horse unlocks most camp crafting stations [2][3].
- **A New Coat** — speak to Gore about his equipment, then ask Taarie at Radiant Raiment in Solitude for new armour (the plugin spells it "Radient Raiment"). Wait one day and give him **Gore's Upgraded Armor** [2].
- **Fear and Loathing** — "Gore and I have been hired by a Vigilant of Stendarr to investigate a suspicious house in Markarth." Investigate, then speak with Gore outside [2].

## Locations
- **Peak's Shade Tower** — vanilla tower outside Falkreath where Gore is found. GORE.esp edits its exterior cell [2][3].
- **Gorse Dimension** (`HD1GorsePrisonLoc`) — a new location record. Its role isn't documented in the sources I found; it may be Gore's isolated camp cell (unverified) [2].
- The plugin also contains an internal worldspace with a joke name (`0GoreLydiaSexSpot`). No quest objective references it [2].
- Gore's **camp** (Planning > "Let's set up camp", or the *Move To Camp* spell) is "a nice, quiet, isolated cell" with alchemy, tempering, tanning, cooking, smithing and sharpening stations [2][3].

## Rewards & notable items
- **Healing Flask** (5 charges, each heals 50% of your health; refill at the camp mortar and pestle) [2][3].
- Camp meals give slight 24-hour attribute buffs [3].
- *Gore's Upgraded Armor* (A New Coat) and his horse *Count Fleet* [2].
- The optional **Alternate Death System**: Gore drags you back to camp instead of you dying. Opt in at camp by asking what he would do if something happened to you. It's incompatible with similar mods. It must be re-enabled after he leaves, goes to jail, or goes missing during *A Night to Remember* or Vigilant [3].

## LoreRim notes
- **Shipped plugins:** `GORE.esp`, `Gore - SaSEC.esp` (Saints & Seducers Extended Cut support, adds background quest `HD1Madness`) and `Press E to heal Gore.esp` (Press E To Heal Followers integration) [2][4].
- **Patches:** *Gore - Paarthurnax Quest Expansion Patch* (three dialogue lines), *GORE - Fishing (CC) Patch* (a camp cell edit), *Alchemy Requires Bottles and Gore Patch* (`GTS - Gore Camp.esp`, adds furniture to the camp), *COTN Dawnstar - Gore patch* (two placed references in Dawnstar, relevant to Blood Ties) and *Redwater Den - Gore Patch* [4].
- LoreRim's generated patches (Dialogue Patch, which touches the "Gore, can I hug you?" topic; NPC Consistency; NPCs and Races; Recipes; Food/Alchemy tweaks; Synthesis outputs; *Requiem for the Indifferent*) all master GORE.esp. None of them overrides Gore's QUST records, so quest flow is unchanged [4].
- **No dedicated Vigilant addon plugin** is present in LoreRim, even though the Nexus page mentions Vigilant coverage and Vigilant credits [3][4]. The author says the Vigilant addon is not safe to add after starting Vigilant [3].
- Gore has about 320 lines with **Remiel (Remi)** and a few idles with **Auri**. Don't add him to NFF-style frameworks via their dialogue option [3].
- **Don't uninstall him mid-playthrough**: the author uses global variables [3].
