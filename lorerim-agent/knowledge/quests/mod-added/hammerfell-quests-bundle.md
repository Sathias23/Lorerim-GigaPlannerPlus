# Betalille's Hammerfell Quests Bundle

Betalille compiled five quest mods set in the Alik'r Desert of MannyGT's *The Gray Cowl of Nocturnal* [2]:
- Hammerfell Blades
- Desertic Dark Brotherhood
- Cyrodilic Collections in Hammerfell
- More to do in Hammerfell
- Desert Lamias in Hammerfell

The author counts 31 playable quests; the plugin has 32 named quests with journal or objective text [1][2]. The bundle also adds a crime system with guards in Ben Erai and the new settlements of Vulstad and Salas Kazas, plus new vendors and an inn in Salas Kazas. All characters except the innkeeper are voiced [2].

## Starting in LoreRim
- **Hard prerequisite:** the Alik'r Desert belongs to The Gray Cowl of Nocturnal. In LoreRim that mod's main quest starts only after you finish the Thieves Guild ("Under New Management") and then steal an item [6]. Plan for the bundle as late-game content.
- LoreRim ships the bundle on top of the **10th-anniversary** Gray Cowl, plus the bundle's own 10th Anniversary Patch and Hotfix [1][3][7]. The Nexus page still names the older SE Gray Cowl as the requirement [2].
- The Nexus page gives the quest givers below. The LoreRim site has no bundle-specific gate [2][6].
  - **Hammerfell Blades:** go to the settlement of **Vulstad**. Talk to old **Duarelm**, who is on the roof of one of the houses. After his quest, talk to **Azita** at her grandfather's hideout or to the Loremaster who joins the temple [2]. Nexus calls it "Azir's Hideout"; the plugin journal says "Azzin's Hideout" [1][2].
  - **Desertic Dark Brotherhood:** finish the vanilla Dark Brotherhood quest **"Hail Sithis!"** An "Unknown Initiate" then appears on a small island north of the Dawnstar Sanctuary. A map marker in the desert shows the Alik'r Desert Sanctuary [2].
    - **Anti-DB route:** destroy the Dark Brotherhood in Skyrim instead. Then go to the **Penitus Oculatus Outpost** in Ben Erai [2].
  - **Cyrodilic Collections:** head north-west of Ben Erai to a small house between two towers flying the Collections flag. Talk to the Argonian lady, **Jukka** [1][2].
  - **Desert Lamias:** look north of the Ancient Alik'r Palace, near two ruined small houses. Talk to the Lamia, **Oziriana** [1][2].
  - **More to do in Hammerfell:** talk to the new people. One quest is in Ben Erai and three are in Salas Kazas. One starts with people in front of an unmarked temple north-west of the Lantern of the North [2].
    - The Solstheim quest is at the Retching Netch in Solstheim. It is available only after the Gray Cowl main quest's Solitude Docks ending, i.e. "Time for the Farewells" [1][2].
    - The finale comes from the Ash'abah **Honai**, after the Ash'abah, ancient-library and necromancer quests [2].

## Quests
### Hammerfell Blades
#### A Lost Temple in Hammerfell
- **Giver:** Duarelm (Vulstad) [1][2].
- **Steps:** Search the first, second and third locations (the third has a trap door). Go back to Duarelm, then head to the Wind Scour Temple with him. Talk to Duarelm. Wait for Duarelm to reclaim the Wind Scour Temple, then return to him [1].
- **Outcome:** the Blades start rebuilding in Hammerfell at the **Wind Scour Temple** [1].
#### Dragonguard Archives, plus "Dragonguard Archives: Book 1–6"
- **Dragonguard Archives:** Duarelm sends you for a book at a variable location (`<Alias=Location>`) [1].
- **Book quests:** misc trackers. Each one is "get the book" and put it in the chest in the Wind Scour Temple:
  - Book 1: Vulstad
  - Book 2: Ben Erai Barracks
  - Book 3: Ben Erai
  - Book 4: Fortress of Ben Erai
  - Book 5: Arenar's Camp
  - Book 6: Khenzedum [1]
#### Protecting What Was Lost
- **Giver:** Azita, who wants her grandfather Azzin's sealed sword [1].
- **Steps:** Interact with the pillar in Azzin's Hideout. Kill the Fire, Frost and Shock Dragons and pick up each one's stone. Use the three stones on the pillars, then pick up the **Boneshaver Replica** [1].
- **Reward:** Boneshaver Replica, a replica of the Ra Gada sword said to be a gift from Onsi [1].
#### In need of Scouts
- **Steps:** recruit two followers to join the Blades in Hammerfell, then talk to **Za'kir** at the Wind Scour Temple [1].
#### Sands of the Past
- **Giver:** Loremaster Za'kir [1].
- **Steps:**
  1. Defeat the ghosts in two caves, then report back.
  2. Search a bigger cave (the Akaviri Cave), where you find **Azzin's Journal**.
  3. Find the fugitive blademaster descendant **Jonathan Seven-Swords**, who is hiding from the Thalmor.
  4. Tell Za'kir that Jonathan is dead.
  5. Burn Azzin's Journal in a fire, then talk to Za'kir again [1].
- **Note:** the Hotfix plugin edits only Jonathan Seven-Swords' placed reference, which sits in the Skyrim (Tamriel) worldspace. So his hiding place is in Skyrim, not the desert (medium confidence on the gameplay reading) [3].

### Desertic Dark Brotherhood (DDB)
- **The Dark Brotherhood in Hammerfell:** Head to the Alik'r Desert Sanctuary; Talk to **Tenerio** [1].
- **Contracts** (each ends with "go back to Tenerio") [1]:
  - **Merchant Problem:** kill the merchant at the Ben Erai Outpost.
  - **Argonian Hero:** kill the Argonian at the Lantern of the North.
  - **Wandering Monk:** kill the monk at the Lucky Lantern.
  - **Alik'r No More:** defeat the Alik'r warrior in Ben Erai.
  - **No Guard Is Safe:** kill **Laurentina** inside the Fortress of Ben Erai.
- **Disappearing in the Dark:** starts from a letter, "Hammerfell needs you" [1].
  - Investigate the missing initiate **Lucya**: first the village (you find a dead vampire), then the warehouse, where Lucya is dead with her soul bound to a soul gem [1].
- **The Crimson Eviscerator:**
  1. Find Lucya's soul in the **Soul Cairn**. This needs Dawnguard Soul Cairn access (unverified beyond the journal text) [1].
  2. Enter the **Soul Cavern** in the Alik'r Desert and use Lucya's soul gem to open the path.
  3. Defeat the protector and pick up the **Crimson Eviscerator** [1].
- **Crimson Scar Believers:** clear the Vampire Hideout, which stops the Crimson Scar's rebirth in the desert [1].
- **Ending the Dark Brotherhood in Hammerfell again** (the alternative route): defeat the Dark Brotherhood leader in Hammerfell, then return to the **Penitus Oculatus Outpost** [1][2].

### Cyrodilic Collections in Hammerfell (giver: Jukka)
- **Black Marsh Maps:** find the first map in Khenzedum and the second in the Alik'r Desert [1].
- **Cyrodilic Collections are Recruiting:** place three signs in Ben Erai [1].
- **Ancient Pottery:** recover an ancient Argonian pottery in **Sadraaka** [1].
- **A Special Smell:** find a censer in **Al Shedim**. This is the Collections' last quest [1].

### More to do in Hammerfell (Salas Kazas and the Ash'abah)
- **Is There a Necromancer in Town?:** **Thaik** of Salas Kazas sends you to clear a necromancer camp in the desert [1].
- **Weltan the Poet:** **Sharza** of Salas Kazas wants two poem books by Weltan. One is in a library and one is in the Mausoleum [1].
- **From Solstheim to Hammerfell:** escort the Dunmer merchant **Lloros Reynel** from Solstheim to Hammerfell, where he opens a shop [1][2].
- **The Library Lost to the Ages** (mages-guild type):
  - Find the ancient library; the journal hints at the Forgotten City.
  - Bring **Dorian** of Ben Erai to it. A teleport stone there leads to a bigger library [1].
- **The Ash'abah:** help **Honai's** Ash'abah clear undead from ancient ruins [1].
- **Helping the Enemy** (finale):
  - **Gate:** available after the Ash'abah, library and necromancer quests [2].
  - Honai warns of the necromancer **Andyn Pamarc**. Defeat him, check on Dorian Masterhart, then talk to Honai [1].

### Desert Lamias in Hammerfell
- **Spectral Lamias in the Desert:** defeat **Feiria the Matriarch**, Oziriana's rogue sister. Then put out the spectral fire and talk to Oziriana again [1].
- **Afterwards:** Oziriana can be recruited as a follower, and you can summon Spectral Lamias [1][2]. The plugin has a "Spell Tome: Conjure Spectral Lamia" [4].

## Locations
- **Salas Kazas** — new village with an inn and vendors; quest hub [1][2].
- **Vulstad** — new settlement; Duarelm's start [2].
- **Wind Scour Temple** — Blades temple, under a trap door in the desert [1].
- **Azzin's Hideout** — sealed sword and the dragon trials [1].
- **Alik'r Desert Sanctuary** — Dark Brotherhood base (Tenerio) [1][2].
- **Penitus Oculatus Outpost** — Ben Erai, the anti-DB route [2][3].
- **Soul Cavern** — the Crimson Eviscerator [1][3].
- **Ben Erai Jail** — part of the crime system [1].
- **Dorian's House** — Ben Erai [3].
- **Cyrodilic Collections** — museum NW of Ben Erai [2].
- For the wider desert, see [../areas/hammerfell-and-coldharbour-gray-cowl.md](../areas/hammerfell-and-coldharbour-gray-cowl.md).

## LoreRim notes
- **Requiem balance:** LoreRim's own `The Gray Cowl - Betalilles LoreRim Patch.esp` (in "LoreRim - xEdit64 Output") covers 213 NPC records, both from the bundle and from Gray Fox Cowl.esm. It also overrides the bundle's weapons, armor, summon effects, spell tome, merchant containers and recipes against Requiem [4].
- **Lux** lighting patch: `Lux - Betalille's Hammerfell Quests Bundle patch.esp` [7].
- **10th Anniversary Patch** (`BetalilleBundle10YPatch.esp`) re-places the bundle's references and navmeshes in the 10th-anniversary desert. This covers Dorian's House, the Penitus Oculatus Outpost and the Soul Cavern [3].
- **Hotfix** (`BetalilleBundleHotfix040625.esp`) touches only Jonathan Seven-Swords' placement [3].
- **The DB intro needs vanilla "Hail Sithis!"** [2]. How it interacts with LoreRim's other Dark Brotherhood mods is unverified; see [../vanilla-changes/dark-brotherhood.md](../vanilla-changes/dark-brotherhood.md).
- **Not included:** the author's "The Remnants of Hammerfell" and "Pirates in the Desert" are left out of the bundle because they need Creation Club content [2]. Neither is in LoreRim's Gray Cowl separator [5].
