# The Frozen Heart

"Step through a magical mirror into a cursed frozen realm. Explore labyrinths, solve puzzles, ponder orbs, battle shadows, and help an ancient Snow Elf warrior cure her curse." (LoreRim site) [2] The mod adds a new worldspace, five dungeon areas, four quests, a voiced follower (Othriel, VA Madeline Harsh), a fully exterior player home, a mirror-based travel method and the Rimeweld weapon/armor set; the author estimates 3–4 hours of gameplay [1][3]. LoreRim ships it with a LoreRim-made Requiem patch [5].

## Starting in LoreRim
- **Prerequisites:** at least 1 word of **Slow Time** and 1 word of **Fire Breath**. The mod page offers a FOMOD option that removes the "must know" requirement, "but these shouts are still needed to progress through the quest" [3]. The install's own string confirms: "Snow Elf Mirrors will only activate with the use of Slow Time." [6] Which FOMOD option LoreRim selected could not be determined.
- **Where to get the shouts (vanilla baseline, unverified for LoreRim word-wall changes):** Slow Time words are on word walls at Hag's End, Korvanjund and Labyrinthian; Fire Breath is taught by Paarthurnax in the main quest "The Throat of the World", with walls at Sunderstone Gorge and Dustman's Cairn [7].
- **Start:** purchase **Snow Elf Mirror (Labyrinthian Passages)** from **Belethor's General Goods** (in Whiterun [8]); picking it up starts "The Frozen Heart" (stage 100). Read its back, shout Slow Time, then equip it from the inventory [1][2][3][6].
- The LoreRim site repeats exactly this start procedure [2]. No LoreRim delayed start or level gate found.

## Quests
### The Frozen Heart
- **Giver / trigger:** buying the mirror at Belethor's General Goods [1].
- **Steps** (plugin objectives, with the author's walkthrough in brackets) [1][3]:
  1. Investigate the mirror → teleports you to the Labyrinthine Passages. [Shout Fire Breath at the door; move forward/back/forward until the effects occur; shrine to Auri-El; optional "Note to a Stranger".]
  2. Find a way through the Labyrinthine Passages.
  3. Investigate the Crag Spire Wastes. [Go west to the maze (Whispering Walls); answer Siwe's ghost: 1 "The Snow Elves", 2 "Auri-El", 3 "To find peace and inner balance", 4 tell the truth. Optional: if you fail, wait 24 h or climb the walls.]
  4. Find a cure to Othriel's curse. [Knock at Othriel's Cabin (or lockpick), agree to help.]
  5. Retrieve equipment from Frostskarn Vault. [Align pillars to the wall characters, then to the mirror reflection; kill 2 Wisp Mothers; take the armor and the Sword of Sealing.]
  6. Show the equipment to Othriel.
  7. Defeat the Cursed Shadow (Whispering Walls Catacombs). [Open the portcullis with the Sword of Sealing; pillar code 1189; shatter the 4 mirrors to stop it respawning.]
  8. Escape the Whispering Walls Catacombs. [Orb-reflection pillar puzzle; take Snow Elf Mirror (Wisp Light Crevasse), use it under Slow Time.]
  9. Defeat the Cursed Shadow again (it was an illusion). [Shatter 2 mirrors; Slow Time on it reveals Othriel's Frozen Heart.]
  10. Dispel the curse. [Fire Breath on Othriel's Frozen Heart.]
  11. Speak to Othriel (give her the heart).
- **Choices & outcomes:** refuse to help (stage 10000); kill Othriel instead (stages 20000/20100); refuse to give back her heart → fight to the death [1][3].
- **Rewards:** with enough disposition (don't skip scenes; choose kind options) Othriel becomes a potential follower and you gain access to all items in Othriel's Cabin [3]. "There is no reward for going fast." [3]

### Seeking Out Shards
- **Trigger:** pick up any Snow Elf Mirror Shard in the Crag Spire Wastes [1][3].
- **Steps** [1]: 1. Find all the shards of the Snow Elf Mirror (0/8). 2. Find a frame for the Snow Elf Mirror. 3. Craft a frame from the shattered Snow Elf Mirror. 4. Reforge the Snow Elf Mirror (forge or anvil). 5. Investigate it → it leads to the Gallery of Mirrors.
- **Shard locations** [3]: chest guarded by 2 Frost Trolls on the north path between the Labyrinthine Passages and the maze; chest on a rock above the maze's south wall; on Torbarik's body (first room, Wisp Light Crevasse); on a rock above the maze→cabin path; chest above the stairs in Othriel's Cabin; chest across the waterfall chasm in Wisp Light Crevasse (needs Whirlwind Sprint); behind a mirror shattered in the first Cursed Shadow fight; ledge in an ice room between the waterfall chasm and the orb room. The Shattered Snow Elf Mirror (for the frame) is in the passage between the first Cursed Shadow room and the waterfall chasm.
- **Reward:** Snow Elf Mirror (Gallery of Mirrors) — its portals travel to major cities you've discovered [3].

### A Book for Othriel
- **Trigger:** 1 day after finishing The Frozen Heart, Othriel asks for a book [3].
- **Steps** [1]: 1. Obtain a copy of "Feyfolken III". 2. Give it to Othriel. (Refusing or saying you can't find it fails the quest.) [3]

### Another Book for Othriel
- **Trigger:** 1 day after completing or failing A Book for Othriel [3].
- **Steps** [1]: 1. Obtain "The Armorer's Challenge". 2. Give it to Othriel. 3. Wait for her to read it. 4. Speak to Othriel. 5. Obtain Stalhrim Ores (0/5). 6. Give 5 Stalhrim Ores to Othriel. 7. Wait 1 day while she crafts Rimeweld Armor. 8. Speak to Othriel.
- **Rewards:** a set of Rimeweld armor and weapons plus **Rimeweld Armoury Plans** (lets you craft Rimeweld gear with Ebony Smithing, per the mod page) [3].

## Locations
- **Crag Spire Wastes** — the frozen worldspace behind the mirrors [1]. See [areas/frozen-heart-areas.md](../areas/frozen-heart-areas.md).
- **Labyrinthine Passages**, **Whispering Walls** (the ghost maze), **Othriel's Cabin** (fully exterior player home), **Frostskarn Vault**, **Whispering Walls Catacombs**, **Wisp Light Crevasse**, **Gallery of Mirrors** (portal hub) [1][3].

## Rewards & notable items
- Sword of Sealing; Rimeweld Sword, Shield, Circlet, Pauldrons, Gauntlets, Greaves, Cuirass; Rimeweld Armoury Plans; Othriel as follower; Othriel's Cabin; Gallery of Mirrors fast travel [1][3][5].

## LoreRim notes
- **LoreRim patch** (`The Frozen Heart - Quest Mod - LoreRim Patch.esp`, enabled) [5]:
  - **Othriel** is changed from player-level scaling (1.0× PC level) to a **fixed level 50**, with many Requiem perks and three extra spells added [5].
  - **Rimeweld armor becomes Light Armor** (vanilla mod: Heavy) with Requiem-scale ratings — e.g. Cuirass 275, Shield 165, Circlet 110, Gauntlets 82, Greaves 83 — and the Pauldrons become a clothing-type piece with 0 armor [5].
  - **Rimeweld Sword / Sword of Sealing** damage raised from 14 to 78 (Requiem scale), value 1000 → 1800 [5].
  - Rimeweld tempering recipes get new perk/quest conditions (not fully decoded) [5].
  - **Seeking Out Shards** gains a quest condition requiring The Frozen Heart's main quest to be at stage ≥ 100 (i.e. started) [5].
- LoreRim also installs "Snowfall - A Othriel Visual Replacer" (v1.0.0.0) and the 3BA BodySlide files [4].
- The author asks players not to use paraglider/parkour to skip puzzles; outdated unofficial translations cause issues [3].
