# The Rift and Riften (LoreRim town expansions)

LoreRim reshapes Riften's waterfront and market and rebuilds the Rift's two villages: Riften Docks Overhaul adds a fish market, larger docks and connecting pathways, Alternative Riften adds new market structures, and an Environs mod turns the docks warehouse into an East Empire shop after the Riften misc quest "Supply and Demand" [1][2][3][4][13]. The Great Town of Ivarstead and The Great Town of Shor's Stone rebuild those villages with new halls and shops, and Orc Exiles turns the Rift Watchtower into an Orc-held Imperial ruin [5][6][7][8][9]. None of these mods adds a journal quest [1][5][7].

## Starting in LoreRim
- Riften, Ivarstead and Shor's Stone changes are present from a new game; no LoreRim-specific gate [1][5][7].
- **Riften warehouse shop:** after you disrupt Riften's skooma supply in "Supply and Demand", the empty warehouse by the docks becomes an East Empire Company outpost run as a general merchant; "a delay is implemented" after the quest so the change is gradual — the mod page says you "must wait at least 15 days" in game [4]. "Supply and Demand" (`FreeformRiften01`) is a vanilla misc quest (report the skooma source, clear Cragslane Cavern), and the plugin's trigger script points at exactly that quest [13].
- Ivarstead's author recommends a new game, "Otherwise the bed in the inn won't show" [6].

## Quests
The town mods add no quests [1][5][7]. Quests LoreRim's site places in the Rift (own files):
- **Sirenroot** — an Ayleid ruin "underneath Lake Honrich near Riften"; start by speaking to Frissa Black-Briar at Elgrim's Elixirs in Riften [10]. See `mod-added/sirenroot.md`.
- **Sleepwalking into a Nightmare** — speak with Ralforn in green-tip cabin, "a new location north-east of Ivarstead" [10]. See `mod-added/sleepwalking-into-a-nightmare.md`.
- **Fists of Fury** — brawling quests including Riften, started after winning vanilla brawls [10]. See `mod-added/fists-of-fury.md`.

## Locations

### Riften — Riften Docks Overhaul
Features: "A fish market with custom food items and NPCs with their own schedule"; "A better decorated Honeyside's deck with a pathway to a player-owned small island"; "Larger docks with dock workers"; pathways connecting Honeyside to the main docks and western Riften "(So you don't have to swim from the shore to the dock)"; new ship variants; and full integration of the Fishing Creation [2]. It requires the Fishing DLC [2]. New location record: **Soderberg's House** (in Riften) [1]. LoreRim ships its patches for Lux, Animated Ships, CFTO, High Poly Head, Shadowfoot Sanctum, SMIM and Song of the Green [1]; the Shadowfoot patch "Moves the entrance so it doesn't conflict with the fish market" [2].

### Riften — Alternative Riften - Revived and Optimized
Revives Kelretu's unfinished Alternative Riften, optimizes its meshes and adds a new market mesh; navmesh redone and landscape edits removed [3]. Specific new buildings are not itemized in the sources read (gap).

### Riften — Environs - Riften Warehouse
The warehouse "will now become an outpost for the East Empire Company" after "Supply and Demand", overhauled "as a general merchant" with "two new NPCs" — a Dunmer merchant and a Nord assistant [4].

### Ivarstead — The Great Town of Ivarstead
Replaces the farmhouses with Riften-style and custom buildings; adds "a new Hall of Kyne" where priests "follow the old ways of Kyne instead of Kynareth", "a new shop", and new bridges [6]. New location records: **Hall of Kyne**, **Henrik's House**, **Pilgrim's Needs** [5]. That Pilgrim's Needs is the new shop is inferred from its name (unverified).

### Shor's Stone — The Great Town of Shor's Stone
Described as "a miserable little mining town north of Riften"; adds "the new Fallowstone Hall", headquarters of the Companions of the Rift in Elder Scrolls Online, whose "great vault still houses a lot of grand treasure", plus "a new inn, new farmland and a small hall commemorating the dead god Shor" [8]. LoreRim uses Rob's Bug Fixes' cleaned plugin for TGC Shor's Stone 1.04 [7]. New location records: **Fallowstone Vault** (two records, one with editor ID `ShorsStoneFallowstoneHall…`), **Emma's and Olafr's House**, and **Shor's Hall Location** (literal in-game name) [7]. LoreRim also enables "The Great Town of Shor's Stone - Religion Mod Addons - Wintersun - Pilgrim" [11].

### Rift Watchtower — Orc Exiles - Rift Watchtower
Overhauls the location "Into an Imperial ruin taken over by Orcs": ruined walls and towers, Orc buildings, "a few Bandits", and "Multiple entry points If you're the sneaky type" [9]. The Rift Watchtower is a Rift Hold location in the base game [12].

## Rewards & notable items
- A player-owned small island reached from Honeyside's deck (Riften Docks Overhaul) [2].
- A new general merchant at the Riften warehouse after "Supply and Demand" [4].
