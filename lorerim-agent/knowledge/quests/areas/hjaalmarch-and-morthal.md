# Hjaalmarch and Morthal (Stonehills) in LoreRim

Hjaalmarch is a marsh hold in north-west Skyrim. Its capital is Morthal, and its other settlements include the mining town of Stonehills and the Hearthfire homestead Windstad Manor [10]. LoreRim gives Morthal defences through **The Great City of Morthal**, which adds gates, watchtowers and a stockade, and new unique architecture with matching interiors through **Cities of the North - Morthal** [2][3]. Smaller mods add a free player home, a rebuilt Hroggar's house, a reimagined Stonehills, roadside hot springs, and new marsh ambience [4][6][8][9][12].

## Starting in LoreRim
- Morthal needs no unlock. The town mods are active from the start [13].
- **"Laid to Rest" is a LoreRim gate.** In LoreRim the Dawnguard recruiter is delayed by Sensible Quest Prerequisites "which requires the quest 'Laid to Rest' to be completed. You can start this quest by entering the burned down house in Morthal." You can also skip the recruiter by going straight to Dayspring Canyon [7].
- **New Moon Cottage (free player home):** the door near Falion's House is locked. The key is "inside a satchel in Movarth's Lair, near the hole with several bodies" [4].
- **Seeking the Cure:** with COTN Morthal, LoreRim's patch makes the quest trigger when you enter **Falion's House** as a vampire. If it does not fire, walk around and let Falion talk to you first [5]. Seeking the Cure repurposes "Rising At Dawn" into a story quest in which Falion's cure ritual fails [7].
- **Hroggar's House:** after you meet Eisa Blackthorn at Frostmere Crypt, she moves to Morthal. Once "The Pale Lady" and "Laid to Rest" are done, she rebuilds Hroggar's burned house and becomes recruitable as a follower [6].
- **Fists of Fury - Skyrim** includes brawl quests for Morthal; it starts by letter after you win several vanilla brawls [7].

## Quests
The Morthal and Stonehills mods add no journal quests of their own. The Great City of Morthal has no QUST records with journal text, and Seeking the Cure's patch only edits `VC01` "Seeking A Cure" [1][5]. Quests tied to Morthal are covered in Related: vanilla "Laid to Rest" (a LoreRim Dawnguard gate), Seeking the Cure (Falion), and the Fists of Fury Morthal brawls [5][7].

## Locations
- **Erika's House** — the only new interior from The Great City of Morthal, home of the new NPC **Erika** ("Key to Erika's House") [1].
- **Defences:** gates, watchtowers and a stockade around the town, added because vanilla Morthal had no defences ("even Riverwood having better defenses"). This is a secondary web summary of the mod page; no independent source was found (unverified) [2].
- **New Moon Cottage** — near Falion's House. It has a cooking station, a bookshelf for 54 books, two beds (you and a follower), safe containers and a Shrine of Talos, but no crafting stations [4].
- **COTN Morthal interiors** (vanilla names, new models and interiors): **Highmoon Hall** (Jarl's hall), **Moorside Inn**, **Thaumaturgist's Hut**, **Falion's House**, **Alva's House** and **Alva's Cellar**, **Jorgen and Lami's House**, **Thonnir's House**, **Guardhouse**, **Morthal Jail**. COTN also adds chimney smoke [3].
- **Hroggar's house** — rebuilt over time by Eisa Blackthorn (Environs) [6].
- **Stonehills** — in Hjaalmarch, east of Morthal and west of Dimhollow Crypt, built around Rockwallow Mine and run by Sorli the Builder [10]. Gonzeh's Stonehills ReRe-imagined gives the village unique architecture and reworks **Sorli's House** to match; the other buildings are open-world. The centre is now an abandoned quarry start "until the workers hit an old Dwemer ruin" (the note "Excavation Denied" says the dig lost its funding). It also adds a workers' hut, crafting stations in a new building, and a small bar or mini tavern with a new barmaid [8]. Its new NPCs are **Betsa** and **Brisk** [8].
- **Stonehills Springs** — a series of ponds and hot springs along the road, across from the Stonehills mining camp [9].

## Getting there
- **Carriage:** vanilla Morthal is a carriage destination only [11]. CFTO adds a carriage to Morthal. Its Hjaalmarch destinations are Morthal, Stonehills and Windstad Manor (once you own the land) [11].
- **Ferry:** Morthal and Windstad Manor are on the north-coast ferry route. Windstad Manor is a minor stop until you build a jetty and hire a ferryman [11].
- LoreRim's Better Carriage Destinations preset uses distance pricing (×0.4, maximum 400 gold, time passes) [11].
- Wait Carriage in Inns lists Morthal and Stonehills among its destinations [11].

## LoreRim notes
- Active COTN Morthal patches include The Great City of Morthal merge, Penitus Oculatus, Thrones of Skyrim, AI Overhaul, Nature of the Wildlands, Book Covers, CC Fishing, Katana, Embers XD, Lux / Lux Orbis, Northern Roads, and a CFTO patch for The Great City of Morthal [13].
- The Great City of Morthal has no cached Nexus description in the install, so its feature list comes from a web search summary [1][2].
- **The Haunting Harmonies of Hjaalmarch** adds marsh ambience: eerie day/night wind loops, insects, and optional silenced music in Morthal's swamps, via Sound Record Distributor [12].
- Stonehills ReRe-imagined ships a College of Winterhold - Quest Expansion patch, which LoreRim loads (`Gonz - Stonehills ReRe - Jay College.esp`), adjusting NPC positions [8][13].
