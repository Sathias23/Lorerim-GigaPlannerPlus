# Solstheim (LoreRim changes to the Dragonborn island)

Solstheim is the Dragonborn DLC island; Raven Rock (south-west coast), Skaal Village and Tel Mithryn are its main settlements [1]. LoreRim adds no new worldspace here, but it rebuilds Skaal Village with a hidden player home, adds a small Skaal fishing camp, random earthquakes, cannibal draugr, a Missives board, an overhauled Abandoned Lodge, and a large set of Raven Rock/Tel Mithryn visual overhauls [4][5][6][7][9][13][14][15]. Quests on the island are covered in `vanilla-changes/dragonborn.md` and `mod-added/miasma.md`.

## Starting in LoreRim
- **Getting there:** In LoreRim, "after completing 'The Way of the Voice' you will be attacked by Miraak's cultists" [2]. After the fight, read the Cultists' Orders and go to the Windhelm docks, where Gjalund Salt-Sage's ship the Northern Maiden takes you to Solstheim (he must first be talked, paid or intimidated into it) [3]. The quest is "Dragonborn" (objectives: Find out who sent the Cultists; Read Cultists' Orders; Travel to Solstheim) [16].
- Once you have docked at Raven Rock, "To Skyrim" and "To Solstheim" map markers allow fast travel between the two [1].
- Because LoreRim's main quest is optional and delayed (you start it at the Helgen inn unless you chose the Dragonborn start), Solstheim's questline comes after a good part of the main quest [2][17].
- **Miasma** (Solstheim Ashlands quest) starts by speaking to Haj-Xul in the Retching Netch; LoreRim recommends level 20+ [17].

## Quests
None of the Solstheim mods in this file adds a story quest [4][7][9]. Solstheim Earthquakes ships a start-game-enabled helper quest "Earthquakes" whose only journal lines are "Show settings" and "Test Quake" — it is the earthquake engine/MCM, not a player quest [9]. The Missives patch adds a Missives board at Raven Rock with a "Kill Rieklings" job [15]. For the Dragonborn questline (LoreRim: Miraak has a cult of dragon priests that buff him) see `vanilla-changes/dragonborn.md` [2]; LoreRim also moves the Bow of Shadows to static loot "as part of the Raven Rock/Morag Tong questline" [18].

## Locations

### Raven Rock
- Vanilla services: inn The Retching Netch; Fethis Alor (general goods), Glover Mallory (blacksmith), Garyn Ienth (food), Milore Ienth (alchemist/Alchemy trainer); player home Severin Manor after "Served Cold"; the Northern Maiden at the long pier [10].
- **Raven Rock Building Tweaks** retextures the stone-pattern buildings with Redoran textures "to make Raven Rock look more like one cohesive Dunmer settlement"; it only overrides texture paths [11].
- **Missives - Solstheim** puts a Missives board "outside Morvayn Manor in Raven Rock" [15].
- **Brazier Solstheim - Base Object Swapper** (`RavenRockBrazier.esl`) swaps Raven Rock braziers; no description is cached beyond recommended mods (visual only, details unverified) [12].
- **Skaal Fishing Camp:** "a small fishing camp to the west of Raven Rock. Haki and his wife will roam the camp doing various work, and sell seafood and other ingredients" [6].

### Skaal Village
- **Skaal Village Overhaul** "replaces every house with a new exterior model and interior, except the Greathall"; interiors become "small, cozy huts" that keep most of their loot [4].
- **Player home (Vintrhus):** the plugin location is named **Skaal Village Player House** [5]. It "needs a key to get in, you can find it in a knapsack at Snowclad Ruins by the altar with dead animals" [4]; the plugin names the items "Key to Vintrhus" and "Torkilds' Knapsack" [5]. Inside are two buttons: one enables a basement trapdoor (follower/children beds), the other the children's beds; spouse/children use requires Hearthfire Multiple Adoptions per the author [4].
- **Skaal Greathall Variant** replaces the Greathall model to match, keeping the interior layout "as untouched as possible" [8].

### Island-wide
- **Earthquakes:** by default a 5% chance of a quake every 120 seconds, random duration and intensity; standing NPCs stagger and you cower ("jump after the earthquakes to get out of cower"); dragons are immune; only on Solstheim [9]. LoreRim keeps the "earthquake" MCM menu enabled; no LoreRim override of the defaults was found in "LoreRim - MCM and INI Settings" [19].
- **Cannibal Draugr on Solstheim:** Solstheim variants of every draugr (299 NPCs) with "Bloodied mouths and beards", "Glowing red eyes", blackened skin, plus a "Grave Tar" ingredient [14].
- **Abandoned Lodge:** overhauled with a completely new interior; it is the site of the vanilla quest "A New Source of Stalhrim" where a missing blacksmith is held by Thalmor [13].
- **Visual overhauls:** Rally's Solstheim AIO (Raven Rock, Tel Mithryn, Riekling Outposts, landscapes, plants, scathecraw, Dark Elf furniture, shrines) with its ParallaxGen add-on [7]; Kanjs' animated Dunmer plinth shrines [7]; Subtleties of Skyrim makes Red Mountain lava, distant ships in the Sea of Ghosts, and Vvardenfell mushrooms visible from Solstheim [7].

## Rewards & notable items
- **Skaal Village Player House (Vintrhus)** — free player home; key at Snowclad Ruins [4][5].
- Seafood/ingredient vendors at the Skaal Fishing Camp [6].
- Grave Tar ingredient from Solstheim draugr [14].

## LoreRim notes
- Also enabled for Solstheim: JK's Raven Rock (+ patch collection), JK's Tel Mithryn (+ patch collection), Tel Mithryn Overhaul, Snazzy Raven Rock AIO, SOFA on Solstheim (furniture swaps in the Reclamations Temple, Severin Manor and Morvayn Manor), Shaders of Solstheim, Freak's Floral Solstheim, Solstheim Exterior Soundscapes, Solstheim Mounted Heads, and the Solstheim and Baan Malur paper map [15][20]. Their content beyond names/short descriptions was not reviewed.
- The Skaal Village Overhaul author says it is "NOT COMPATIBLE with JK's" (a JK's Skaal Village overhaul); LoreRim ships JK's Raven Rock and Tel Mithryn, not a JK's Skaal Village mod [4][20].
- The Missives patch page also describes a Bruma board, but LoreRim only ships the `Missives - Solstheim.esp` plugin [15].
