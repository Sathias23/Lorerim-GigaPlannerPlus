# Whiterun Hold (Whiterun, Riverwood, Rorikstead) in LoreRim

Whiterun Hold is the central hold of Skyrim; its capital is Whiterun and its towns include Riverwood and Rorikstead [15]. LoreRim's biggest change here is **Capital Whiterun Expansion** (CWE), shipped as the original mod's archives plus Rob's Bug Fixes' replacement plugin `SurWR.esp`, which adds two new districts, new shops, merchants, NPCs, the player home **Bluesky Hall**, and a set of small quests [1][4][5]. Smaller mods rebuild Whiterun's walls, keep the Western Watchtower intact until the first dragon attack, turn the stables into a farm, and expand Rorikstead with a Great Hall [8][9][10][13][14].

## Starting in LoreRim
- Whiterun needs no unlock; the CWE quests start from notes, gossip and NPCs in and around the city (details per quest below) [1][2].
- **Bluesky Hall:** read the "For Sale!" notice — the deed "as well as various trinkets" are sold by the seller's agent **Quintus**, "most likely … by the well in the marketplace" [2]. The quest "Bluesky Hall" then says the hall is "in the city district behind the Hall of the Dead" [1]. The price was not found in the records (unverified).
- **LoreRim gate that runs through Whiterun:** Wyrmstooth's courier, Theodyn Bienne, tracks you down "starting from the Bannered Mare in Whiterun", but only after you reach level 10 and finish "Way of the Voice" and the Windhelm quest "Rise in the East" [18].
- LoreRim also enables **Capital Whiterun Expansion Lite**, a Base Object Swapper INI that disables "more than 200 objects" of CWE for performance. Some decorative clutter from CWE screenshots may therefore be missing in game [6].

## Quests
All of these come from `SurWR.esp` (Rob's Bug Fixes - Capital Whiterun Expansion) [1].

### Ode To The Tundrastriders
- **Giver / trigger:** the note "Jarl's Bounty: Giant" ("The giants outside the city has a new chieftain…") or asking "Anything new happening around town?" [1][2].
- **Where:** Whiterun, then the giant camp in **Bleakwind Basin** [1].
- **Steps:** Deal with the Giant outside Whiterun → (optional) ask around town for alternatives → read **Urik's note** → give Urik 300 gold for a cow → buy blue dye (50 gold, from a shop in town) → bring a copy of the poem "Ode to the Tundrastriders" to Urik → get paint from the bucket → paint **Gertie the Cow** → have Gertie follow you → speak to the Giant Chieftain → (optional) tell Urik → see the Jarl's Steward in Dragonsreach [1][2].
- **Choices & outcomes:** fight the chieftain; poison the giants' food (a giant-strength poison costs 100 gold from the witch at the alchemy shop, or 25 gold in supplies if you brew it yourself); or negotiate in shouted verse with the giant **Hrungnir**. A truce stops the giants harassing travellers who keep away from the camp, and the chieftain will trade with you. The Jarl "would have preferred the Giant to be dead", so the truce pays less, or nothing on one branch [1][2].
- **Rewards:** gold from the steward; giant trading after a truce [1].

### Skirmish at Whitewatch
- **Giver / trigger:** a messenger's note ("I've received a note by a messenger from Whiterun") [1]. What triggers the courier was not found; the text is addressed to the Thanes of Whiterun (unverified gate: being Thane of Whiterun).
- **Where:** Whitewatch Tower [1].
- **Steps:** Read the message → speak to **Thane Hroa Hearth-Healer** at Whitewatch Tower → keep watch (optional small talk with the other Thanes) → defend the tower → regroup with Hroa → defend against the final raid → report to the Steward in Dragonsreach [1].
- **Enemies:** "strange raiders" bringing wild beasts and siege equipment. The plugin defines a **War Mammoth**, a **Trained Sabre Cat** and **Tundra Raider** NPCs for it [1][2].

### Running Wild
- **Giver / trigger:** the notice "A reward in gold!", offering the white stallion's weight in gold [2].
- **Steps:** Catch the white (palomino) horse roaming the fields → bring it to Whiterun's main gate → talk to the stablemaster outside Dragonsreach **or** to **Joslin** in the small stables behind the Bannered Mare [1].
- **Choices & outcomes:** Dragonsreach pays gold and the Jarl keeps the horse. Joslin pays nothing, but you keep the horse and she makes you an Imperial or a Nord saddle [1].

### A Rat Problem
- **Giver:** **Igna**, in Whiterun [1].
- **Steps:** deal with whatever is in Igna's basement (**Igna's Basement**) → report back [1]. The thing down there "sure wasn't a normal rat": the plugin has a boss **Ratmulaag**, a "Conjurer's Journal" and a "Spell Tome: Conjure Skeever" tied to this quest [1][2].
- **Outcomes:** paid by Igna, or Igna "doesn't want anything more to do with me" [1].

### Bluesky Hall
- The house-purchase tracker: "Inspect your new property, the Key seems to be wrapped inside the Deed" [1].

### Grace of Kynareth / Victory Celebrations / Farmland Saga
- **Grace of Kynareth:** after you help Danica, Gildergreen saplings start growing all around Whiterun [1].
- **Victory Celebrations:** guards show off a new trophy by the statue of Talos, and you receive a gift [1].
- **Farmland Saga:** its journal text reads like design notes (help a ruined farmer as an equal, take over his farm, or evict him; then rebuild the farm). It has no objectives and no quest type, so it may be unfinished or unused (low confidence) [1].

## Locations
New `SurWR.esp` locations [1], with what the plugin records show about them [2]:
- **Bluesky Hall** — purchasable player home behind the Hall of the Dead. The "For Sale!" notice promises a feast hall, a defensible second floor, a kitchen and crafting space [1][2].
- **Happy Homestead General Goods** — construction shop ("Construction Shop Key"). Its owner **Elam** buys "all sorts of goods" [2].
- **Brightflour Bakery** — the baker **Scylla** sells meals [2].
- **Witch's Wisdoms** — alchemy shop; **Jaeshe** sells potions and ingredients [2].
- **The Roadhouse** (owner **Keld**) and **Whiterun Customs Hall** (East Empire clerks Caula Statlilia and Sturn) — outside the walls. The Tweak Patch author notes CWE adds just three buildings outside the walls: the Roadhouse, the Customs Hall and a courier [2][7].
- **Home of Thane Valdemaar** (Thane Valdemaar Steel-Arm) and **Home of Thane Hearth-Healer** (Thane Hroa Hearth-Healer) [2].
- **Igna's House**, **Igna's Basement**, **Joslin's House**, **Titus' House**, **Karlsten's House**, **Erundil's House**, **Ronja's House**, **Fine-Hair's House**, **Wanderer's House**, **Warehouse**, **Guard Tower** [1].
- **Merchants on the road to the main gate:** **Honditar** (bows), **Basil** (Redguard trader), **Urik**, and **Vinopola Vlanarus** [2]. The mod page describes these as an Imperial wine merchant, a Redguard trader and his daughter selling Hammerfell goods, a Nord fur trader, and an Altmer merchant (secondary source) [4].
- **Great Hall (Rorikstead)** — The Great City of Rorikstead's only new interior [13]. The mod turns the area around Rorik's manor and the inn into an estate with extra crops, windmills and shacks. Since its v1.2 the Great Hall is a public area [14].

## Other changes in the hold
- **City walls:** Roastlawyer's Restored Whiterun Defences rebuilds the drawbridge bailey and towers with walkable walls, rebuilds the main entrance building, and turns the barracks tower into a climbable multi-level tower (new interior **Guard Barracks**) [8].
- **Western Watchtower:** stays undamaged until "Dragon Rising" begins, then reverts to the vanilla ruin [9]. The Siberpunk's Cut add-on then gradually rebuilds its walls after the attack [9].
- **Whiterun Stables:** turned into "a little farm" [10].
- **Talos statue:** after an Imperial victory in the Battle for Whiterun, the Talos statue and shrine are removed after 2–5 days, and a Kynareth statue goes up 5–7 days later. Danica gains new lines [11].
- **Stables scene:** a restored cut scene between Skulvar and Uthgerd outside the Whiterun stables. Only this piece of Cut Content Restoration ships; Barleydark Farm and the other restored farms are not in LoreRim's profile [12][5].
- **Guards:** Whiterun Hold guards are neutral to both civil-war sides until the Battle for Whiterun [19].
- **Riverwood:** JK's Riverwood (a script-free town overhaul), Ryn's Riverwood Trader (redesigned interior), and Riverwood Trader Is A Mess. In the last, the shop is actually messy, and you can help Lucan clean it (6 in-game hours) for a little gold and a random item [19].

## Getting there
- Whiterun has a carriage in vanilla. LoreRim runs Carriage and Ferry Travel Overhaul, whose Whiterun Hold destinations are Whiterun, Rorikstead and Riverwood [16].
- LoreRim layers **Better Carriage Destinations** on top. You pick any map marker, and the price scales with distance. LoreRim's MCM preset sets cost ×0.4, maximum 400 gold, a dangerous-route multiplier of 0.8, and time passing during travel [16].
- **Wait Carriage in Inns** lets you wait for a carriage at minor inns. Its destinations include Riverwood and Rorikstead [17].

## LoreRim notes
- The `SurWR.esp` that runs is Rob's Bug Fixes' cleaned replacer for CWE 1.5 "Normal Version", with USSEP edits forwarded [3]. The original mod folder supplies the archives and two 49-byte stub plugins [5].
- CWE Lite disables >200 CWE objects via Base Object Swapper [6]. The Tweak Patch moves trees that clipped CWE buildings and removes the boardwalk [7].
- Battle-Brew mead deliveries from Windhelm ("The Mead Must Flow") send you to the Bannered Mare — see [eastmarch-and-windhelm.md](eastmarch-and-windhelm.md) [5].
