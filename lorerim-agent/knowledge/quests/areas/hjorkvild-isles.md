# Hjorkvild Isles (Siege at Icemoth)

*Siege at Icemoth* adds the **Hjorkvild Isles**, a group of islands in the Sea of Ghosts with a worldspace of their own [1][2]. The LoreRim site describes it as "a small scale vanilla-esque new lands mod". It adds new islands, locations, dungeons, and unique items from *The Elder Scrolls: Blades* [4]. In the story, mercenaries working for Marus Antonius came here looking for the **Band of the Wraith**, a ring held by the necromancer **Silas Marceau**. He moved into Fort Icemoth, an Imperial naval fort abandoned about 20 years earlier [3]. The mod also includes a Black Book and a new voiced shout [3].

## Starting in LoreRim
- **Getting there:** go to the **Old Wooden Jetty** "at the most north western beach in Skyrim", or travel west from Northwatch Keep until you reach it [3][4]. Both the Old Wooden Jetty and a "Hjorkvild Isles" travel marker are placed in Skyrim's own worldspace [2].
- **Unlocking the boat:** read the **waterlogged journal** in the dead mercenary's inventory. This starts *Siege at Icemoth* and makes the boat usable [3][4]. The quest's first journal entry calls it a corpse "by a beached sailboat" whose journal mentions Fort Icemoth "far north of Jehanna just over the horizon" [1].
- **No level gate:** the quest record is start-game-enabled, and neither the LoreRim site nor the mod page mentions a level gate or delayed start [1][3][4]. The LoreRim site's instructions match the mod page [3][4].
- **Ferries:** LoreRim ships *Skyrim Ferries*, whose add-on list includes Siege at Icemoth (`Ferries - Siege At Icemoth Addon.esp`) [5][6]. Its exact routes were not verified.
- Inside the isles' worldspace there are five "Sailboat" markers and a "Skyrim" marker, presumably the boat links back to the mainland. Their exact behaviour was not verified [2].

## Quests
Full detail is in [mod-added/siege-at-icemoth.md](../mod-added/siege-at-icemoth.md).

### Siege at Icemoth
- **Trigger:** the waterlogged journal at the Old Wooden Jetty [1][3].
- **Steps:** travel to Fort Icemoth → deal with the source of the undead there. The quest ends once the necromancer **Silas Marceau** and his undead army are defeated [1].

### The Topaz Claw
- **Trigger:** Silas's journal mentions an ancient Nord dragon-claw key [1].
- **Steps:** obtain the **Topaz Dragon Claw**, a silver-and-topaz claw found among Silas's things → use it on the portcullis of an ancient Nord barrow on the main island → explore **Winter Shroud Sanctum** → defeat the dragon priest **Tovinaan** [1].
- The author notes that Winter Shroud Sanctum was first planned for Beyond Skyrim: Cyrodiil [3].

### Black Book: The Font of Memory
- Reading the Black Book takes you to Hermaeus Mora's realm of **Apocrypha**, where you uncover its hidden knowledge. Read the book again to escape [1]. Where the book is found on the isles is not stated in the sources read.

### Caches of Skorjan Iron-Beard
- **Steps:** read *The Caches of Skorjan Iron-Beard* → travel to the Old Wooden Jetty → (optional) investigate the dead mercenary → find the hidden cache near the jetty [1].

## Locations
Names come from the plugin's location records and map markers [1][2].
- **Fort Icemoth:** the abandoned Imperial naval fort and the necromancer's base. Interiors: Fort Icemoth, Fort Icemoth Dungeons, Fort Icemoth Storage Room [1][2]. According to the lore, the fort protected East Empire Company whale-product trade with the Nords of Fjolgen against corsairs [3].
- **Fjolgen:** the local Nord settlement [1][3]. The mod page notes its houses use Raven Rock models "because they're actually EEC colonial houses" [3].
- **Winter Shroud Sanctum:** an ancient Nord barrow with the Winter Shroud Catacombs. The dragon priest Tovinaan is here [1][2].
- **Dungeons and caves:** Saervild's Trench, Frostcaller Cave, Rimewind Grotto, Ghoruun Hall [1][2].
- **Shipwrecks and coast:** Eyndis' Folly, Wreck of The Northern Grace (location "Northern Grace"), Hjaalskar's Point, Abandoned Fishing Hut, Freezewater Plunge (map marker only) [1][2].
- **On the Skyrim mainland:** the Old Wooden Jetty, the departure point [1][2].
- **Other records:** an "East Empire Company Warehouse" location and a "The Mearl Pearl" interior cell. The plugin also edits interior cells named Dragonsreach and The Arcanaeum. Their roles are not documented in the sources read [1][2].
- The mod page describes the isles as "Fully navmeshed and follower friendly" [3].

## Rewards & notable items
- The **Band of the Wraith** ring is the goal of the mercenaries' expedition [3].
- **Crimson Kiss** is "In a chest beside the rear end of Eyndis' Folly (in a small rock opening in the sea floor)" [3].
- The mod adds items and a skeleton variant from *TES: Blades*, plus a new shout [3].

## Dangers and level hints
- Undead and the necromancer Silas Marceau at Fort Icemoth, and the dragon priest Tovinaan in Winter Shroud Sanctum [1]. No source gives a recommended level.
- The author suggests enabling the Arcane Archer pack and zombie Creations "For Extra challenge" [3]. Whether LoreRim does this was not checked.

## LoreRim notes
- LoreRim ships *Siege at Icemoth - Fishing Patch* (`HYORFishingIntegrationPatch.esp`), *Metallurgy - Siege at Icemoth*, a Lux lighting patch, an occlusion add-on, a waves patch, and the FWMF paper map for the isles [5]. No Requiem-specific patch for the mod was found in the install [5].
- The site lists Siege at Icemoth under New Lands. It does not contradict the mod's own start instructions [4].
