# Winterhold (town and hold) in LoreRim

In vanilla, Winterhold is a near-empty village next to the College, the remnant of a city that mostly fell into the sea in the Great Collapse [3]. LoreRim stacks **The Great City of Winterhold** (v4), which restores walls, ruins and new buildings in an architecture "between the ancient Nordic architecture of Windhelm and the high medieval sophistication of Solitude", with **Cities of the North - Winterhold**, which gives every building a unique model and matching interiors [3][4]. A combined patch, `TGCotN Winterhold.esp`, merges the two [5][12]. The town adds NPCs, vendors and a home, but **no quests**: the mod page says the new voiced NPCs "do not offer any quests" [3].

## Starting in LoreRim
- No unlock is needed to visit. The town expansions are always active [12].
- **Frostview Hall (player home):** townsfolk mention "a house for sale… Frostview Hall. Talk to that rat in the keep, Malur Seloth." Ask the steward "I would like to purchase a house in the city now that I am thane", then "I would like to buy Frostview Hall. (4000)". He hands over the **Key to Frostview's Hall** [2]. The mod page confirms the home "becomes available after achieving the position of thane" [3].
- **The Chill (jail):** with Immersive Winterhold Jail it becomes an explorable location with a frost atronach boss and two ways of escape [7].
- The mod author states The Great City of Winterhold needs a new game to work fully. LoreRim ships it from the start, so this matters only for mid-playthrough updates [3].

## Quests
The town mods add no journal quests. Their quest records are scene and dialogue helpers (Frode/Skadi, Magna/Chayim scenes, the house-purchase dialogue) [1]. Quests that pass through Winterhold:
- **The Mead Must Flow** (Capital Windhelm Expansion): deliver Battle-Brew mead to the **Frozen Hearth** [11].
- **Knight of the North:** the mod author recommends starting near the **Tower Stone in Winterhold** [10].
- College questlines (College of Winterhold - Quest Expansion, Finding Velehk Sain, Tools of Kagrenac): see Related [10].

## Locations
New `The Great City of Winterhold v4.esp` locations [1], with residents from the plugin's NPC, key and cell records [2]:
- **Frostview Hall** — purchasable player home (4000 gold, thane only) [2].
- **Hall of Runes** — a library run by the **Jhunal Cult**, Nords devoted to Jhunal, "the Nordic god of knowledge and wisdom, represented by the owl and known to the Imperials as Julianos". The keeper **Chayim** tells buyers to talk to **Skadi** (Skadi Frost-Vein). **Frode Snow-Owl** speaks for the cult [2]. The mod page lists a spellbook vendor among its two new vendors [3].
- **Magnar's House** — **Magnar Frost-Vein** is the new blacksmith ("Return anytime you need arms or armor. My forge never rests.") [2][3]. Clan Frost-Vein also includes Magna, Njal and Skadi (record "House of Clan Frost-Vein") [2].
- **Magna's House** — **Magna Frost-Vein** "mostly take[s], without the giving" and sends traders to Birna [2].
- **Frida's House**, **Runi's House**, **Chayim's House** (cell "Chayim and Euthymia's House") [1][2].
- **Winterhold Guard House** (cell "Winterhold Guardhouse"), **Winterhold Docks**, **Winterhold Warehouse** [1][2].
- **Stonecold Fortress** — the Jarl's longhouse. The Great City of Winterhold names this cell "Frostveil Fastness", but COTN Winterhold, the TGCotN merge patch, LoreRim - World Fixes and the last overriding plugins all name it **Stonecold Fortress**, so that is the in-game name in LoreRim [6]. COTN also changes the longhouse music to match the major cities [4].
- **The Chill** — overhauled jail with an interior overhaul, exterior details, a new frost atronach boss, interactions, and two escape routes. Its records add an **Ancient Guard**, a **Cellmate**, a **Ghost Prisoner**, a **Key to the Ice Door** and a **Key to the tunnel** [7].
- Vanilla buildings keep their names but get new COTN models and interiors: **The Frozen Hearth**, **Birna's Oddments**, **Kraldar's House** [4][6].

## Getting there
- **Carriage:** in vanilla, Winterhold can be a carriage destination but has no carriage of its own [9]. LoreRim's CFTO fix adds **a carriage to Winterhold**, and its knapsacks can be looted at the risk of angering the driver [8]. CFTO's base tariff charges Winterhold the higher "distant minor settlement" price "due to its remote location" [9].
- **Ferry:** Winterhold is a north-coast ferry stop (… Dawnstar – Frostflow Lighthouse – Winterhold – Windhelm) [9].
- LoreRim's Better Carriage Destinations preset replaces the fixed tariff with distance pricing: ×0.4, maximum 400 gold [9].

## LoreRim notes
- Requiem patch: `TGCotN Winterhold - Requiem Patch.esp` is active [12].
- Other patches in the profile include AI Overhaul, Embers XD, Lux / Lux Orbis, Katana, Northern Roads and a CFTO patch for TGC Winterhold [12].
- The Patch Collection's compatibility notes (Alternate Start: Live Another Life markers, AI Overhaul idle markers, Frozen Hearth book placements) are why these mods coexist in LoreRim [5].
