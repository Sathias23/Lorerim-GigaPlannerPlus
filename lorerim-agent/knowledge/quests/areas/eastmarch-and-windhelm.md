# Eastmarch and Windhelm (Kynesgrove, Mixwater Mill, Darkwater Crossing) in LoreRim

Eastmarch is Skyrim's eastern hold. Its capital is Windhelm, and its settlements include Kynesgrove, Darkwater Crossing and Mixwater Mill [14]. In LoreRim, Windhelm is heavily expanded by **Capital Windhelm Expansion** (`WindhelmSSE.esp`): new buildings, shops, a restored Pit arena where you can fight repeatedly, a museum, and a dozen small quests, with voiced NPCs [1][2][3]. The hold's villages get The Great Cities treatment: Kynesgrove, Mixwater Mill and Darkwater Crossing are rebuilt with Eastmarch-style architecture [6][7][8].

## Starting in LoreRim
- Windhelm is open from the start, and every Capital Windhelm Expansion quest is picked up in town. Givers and triggers are listed per quest below [1][2].
- **Graystone Lodge** (player home, Gray Quarter): the "For Sale" note says to buy the deed from **Sadri** in Sadri's Used Wares for **1000 gold** ("The smith above tends to make some noise"). The quest is named "Graystone" [1][2].
- **Kynesby** (player home, Kynesgrove): ask **Gala** about the abandoned home and pay **2000 septims**. It is "the two-story home near the wheatfield" [6].
- **LoreRim gates that touch Windhelm:** Wyrmstooth requires "Rise in the East", where you help the East Empire Company in Windhelm deal with pirate attacks, on top of "Way of the Voice" [15]. Fists of Fury - Skyrim includes brawl quests for Windhelm and starts by letter after you win several vanilla brawls [15].
- **Dark Elf players:** with Windhelm Segregation, Elda Early-Dawn refuses you a room at Candlehearth Hall. Ambarys Rendar rents you the upstairs bed at the New Gnisis Cornerclub instead [10].

## Quests
All from `WindhelmSSE.esp` (Capital Windhelm Expansion) [1].

### Collecting the Edda - Windhelm
- **Giver:** **Higil** (Higil Hammer-Shield), after you read his book [1][2].
- **Steps:** collect a verse from each of **Thane Alarik** (Higher Valunstrad, west of the palace, or at court), **Jorn Scarred-Skald** (Iron Quarter, near the Pit; evenings at the Iron Hall), **Hellte** (the Iron Hall's serving maid), **Audmund** (his axe shop between the Gray Quarter and the Iron Quarter), **Tormund Tall-Tale** (the old bookshop right of Candlehearth Hall), **Sulvar** (the Old Wheelhouse outside the city, through a gate from the market), **Frida the Younger** (Valunstrad; also Wilhelm's place in the market or the Five Hundred's Wall), and **Hjarrandi** (Hall of Relics) → return all verses to Higil [1].
- Note: the objective says "Skulvar's verse", the journal says "Sulvar", and the NPC record is "Sulvar the Steady" [1][2].

### Kyne's Trial / Hunting Trip
- **Giver:** **Frida the Younger** ("If you need any more pelts, I could go hunting") [1][2].
- **Kyne's Trial steps:** take a carriage from the Windhelm Stables to the hills (a separate "Somewhere in the Mountains" worldspace) → find and slay the great bear → talk to Frida → ride back. You keep the pelt [1].
- **Hunting Trip (repeatable):** bring Frida pelts — six Wolf, three Sabre Cat, three Bear, four Ice Wolf, two Snow Sabre Cat, two Snow Bear, or three Cave Bear [1].

### Pit Fighter (repeatable)
- **Giver:** ask **Benkum** "Who do I talk to if I want to fight in the pit?". He sends you to **Huki Seven-Swords** in the **Bloodworks** [1][2].
- **Steps:** "I want to fight in an unranked match" → head down to the Pit → fight (you are moved into **The Pit** worldspace) → return to Huki ("I've earned my pay in the Pit") [1][2].

### Severed Cold
- **Trigger:** a note or letter, then a guard investigation into Dunmer attacked outside Windhelm's **East Gate** [1].
- **Steps:** you fall into the **Frozen Caverns** → explore → (optional) slay the **Volkihar Brute** or run away → speak to the guardsman → report to **Wuunferth** in the Palace of the Kings [1].
- **Story:** Volkihar vampires came through the ice and dragged the Dunmer into the caverns, where they were all turned. Killing the vampire lord ends the nest. If you flee, Wuunferth deals with it, but the guards who went in die. Enemies include an **Exiled Volkihar Warlord**, a **Volkihar Brute** and **Bloodstarved Fledgling**s [1][2].

### The Talos Mistake
- **Giver:** **Beorn** hands you "Hverung's Manifesto" [1][2].
- **Steps:** speak to **Hverung** → kill **or** warn the Thalmor agent hiding in the Gray Quarter (his note says the agent likely lives in a rooftop loft on the Gray Quarter's upper level) → bring Hverung the agent's ears and dossier, **or** kill Hverung [1][2].
- **Outcome:** loyal to Hverung, you may use his headquarters (likely the **Mustering Hall**, whose record ID is `0WHSonsOfTalosHQLocation`), and he hints at future tasks. Kill him and his plans end [1].

### Unusual Imports
- **Giver:** **Signy**, boarding up a warehouse ("Why are you boarding up the warehouse?") [1][2].
- **Steps:** enter the warehouse and kill the **rieklings** that stowed away on a shipment from Solstheim → search it, then tell Signy the job is done (you can also just claim it is) → collect your reward [1].

### The Mead Must Flow (one-off and repeatable)
- **Giver:** the **Battle-Brew Meadery** ("How's the meadery working out? Need any help?") [1][2].
- **Steps:** deliver Battle-Brew reserve to the **Bannered Mare** (Whiterun), the **Frozen Hearth** (Winterhold) or **Candlehearth Hall** (Windhelm). The one-off version goes to the Frozen Hearth [1].

### A Simple Delivery
- **Giver:** **Wilhelm**. Deliver a purchase agreement to Higil's home in the Upper Valunstrad (leave it on his desk if he is out), then tell Wilhelm [1].

### Graystone / Death Do Us Part / A Night You Can't Remember
- **Graystone:** house-purchase tracker for Graystone Lodge [1].
- **Death Do Us Part:** carry two rings to a certain spot to "reunite two dead lovers" (a Dunmer couple) [1].
- **A Night You Can't Remember:** a drinking blackout that begins at Candlehearth Hall and ends with "Get some fresh air". Its journal stages are mostly placeholders [1].

## Locations
New `WindhelmSSE.esp` locations [1], annotated from plugin NPC/book/dialogue records [2]:
- **Shops:** **Wilhelm's Winter Wares** (Wilhelm), **Bread & Salt** and **The Bakeship Valand** (bakeries), **Tormund's Tales** (Tormund Tall-Tale's bookshop), **Audmund's Axes** (Audmund), **Mathendi's Magics and Imports** (Gray Quarter slums) [1][2]. The mod page mentions "a pit arena, some bakeries, a museum, three new merchants" (secondary source) [3].
- **The Iron Halls** — Iron Quarter tavern and lodging, with **Morganna and Hellte's Lodging** and **Ulf and Ringvild's Lodging** [1].
- **The Pit** (worldspace) and **Bloodworks** — the arena and its fighters' quarters (Huki Seven-Swords, Benkum, Brond) [1][2].
- **Hall of Relics** — museum, home of Hjarrandi [1].
- **Mustering Hall** — Hverung's headquarters (see The Talos Mistake) [1].
- **Battle-Brew Meadery**, **Wallside Inn**, **Cult of Tsun**, **Temple Location**, **Thalmor Embassy** (a Windhelm-internal location record; its purpose was not confirmed) [1].
- **Gray Quarter slums:** **Varasas' Loft**, **Sulen's Loft**, **Rooftop Passage**, **The Rooftop** [1].
- **Homes:** **Graystone Lodge** (player), **Home of Thane Alarik**, **Home of Thane Skjorta**, **Home of Clan Hammer-Shield**, **Dockmaster's Home**, **Shack**, **Home** [1].
- **Dockside Warehouse** (Unusual Imports) and **Frozen Caverns** (Severed Cold) [1].
- **Somewhere in the Mountains** — the worldspace used by Kyne's Trial [1].
- **Kynesgrove** (The Great Village of Kynesgrove) — new location records **Kynesgrove** (farms), **Kymesby** (the record's spelling; the key and dialogue say "Kynesby"), **Gala's Stead**, **Herleif's and Ragna's Farm**, **Pilgrim's House**, **Hall of Kyne** and **Roggi's House** [5][6]. The expanded sacred grove is guarded by **Eskil** of the Keepers of the Grove and visited by pilgrims. **Herleif Knot-Beard** is the new blacksmith [6][7].
- **Mixwater Mill** — **Leif's and Kyne's Farm**, home of a "proud (though bigoted)" Nord family (Leif, Kyne, Haukr). The mill and farmhouses use new Eastmarch meshes [5][8].
- **Darkwater Crossing** — **Verner and Annekke's House** is rebuilt in Great Cities Eastmarch architecture [9].

## Other changes in the hold
- **Windhelm Bridge Revived:** a guardhouse under the bridge, stairs, battlements and portcullises, and 8 Stormcloak/Imperial guards whose faction gear changes with the civil war [11].
- **ALT - Windhelm Entrance - Defensive Towers:** two decorative towers at the entrance that cannot be entered [11].
- **Dialogue Expansion - Windhelm:** NPC greetings react to your race (beast races and elves are looked down on), sex, civil-war allegiance and completed Windhelm quests. It adds 30 scenes, including 18 in Candlehearth Hall, market and Cornerclub scenes, and the first dockworker scenes [12].

## Getting there
- Windhelm has a carriage in vanilla. Under CFTO, Eastmarch carriage destinations are Windhelm, Kynesgrove, Darkwater Crossing and Mixwater Mill, and Windhelm is the eastern end of the north-coast ferry route [13].
- LoreRim's Better Carriage Destinations preset lets you pick any map marker: cost ×0.4, maximum 400 gold, time passes [13].
- Wait Carriage in Inns lets you call a carriage at minor-settlement inns (destinations include Kynesgrove and Darkwater Crossing) [16].

## LoreRim notes
- **Capital Windhelm Expansion Lite** is enabled. It disables "more than 150 objects" of CWE via Base Object Swapper for performance [4].
- LoreRim also loads CWE fix mods: Collision Fixes, the Eastern gate blackscreen fix, a USSEP patch, Parallax Fix, Icicle performance, and an Embers XD patch [5].
- Kynesgrove runs Rob's Bug Fixes' replacer plugin (cleaned, improved navmesh, USSEP forwards), with the original mod's assets [5][7].
- The Nexus description for Capital Windhelm Expansion is not cached in the install (`meta.ini` holds none), so the feature summary relies on a web search summary [3].
