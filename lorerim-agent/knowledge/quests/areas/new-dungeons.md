# New and Overhauled Dungeons in LoreRim

LoreRim ships a set of small dungeon mods that put new interiors into Skyrim and Solstheim. Some are new sites, such as Forsaken Crypt and Dragon's Teeth Prison. Others add a dungeon to a vanilla exterior that previously had none, such as Snowpoint Beacon and the Shrine of Azura [1][2][3][5]. Several quest mods also add large dungeons: the Ever-Bog, Itharzel, the flooded Ayleid ruins under Lake Honrich, and the Ashbound Prison [16][17][18][19]. A few mods only rebuild existing vanilla interiors: Redwater Den, the Abandoned Lodge and Mara's Eye Den [12][14][15]. All mods listed here are enabled in LoreRim's Default profile [1].

## Starting in LoreRim
- **Standalone dungeons** (Forsaken Crypt, Dragon's Teeth Prison, Snowpoint Bastion, Ven Fillaan Miin, Morihaus' Refuge) have no quest gate. Find the entrance and go in [1][2][3][5][10].
- **Forsaken Crypt** can also be chosen as the target of radiant quests [1].
- **Ebony Keep** is tied to the vanilla quest The Ebony Warrior. That quest triggers at player level 80 in vanilla [8]. LoreRim also ships *Mr. Ebony... Get Lost*, which overrides `DLC2EbonyWarriorQuest` and lets you decline the duel or accept it [7][9].
- **Quest dungeons**: Heart Of The Reach starts with Gwilym at the Silver-Blood Inn in Markarth [16][9]. Legends Of Aetherium starts from a Hiring Notice in The Bee and Barb, The Frozen Hearth, Candlehearth Hall or The Bannered Mare, or by going straight to the excavation site [17][9]. Deluge of Deceit starts with Frissa Black-Briar at Elgrim's Elixirs in Riften [18][9]. Miasma starts with Haj-Xul at The Retching Netch in Raven Rock [19][9].
- The LoreRim site does not list any extra start gate for these four quest mods beyond the vanilla-style starts above [9].

## Quests
### The Ebony Warrior (vanilla, Dragonborn DLC)
- **Giver / trigger:** In vanilla, a warrior in ebony armor approaches you in a major city once you reach level 80. He then directs you to his "last vigil", a camp northeast of Fort Greenwall [8].
- **In LoreRim:** *Mr. Ebony... Get Lost* adds dialogue that lets you refuse ("Sorry. You'll have to find some other way to stroke your ego.") or accept ("Very well, I'll be there.") [7]. *Ryn's Snazzy Last Vigill* turns the camp into a real location and makes the fight harder (see Ebony Keep below) [6].

### Heart Of The Reach
- **Giver / trigger:** Gwilym, a priest at the Silver-Blood Inn in Markarth [16].
- **Where:** Ever-Bog, an underground swamp in the Reach [16].
- **Steps:** Travel to Ever-Bog. Collect Imbued Algae, Bog Beacons, Swamp Spider Eggs and Swamp Taproot. Craft the remedy or the poison. Go to the heart. Report back [16].
- **Choices & outcomes:** Heal the heart for Gwilym, or take Vinillian's poisonous root and kill it. Each side gives a different reward. Ring Of The Tree is the reward for siding with Gwilym [16].
- Full write-up: `mod-added/heart-of-the-reach.md`.

### Legends Of Aetherium
- **Where:** the Itharzel excavation site. The Nexus page places it south of Riften and Crystaldrift Cave [17].
- **Steps:** Travel to the excavation site. Optionally find Amandia Screvius. Explore the site and investigate the central tower. Reach the top of the tower and enter the throne room. Defeat the Aetherial Colossus. Find the Aetherial Armoury [17].
- Full write-up: `mod-added/legends-of-aetherium.md`.

### Deluge of Deceit (Sirenroot)
- **Where:** a cave at the bottom of Lake Honrich, north of the Riften docks by Merryfair Farm. It leads into the Ayleid ruins below [18].
- **Steps (abridged):** Accept the Black-Briar diving job. Meet the team. Investigate the cave. Find Varla Stones to flood the Atasel Hall. Find Tilael and the other team members. Escape. Return to Frissa Black-Briar [18].
- Full write-up: `mod-added/sirenroot.md`.

### Miasma
- **Where:** the Abandoned Mine and the Ashbound Prison on Solstheim [19].
- **Steps (abridged):** Follow Haj-Xul. Search the wreckage and the reaver hideout. Find and defeat Zirath Ryon. Find a way out. Defeat Durvith. Return to Haj-Xul [19].
- Full write-up: `mod-added/miasma.md`.

## Locations
- **Forsaken Crypt** (Whiterun Hold): a Nordic ruin west of Whiterun, revealed by an earthquake. It is filled with draugr, skeletons and other undead [1]. The plugin adds a Crypt Key and a "Silver Claw" item [1]. The mod requires Dawnguard [1].
- **Dragon's Teeth Prison** (Falkreath Hold): an old Imperial Legion prison from the Great War. It is just west of Falkreath, at the base of the Dragon's Teeth mountains [2]. It is held by the Frosthide Tribe, a new faction. The plugin defines Frosthide Whelp, Tracker, Brute, Berserker, Shaman, Wolf and Chief, plus a "Frosthide Beast Form" spell [2]. Lore books include Vigilant's Orders, Legate Dratis' Diary, Interrogator's Journal and Elven Contraband [2]. The mod author calls it a small dungeon [2].
- **Snowpoint Beacon / Snowpoint Bastion** (mountains between Dawnstar and Winterhold): Snowpoint Dungeon adds an enterable dungeon, a cell named "Snowpoint Bastion", to the vanilla Snowpoint Beacon. Snowpoint Beacon is on the western edge of the mountains between Dawnstar and Winterhold. The author estimates about half an hour to clear it [3]. LoreRim also ships Snozz's *Snowpoint* exterior overhaul and its Snowpoint Dungeon patch [4]. That overhaul extends the lower level, raises the bandit count, adds patrol routes and places player-levelled loot [4].
- **Ven Fillaan Miin** (under the vanilla Shrine of Azura): Ryn's Azura's Shrine adds an undercroft beneath the statue. The undercroft holds a home for the shrine's priestess. The plugin cell is named "Aranea Lenith's Home". The mod also adds "a new, short but semi difficult dungeon", Ven Fillaan Miin. The plugin also defines a "Key to Ven Fillaan Miin" [5]. See `areas/new-landmarks-and-shrines.md` for the shrine itself.
- **Ebony Keep / Ebony Keep Mine / Ebony Warrior's Camp** (at Last Vigil): Ryn's Snazzy Last Vigill turns the Ebony Warrior's camp into a site with a player home, the Ebony Keep, and an ebony mine [6]. The keep holds the Ebony Warrior's riches, his journal, and letters to him. The plugin has letters from General Tullius, Jarl Ulfric, Kodlak Whitemane and Stentus [6]. It also adds a dremora housekeeper, "Zruill - Dremora Housekeeper", and an Ebony Keep Key [6]. In vanilla the camp is northeast of Fort Greenwall [8]. How the key is obtained was not confirmed from records (unverified).
- **Morihaus' Refuge**: a dungeon among Ancient Imperial ruins. Its boss, the Minotaur Lord, wears the Lord's Mail, so watch for its health-draining effect. The mod removes the Creation Club Redguard-thieves quest for the armor [10][11]. LoreRim ships the "No Bruma Version". Its plugin overrides the CC quests "Gift of Kynareth" and "Seeding Redguard Armor" [10]. A search snippet of the Nexus page says the No-Bruma entrance sits in the mountains near the Cyrodiil border (low confidence) [20]. Full write-up: `mod-added/morihaus-refuge.md`.
- **Redwater Den / Redwater Warehouse** (the Rift, vanilla Redwater Den): rebuilt as a former brewery. The backstory is that Black-Briar competition ruined it, low-lives took it over, and later vampires (Dawnguard) [13]. The plugin adds a new "Redwater Warehouse" interior cell [12]. LoreRim installed the Core file plus its Embers XD, Gore and Lux Orbis patches [12].
- **Abandoned Lodge** (Solstheim): a completely new interior for the lodge used in the vanilla quest A New Source of Stalhrim. There, the missing blacksmith is held by Thalmor soldiers. The interior is built from Cities of the North – Falkreath meshes and is navmeshed [14].
- **Mara's Eye Den** (vanilla Mara's Eye Pond; hold not sourced here): purely natural and visual dressing of the Mara's Eye Pond exterior and the vampire den interior. There are no navmesh or landscape edits [15].
- **Ever-Bog** (the Reach, underground): cells include Ever-Bog Cave, Caverns, Glen, Falls, Chamber, Bowels and Heart Chamber [16]. Enemies include Forsworn Heart Sentinels, a Forsworn Matriarch, Moss Spiders of several sizes including Brood Mothers, Swamp Spriggans and "Hagraven Of The Glen" [16].
- **Itharzel** (the Rift): cells include Itharzel Excavation Mine, Grand Chamber, Manufactorium, Chironasium, Siphonasium, Mage's Quaters (sic), Throne and the Aetherial Armory. Enemies are Aetherial Centurions, Spheres, Spiders and the Aetherial Colossus [17].
- **Honrich ruins** (under Lake Honrich, the Rift): cells include Honrich Lake Cave, Honrich Ruins, Honrich Atasel, Gardens, Aranseli, Buroseli, Gordarseli, Morbalseli, Varlorsel and Welkeseli [18]. Expect long underwater sections. Waterbreathing helps but is not required [18].
- **Abandoned Mine / Ashbound Prison** (Solstheim ashlands): cells are Abandoned Mine, Ashbound Prison, Ashbound Depths and Ashbound Sanctum. Bosses include Zirath Ryon, "Ulzaam, the Warden" and the dragon priest Durvith [19].

## Rewards & notable items
- **Ebony Warrior:** in LoreRim he carries "Ebonarm's Might" and a "Savage Ebony Scimitar". He wears Ebony Warrior's armor pieces, and Vitreous Ebony armor is also added [6]. He has a resistance-ignoring Frost Cloak and Twin Souls, and the plugin adds "Conjure Titanic Frost Atronach" [6]. The author says the craftable set needs the Daedric smithing skill and rates slightly above Daedric armor (author's claim) [6].
- **Morihaus' Refuge:** the Lord's Mail, plus Alessian Battleaxe and Minotaur Battleaxe records [10].
- **Heart Of The Reach:** Antler Bow, Heart Sentinel Bow and Sword, Ring Of The Tree, and the spell tome Conjure Swamp Spriggan [16].
- **Legends Of Aetherium:** levelled Aetherial armor and weapons in four tiers (base, Adorned, Enriched, Ascended), Aetherial Mage Armor, Dwarven Pickaxe, and the Conjure Aetherial Sphere and Conjure Aetherial Spider tomes [17].
- **Deluge of Deceit:** Coronet of the Deep, Siren's Coronet, and the "Blessing of the Spring" spell (outcome-dependent; exact conditions not sourced) [18].
- **Miasma:** seven Tomes of Miasma, Conjure Miasmic Thrall, the Miasmic Breath shout, Haj-Xul's Bow and Axe of the Warden [19].

## LoreRim notes
- **Requiem balance for the Ever-Bog:** LoreRim ships *Requiem - Heart of the Reach*. It removes levelling from all of the mod's content and gives enemies static levels: level 30 Forsworn bosses, a level 55 Spider Queen and a level 35 Hagraven. The installed patch's NPC records confirm static levels: Heart Sentinels 30, the Moss Spider boss 55, the Hagraven 35, and Heart Matriarchs and a lesser Heart Sentinel 20 [21]. The Nexus "10-15+" recommendation therefore does not apply in LoreRim [16][21].
- **Miasma** is recommended at about level 20 or higher, per both the LoreRim site and the author [9][19]. LoreRim installed its USSEP, Timing Is Everything, Locked Chests Have Keys and Striding Silt Striders patches [19].
- **Sirenroot:** the author says enemies scale with level and the story suits levels 3-15. No LoreRim-specific rebalance was found [18].
- **Last Vigil:** LoreRim selected the "The Ebony Keep" and "Ebony Warrior Overhaul" FOMOD options, plus Lux, Lux Orbis, Embers XD and Additional Hearthfire Dolls patches, and the Immersive Sounds Compendium patch for the Ebony Warrior overhaul [6].
- Dragon's Teeth Prison makes no navmesh edits to Tamriel and only minimal world edits [2].
