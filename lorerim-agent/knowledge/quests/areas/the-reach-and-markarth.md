# The Reach and Markarth (LoreRim town expansions, incl. Markarth Side)

LoreRim expands Markarth in two directions: SKY CITY builds new levels above the vanilla city, and Markarth Outskirts rebuilds everything between Salvius Farm and the main gate with new Dwemer-themed businesses [1][2][3][4]. Smaller Reach settlements — Karthwasten, Old Hroldan and Kolskeggr Mine — get Archinatic-style "Great Towns" rebuilds [6][7][8][9][10]. The new town of Markarth Side (a town from TES: Arena) is the only mod here with quests and a buyable home; despite its name it is placed south-east of Dragon Bridge and its plugin assigns it to Hjaalmarch Hold [5].

## Starting in LoreRim
- All of these areas are present from a new game; no LoreRim-specific gate applies [1][3][5][6][7].
- **Markarth Side quests** are both flagged start-game-enabled in the plugin; you pick them up by talking to Varimo at the magic shop or Grit-dar at the inn in Markarth Side [5].
- **Cliffside Manor** is bought, not earned: the plugin prompt reads "Would you like to purchase Cliffside Mannor for 3000 gold?", and furnishing upgrades are offered at "800 Gold Each" [5].

## Quests
### The Lost Family Relic
- **Giver / trigger:** Varimo, at the magic shop (The Boiling Cauldron) in Markarth Side [5].
- **Where:** Markarth Side; the relic is found on a dead draugr [5].
- **Steps:** 1. Find the missing employee and retrieve the relic. 2. Return the amulet to Varimo in Markarth Side [5].
- **Choices & outcomes:** The relic turns out to be an amulet of necromancy; confronting him, Varimo (spelled "Verimo" in later journal text) confesses an interest in necromancy and swears to stop [5].
- **Rewards:** Varimo gives you the amulet and 500 gold for your discretion [5].

### The Royal Relic
- **Giver / trigger:** Grit-dar, a Khajiit at the Markarth Side inn (The Kings Rest) [5].
- **Where:** Morvunskar Crypts, "near Windhelm" — a new dungeon assigned to Eastmarch Hold [5].
- **Steps:** 1. Find the missing royal relic in Morvunskar Crypts. 2. Return to Grit-dar in the Markarth Side inn [5].
- **Rewards:** 500 gold, and Grit-dar offers himself as a recommendation for future work [5].

Other quests in the Reach covered elsewhere: **Heart of the Reach** starts by speaking to Gwilym at the Silver-Blood Inn in Markarth [12] (see `mod-added/heart-of-the-reach.md`).

## Locations

### Markarth — SKY CITY - Markarth Rising
Adds "3 new upper house levels", "a large oil refinery facility", watchtowers and city towers, "functioning Dwemer lifts to travel up and up", a "large mansion at mountain peak", a great statue overlooking the city, and new inhabitants; it edits only the upper layers and uses no scripts [2]. New location records: **Vigdis' House, Sillia's Cookhouse, Borace's House, Hallar's House, Drahf's House** [1].

### Markarth Outskirts — Markarth Outskirts (+ small update)
Overhauls all exterior space between Salvius Farm and Markarth's main door, adding "ten new cells and a dozen new NPCs" [4]. New location records (all under Salvius Farm / Markarth Outskirts) [3]:
- **Markarth East Empire Dormitory** — a new inn for travelers and East Empire Company workers, bedrooms underground [3][4].
- **Markarth East Empire Goods** — a shop run by an Altmer working for the East Empire Company (hinted to be a Thalmor agent) [3][4].
- **Markarth Waste Collector** — Dwemer items dealer "with brand new levelled lists" [3][4].
- **Markarth Mill Maintenance** — a man trying to keep the Dwemer mill machinery from exploding [3][4].
- **Markarth Mushroom Farm** and **Hilarius Farmhouse** — a new house and mushroom farm helping the Salvius family [3][4].
- **Markarth Old Temple** — a Dwemer ruin turned Nordic temple whose worshippers revere "some kind of strange dwemer lighting" [3][4].
- **Markarth Old Dam** — editor ID `MarkarthEntranceThievesGuildLocation`; "a few thieves seem to live down there, maybe they could help any fellow thief once the guild a bit more renowned"; tunnels connect to Markarth locations [3][4].
- **Markarth Old Observatory** — a Dwemer tower observatory with a skilled conjurer, up many stairs [3][4].

### Markarth Side (Arena - Markarth Side Town)
"Markarth Side, featured in TES Arena. Located south east of Dragon Bridge." [5] The plugin parents it to Hjaalmarch Hold [5]. Features: 8 shops, barracks and guards, custom NPCs, an inn with a bard, and a player home furnished "through a menu system" with all crafting stations, a garden, and room for two children, a spouse and a follower [5]. Location records [5]:
- **The Kings Rest** — inn (Grit-dar).
- **The Boiling Cauldron** — magic shop (Varimo).
- **The Silverspoon Trading Company** — general store.
- **Iron Maidens** — blacksmith.
- **Healers and Dealers** — alchemy shop.
- **The Splended Spool** — clothing shop.
- **Markarth Side Barracks**.
- **Cliffside Manor** — player home, 3000 gold [5].
- **Morvunskar Crypts** — quest dungeon in Eastmarch [5].

### Karthwasten — The Great Town of Karthwasten
A mining town described as "the only major settlement left in the Reach still owned by a Reachman"; rebuilt with a new Breton-inspired Reach building set, 5 new NPCs, "a new blacksmith, apothecary, farms and an inn" [7]. New location records: **Meredith's House, Sleepy Hags Inn, Maddock's House, Grokmar's House** [6].

### Old Hroldan — The Great Village of Old Hroldan
"A small settlement in the Reach" where Tiber Septim shouted down the walls; the inn gets a unique exterior, two farmhouses are added, the nearby Soljund's Sinkhole farmhouse is replaced, and a statue commemorates Talos's victory [9]. LoreRim uses Rob's Bug Fixes' cleaned plugin for TGC Old Hroldan 1.03 [8]. New location records: two houses both named **Doughlas House** (the second's editor ID is `OldHroldanTobiasHouse…`, likely a naming slip) and a **Miner's House** at Soljund's Sinkhole [8].

### Kolskeggr Mine
Kolskeggr Mine TGC Addon rebuilds the mine's single house in the Great Towns Reach style [10]. Environs - Kolskeggr shows signs of the Forsworn raid first; after you finish Pavo Attius's vanilla quest at Left Hand Mine, the site evolves — "a rebuilt house, two new miners, an extra guard" [10].

## Rewards & notable items
- **Cliffside Manor** (Markarth Side) — buy for 3000 gold; upgrades 800 gold each [5].
- 500 gold each from The Lost Family Relic and The Royal Relic; the necromancy amulet from The Lost Family Relic [5].
- Markarth Waste Collector sells Dwemer items from new levelled lists [4].

## LoreRim notes
- Contradiction (location): Markarth Side's page says it is south-east of Dragon Bridge, while its name suggests Markarth; the plugin parents it to **Hjaalmarch Hold** [5]. Treat it as a Haafingar/Hjaalmarch-border town, not part of Markarth city.
- Contradiction (quest-giver spelling): objective text says "Varimo", later journal text says "Verimo" [5].
- LoreRim also enables "Arena - Markarth Side Lux Patch - ESLIFIED - UNDELETED", "Markarth Outskirts - SKY CITY patch", "Markarth Outskirts Natural Waterfalls Patch", the Karthwasten and Old Hroldan patch collections, Kolskeggr's Environs patches, and "BETA Great Village of Old Hroldan - Meridia's Order Patch" [13]. What the Meridia's Order patch changes was not checked (see `mod-added/meridias-order.md`).
- The "small update" and the base "Markarth Outskirts" folder both ship `Lux Via - Markarth Entrance and Farm Overhaul.esp`; both are enabled, and the small update's copy is the one LoreRim's import records [3][13][15].
- Kolskeggr Mine, Karthwasten, Old Hroldan and Markarth are Reach Hold locations in the base game [14].
