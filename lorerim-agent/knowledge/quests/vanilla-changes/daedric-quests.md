# Daedric Quests (vanilla) in LoreRim

Skyrim's 16 Daedric quests each end in a Daedric artifact (Azura's Star, Ebony Mail, Volendrung, Ebony Blade, Dawnbreaker, Mace of Molag Bal, Ring of Namira, Spellbreaker, Wabbajack, and so on) [1]. Every one of them ships in LoreRim. LoreRim's changes come in three kinds: later, rumor-based starts for several quests; "good guy" or alternate routes, so most quests can be finished without serving the Prince; and Wintersun religion integration, where your choices change your favor with the Princes [4][5][7][12][17]. The LoreRim site describes the goal as fixing quests that "don't leave much room for player choice" [4].

## Starting in LoreRim

| Quest (EditorID) | Vanilla gate [1] | LoreRim gate |
|---|---|---|
| The House of Horrors (DA10) | level 1 | **Level 20**, then ask Kleppr or Frabbi (Silver-Blood Inn, Markarth) "Anything noteworthy happening?". The helper objective is "Speak to the witchhunter about the abandoned house" (Tyranus) [5][6] |
| The Taste of Death (DA11) | level 1 | **Level 20**, then ask the Silverblood Inn innkeeper for rumors until you hear about the Hall of the Dead and Brother Verulus. Before that, the Understone Keep scene does not play, and you can visit the Hall of the Dead freely [5][6] |
| The Mind of Madness (DA15) | level 1 | **Level 20**, then ask the Winking Skeever innkeeper (Solitude) for rumors. The rumor objective reads "Investigate the man wandering the streets near the Bards College", and Dervenin then force-greets you [5][6] |
| The Cursed Tribe (DA06) | level 9 | Be an **Orc or Blood-Kin** and ask the chief of another Orc stronghold for rumors. You get a "Letter from Largashbur" (objective "Read the letter from Largashbur"). The mod page says "at least level 10" [9] |
| Boethiah's Calling (DA02) | level 30 | **Conflict:** 20 or 30 — see below [5][7] |
| A Night To Remember (DA14) | level 14 | **Conflict:** 20 or 14 [5][7] |
| The Break of Dawn (DA09) | level 12 | **Conflict:** 20 or 12; LoreRim also sets "no start while a vampire" in the plugin [5][7] |
| The Whispering Door (DA08) | level 20 + Dragon Rising | Unchanged (20). The pointer objective now reads "Ask Balgruuf about his children" [2][7][11] |
| The Only Cure (DA13), Discerning the Transmundane (DA04), Pieces of the Past (DA07) | 12 / 15 / 20 | Unchanged (Timing is Everything defaults) [7] |
| The Black Star, A Daedra's Best Friend, Ill Met By Moonlight, Waking Nightmare | 1 / 10 / 1 / 1 | No LoreRim level change found. LoreRim ships only the House of Horrors, Mind of Madness and Taste of Death modules of Delayed Quest Starts, so that mod's Black Star, Waking Nightmare and A Daedra's Best Friend delays are **not** present [6][16] |

**About the conflicting gates.** LoreRim's own `LoreRim - Global Modifiers.esp` loads late and sets `DA02MinLevel`, `DA14MinLevel_KRY` and `DA09MinLevel` to 20, and `MeridiaNoVampire_KRY` to 1 [5]. However, the enabled *Timing is Everything SE - Settings Loader* runs a script on every game load that rewrites these same globals from its `settings.ini`. That file still holds the vanilla values: Boethiah's Calling 30, A Night to Remember 14, The Break of Dawn 12, and "no vampires" off [7]. No LoreRim override of that ini was found [7]. The effective gate in a running game is therefore probably the MCM value (inferred from the scripts; not tested in game). To see the live number, open MCM → Timing is Everything → Daedric Quests, where you can also change it [7]. The three Delayed Quest Starts levels have no MCM, so their value of 20 stands [5][6].

**The Choice is Yours** stops greetings and rumors from adding quests automatically; a quest is logged only once you agree to it [8]. It edits the Black Star intro (objective "Visit the Shrine of Azura to the south of Winterhold") and Boethiah's Calling [8].

## Quests

### The Black Star (DA01)
- **Vanilla:** Aranea Ienith at the Shrine of Azura sends you to recover Azura's Star [1].
- **In LoreRim:** You choose to pursue the Shrine of Azura lead through The Choice is Yours (DA01Intro) [8]. There is no level delay [6]. The other edits are fixes and Wintersun favor hooks only [16].

### Boethiah's Calling (DA02)
- **Vanilla:** From level 30, read *Boethiah's Proving* or visit the Sacellum of Boethiah. You sacrifice a follower at the pillar, kill the cultists, then kill the Champion at Knifepoint Ridge and equip the **Ebony Mail** [3].
- **In LoreRim, refusal route (The Choice is Yours):** If you refuse to sacrifice a follower, Boethiah curses you. Objective "Visit a Shrine of Stendarr" leads to the **Gauntlets of the Crusader** as Stendarr's reward. Alternatively, "Visit the Altar of Molag Bal" in Markarth leads to the **Hands of Coldharbour** for defeating Boethiah's followers [8]. LoreRim's `Wintersun - Reqtificated.esp` is the final DA02 record, and it keeps these stages [17].
- **In LoreRim, new quest "The Man in Black" (DA02AltQuest, Boethiah's Calling - Alternate Questline):** Talk to the Dunmer Rudin Filaro at the Braidwood Inn in Kynesgrove [9]. The objectives run: "Escort Rudin to the Shrine." → "Investigate the nearby area." → "Search for clues on Rudin's whereabouts." → "Find Rudin Filaro." → "Kill Rudin Filaro." [9]
  - Rudin paralyzes you with a rune and sacrifices you to Boethiah. You survive, track him to Knifepoint Ridge where he has killed the old Champion, and kill him for the Ebony Mail. No friend has to die [9].
  - It starts at the same level as Boethiah's Calling [9].
  - If you carry the Ebony Mail with fewer than 5 murders, a Dunmer named **Mia** ambushes you in the wilderness 2–3 weeks later, whichever route you took [9]. A Great Village of Kynesgrove patch is installed [9].

### A Daedra's Best Friend (DA03)
- **Vanilla:** Haemar's Shame, from level 10; the reward is the Rueful Axe or the Masque of Clavicus Vile [1].
- **In LoreRim:** Only Wintersun hooks and fixes are present. No delay is installed [6][16].

### Discerning the Transmundane (DA04)
- **Vanilla:** Septimus Signus, level 15; reward Oghma Infinium [1].
- **In LoreRim:** Gate unchanged at 15 [7]. Only fixes and Wintersun hooks [16].

### Ill Met By Moonlight (DA05)
- **Vanilla:** Falkreath, level 1; reward Savior's Hide and/or Ring of Hircine [1].
- **In LoreRim:** Only fixes and Wintersun hooks [16]. Hircine is revoiced (loose audio only) [18].

### The Cursed Tribe (DA06)
- **Vanilla:** Largashbur, level 9; reward Volendrung [1].
- **In LoreRim (The Cursed Tribe - Quest Expansion, added in LoreRim 5.0)** [4][9]:
  - Restores cut content: Yamarz was cursed by the ghost of Shagrol, the chief he killed [9].
  - **Normal route:** "Return to Atub" → "Find the Shrine of Malacath" (a new shrine northwest of Largashbur, near Avanchnzel) → "Inspect the Skeleton" (it carries the Dead Orc's Note) → Troll Fat and Daedra heart → ritual → escort Yamarz → "Defeat Garok Longstride" (sic) → place Shagrol's Hammer → **Volendrung** [9].
  - **Resto route:** If you know Bane of the Undead or carry its scroll, offer to help. Then "Cast Bane of the Undead in Largashbur" and defeat 10 ghosts, Shagrol among them. Yamarz lives, you get no Volendrung, and Atub gives you the **Necklace of the Wise Woman** ("Wisdom of the Wise Woman" enchantment). With Wintersun, this route lowers your favor with Malacath [9].
  - Giant's Grove is renamed **Sanctum of Malacath** [9].
  - LoreRim's `The Cursed Tribe - LoreRim Patch.esp` adds Requiem stats for the new items and NPCs and is the final DA06 record, with all of the new objectives [9][17].

### Pieces of the Past (DA07)
- **Vanilla:** Silus Vesuius, level 20; reward Mehrunes' Razor or gold [1].
- **In LoreRim (Mehrunes Dagon's Shrine Unlocked)** [4][10]:
  - The shrine door now has a Master lock you can pick. Silus carries a spare key that can be pickpocketed. His new journal, found in his house, lets you confront him or pass a speech check for the key [10].
  - **New quest "Reforging the Past"** (SilusJournal_ReforgeRazor.esp): "Reforge the Razor at any forge." → "(Optional) Return the Razor to Silus." You can give it to Silus for gold or keep it, in which case Silus may try to take it back [10]. Reforging needs all the fragments, three Daedra Hearts, and the Daedric and Arcane Blacksmith perks, per the mod page. Reforging ends or fails Pieces of the Past automatically [10]. The plugins for Ryn's Mehrunes Dagon's Shrine and the Cities of the North (COTN) Dawnstar patch are installed [10].

### The Whispering Door (DA08)
- **Vanilla:** Level 20 after Dragon Rising, started from Hulda's rumor. You get the key and give Mephala the **Ebony Blade** [2].
- **In LoreRim (The Whispering Door - Quest Expansion):** "Speak to Nelkir OR warn the Jarl". On the good path you "Report to Jarl Balgruuf" and then "Speak to a Vigilant of Stendarr", which disposes of the blade. The evil path is "kill allies with the Ebony Blade" [11][19].
  - The Wintersun patch lets you become a follower of Mephala on either path and applies favor changes [11].
  - Mephala's Curse's `Whispering_Door_Expansion_Addon.esp` is installed. With it, Mephala disapproves of handing the blade to the Vigilant and retaliates directly [12].
  - Mephala is revoiced [12].
- **New quest "The Fate of the Ebony Blade"** (DA08MephalaHunt, Mephala's Curse): This starts once you hold the blade. Objective: "Decide the fate of the Ebony Blade."
  - Journal: to destroy it you must find the **Aetherium Forge**, a fire hotter than the Skyforge.
  - Until the blade is appeased (restored by killing friends) or destroyed, **Spider Daedra** and Morag Tong/Mephala Cultist ambushes hit you every 1–2 weeks. Storing the blade in a chest pauses them [12].
  - A Spell Tome: Conjure Spider Daedra sits near the blade [12].

### The Break of Dawn (DA09)
- **Vanilla:** Statue to Meridia, level 12; reward Dawnbreaker [1].
- **In LoreRim:** The level gate is in conflict (see above) [5][7]. Requiem also edits the quest record; the details are unverified [16]. Completing it unlocks the mod questline Meridia's Order [14].

### The House of Horrors (DA10)
- **Vanilla:** Abandoned House, Markarth; reward Mace of Molag Bal [1].
- **In LoreRim:** Delayed to level 20 behind the inn rumor [5][6]. House of Horrors - Quest Expansion adds a Tyranus route: "Follow Tyranus" → "Hit the altar to destroy it" → "Escape the basement" → "Talk to Tyranus" [13].
  - Journal: "I helped Tyranus destroy Molag Bal's altar. The house is now safe." [13]
  - The reward is **Tyranus as a voiced follower** instead of the mace. He refuses if he suspects you are a vampire or werewolf [13].
  - Killing Tyranus before the ritual returns you to the vanilla mace path [13].

### The Taste of Death (DA11)
- **Vanilla:** Hall of the Dead, Markarth; reward Ring of Namira or gold [1].
- **In LoreRim:** Delayed to level 20 [5][6].
- **New quest "A Bitter Aftertaste"** (The Taste of Death - Quest Addon): Kill Eola and read **Eola's Journal**. Three dialogue lines provoke her: "Your tricks won't work on me, monster.", "I didn't come to help…", "Helping you was a mistake…" [15].
  - Then "Search for evidence in Reachcliff Cave" → (optional) Verulus → "Speak to the Jarl of Markarth" → "Confront Hogni Red-Arm" / "Confront Lisbet" / "Confront Banning" → Jarl's reward [15].
  - With the Jarl's permission you can arrest the cannibals, or blackmail them for gold. You find the **Ring of Namira** without becoming a cannibal [15].
  - The cursed-ring module is installed (`TasteOfDeath_Addon_RingCurse_SMI.esp`): while you carry the ring, only cannibalism reduces hunger [15].

### The Only Cure (DA13)
- **Vanilla:** Kesh at Peryite's shrine, level 12; reward Spellbreaker [1].
- **In LoreRim (The Only Cure - Quest Expansion):**
  - **Kesh's Journal** lets you go to Bthardamz without summoning Peryite [14].
  - New objectives: "Kill or Talk to Orchendor", "Speak with Peryite or Destroy his Altar" [14].
  - Journal outcomes include refusing Peryite outright, and "I killed Orchendor, stopped the plague from spreading and destroyed Peryite's altar." [14]
  - Destroying the altar after accepting Spellbreaker curses the shield with poison [14].
  - Peryite is revoiced [14].

### A Night To Remember (DA14)
- **Vanilla:** Sam Guevenne's drinking contest, level 14; reward Sanguine Rose [1].
- **In LoreRim:** The level gate is in conflict (20 vs 14) [5][7]. No quest content change was found [16].

### The Mind of Madness (DA15)
- **Vanilla:** Dervenin in Solitude, level 1; reward Wabbajack [1].
- **In LoreRim:** Delayed to level 20 via the Winking Skeever rumor [5][6]. Completing it is required to start Saints & Seducers Extended Cut (level 20+) [14].

### Waking Nightmare (DA16)
- **Vanilla:** Erandur in Dawnstar; reward Skull of Corruption or a follower [1].
- **In LoreRim:** No delay [6]. A dream-charge widget for the Skull of Corruption is added [18].

## Locations
- **Sanctum of Malacath** — renamed Giant's Grove at the end of Fallowstone Cave. A new Malacath shrine also sits northwest of Largashbur [9].
- **Kynesgrove / Braidwood Inn** — where you meet Rudin Filaro for The Man in Black [9].

## Rewards & notable items
Gauntlets of the Crusader, Hands of Coldharbour (Boethiah alternatives) [8]; Necklace of the Wise Woman (Cursed Tribe alternative) [9]; Tyranus follower (House of Horrors alternative) [13]; Spell Tome: Conjure Spider Daedra [12]; a self-reforged Mehrunes' Razor [10].

## LoreRim notes
- **Fixes and integration only (not design changes):** USSEP and the cleaned official masters fix most DA quests. Wintersun - Faiths of Skyrim overrides all 16 to add worship and favor hooks. LoreRim's `Wintersun - Reqtificated.esp` is the final record for DA02, DA06, DA10, DA13 and DA16 and keeps the expansions' stages [16][17]. Requiem also edits DA03Start, DA09 and WEJS14 (the Afflicted refugee hook for The Only Cure) [16].
- **Mephala's Curse caveats (inferred from the profile):** Aetherium Forge Destroys Items, which the Mephala's Curse author names as the way to destroy the blade, is **not** in the Default profile. Without it, the author says the quest "simply ends when you get to the Aetherium Forge" [12]. The Stress and Fear sanity mod that drives the blade's madness curse is also absent [12][17].
- The Taste of Death add-on's optional undead boss was not found among the shipped plugins (unverified) [15].
- These candidate mods are **not Daedric quests**: The Heart of Dibella - Quest Expansion and Spare Anise (Anise near Riverwood) are side quests, and Toying With The Dead - minor Quest Expansion is a Thieves Guild misc quest. See `vanilla-changes/side-quests-and-misc.md` [16].
