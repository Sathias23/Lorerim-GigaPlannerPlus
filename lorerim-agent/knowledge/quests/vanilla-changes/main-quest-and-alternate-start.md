# Main Quest and Alternate Start (Alternate Perspective)

The vanilla main quest runs from **Unbound**, the escape from Helgen when Alduin attacks, to **Dragonslayer**, Alduin's defeat in Sovngarde. Along the way it passes through the Greybeards, the Blades, the Thalmor Embassy, the Elder Scroll and Skuldafn [1][2]. LoreRim replaces the opening with **Alternate Perspective (AP)**, a rewritten intro. You pick a start scenario, and Helgen stays a functioning town until you choose to trigger the intro [10][8]. LoreRim says it has expanded the main quest "to provide more choices" and lengthened it "with the added Dragon Cult" [8].

## Starting in LoreRim

- **Character creation room.** A new game starts in an isolated cell styled after a room in Helgen's inn, "The Resting Pilgrim". The room holds items for setting up your character. Talk to the little dragon on a lantern to choose a start, then go through the door next to him [10]. If you ignore the dragon (or use `coc` to leave), you get the "Default" start [10]. In the installed translation, the "None" option reads "Begin your journey in the Resting Pilgrim (Helgen's Inn)" [10].
- **Starting the main quest later.** Go to Helgen's inn and talk to the innkeeper. One extra option reads "I'd like to rent a room. (Start Intro (10 gold))". The next time you sleep in that inn, the execution scene begins [10]. The LoreRim site says the same: rent a room at the Helgen inn, and on waking "a familiar scene" plays from "an alternate perspective" [8]. The innkeeper is named **Matlara** on the Gate to Sovngarde wiki, a different modlist that uses the same Voiced Addon (medium confidence) [17]. The Voiced Addon rewrites some AP dialogue to fit spliced vanilla lines, so in-game wording can differ slightly from the AP page [11].
- **Starting it right away.** The **Dragonborn** category in the start menu begins the main quest immediately. LoreRim calls this "not recommended". With any other choice, "Helgen will not be destroyed" [8]. The installed Dragonborn sub-options are **Random**, **Vanilla Start**, **Helgen Keep (Start)**, **Helgen Keep (End)** and **High Hrothgar** [10].
- **Skipping the intro.** The LoreRim FAQ says you can go behind the inn at Helgen and talk to a dragon there, which "let's you skip through the intro" [9].
- **Dragons don't spawn early.** The FAQ says to continue the main quest "as you normally would to get dragons to start spawning, by going through the Bleak Falls Barrow quest, etc." [9].
- **Helgen before the attack.** Until you start the main quest, Helgen is a rebuilt, living town with its own inn, a shopkeeper and new NPCs [10]. The Voiced Addon page mentions "a couple of radiant quests" there [11]. The install adds the locations **Helgen** (interiors) and **The Resting Pilgrim** [10].

### Start scenarios available in LoreRim (installed menu JSON)
- **Alternate Perspective (base):**
  - **Regular (Inn):** 17 inns across Skyrim and Solstheim.
  - **From Overseas:** East Empire Trading Company, Windhelm Docks, Dawnstar's Shore or Raven Rock Docks.
  - **Wanted Criminal:** Whiterun, the Rift, the Pale or Winterhold.
  - **Left for Dead:** the Rift, the Reach or Winterhold.
  - **Shipwrecked.**
  - **My own Home:** Whiterun, Solitude, Riften or Markarth.
  - **Vampire:** Pinemoon Cave, Bloodlet Throne, Movarth's Lair or Haemar's Shame.
  - **Werewolf.**
  - **Forsworn:** Druadach Redoubt.
  - **Guilds:** College of Winterhold, Thieves Guild, Dark Brotherhood or Companions.
  - **Dragonborn:** see above. [10]
- **New Beginnings:** Addict, Dwemer Ruin, Alchemist, At Angi's Camp, Beggar, Jailed, Mercenary, Merchant, Pilgrim (Shrine), Pilgrim (Temple), Thalmor Prisoner, Vampire, Warlock and Werewolf [12]. Each start has a short journal quest, for example "Locked Up" (Serve your time, or break out) or "I Didn't Expect a Kind of Thalmor Inquisition" (Escape) [12]. The plugin still contains the "Attacked by a Dragon" start, quest "The Bodies of Men on His Wings", but the installed menu JSON does not list it. It is probably not selectable (medium confidence) [12].
- **Adventurer's Start:** Roadside Ambush, Fell in a Spike Pit, Bounty Hunter, Prisoner in a Bandit Fort, Orc Stronghold (you start as Blood-Kin), Saved by Vigilants and Out in the Wilderness [13]. Its journal quests include "Ambushed", "Bounty Hunter" and "Prisoner" [13]. Its Bruma variants need Beyond Skyrim: Bruma, which LoreRim does not ship [13].

## Quests

### Unbound (MQ101)
- **Vanilla:** You are a prisoner taken to Helgen for execution. Alduin attacks, and you escape through Helgen Keep with Ralof or Hadvar [2].
- **In LoreRim:** Alternate Perspective rewrites the intro. It overrides `MQ101` and `MQ101DragonAttack`, and it starts the intro from the Resting Pilgrim instead of the execution cart (see above) [10][18]. The AP author says the execution may not happen on 17th of Last Seed, 4E 201, and that "the Dragonborn wont be almost executed" [10]. The Voiced Addon also overrides `MQ101`. It adds spliced voice lines, music and lighting in the start cell, an ear-ringing effect when Alduin knocks you out, and other small touches [11][18]. The TrueHUD fix hides the HUD during AP intro cutscenes and shows it again afterwards [14]. The NPC replacer changes the look of Helgen's citizens and AP NPCs. Ulfric, Hadvar, Ralof and Tullius are unchanged [15].

### Before the Storm (MQ102)
- **Vanilla:** You tell Whiterun's Jarl about the dragon attack at Helgen [1].
- **In LoreRim:** Delay Bleak Falls Barrow - Voiced edits Balgruuf's reward dialogue (`MQ102BalgruufReward`). After his reward, he says Farengar needs time: "If you want to help, give him time. Come back in a few nights and go talk to him. I'll reward you." [16]. Otherwise only bug fixes apply (USSEP) [18].

### Bleak Falls Barrow (MQ103)
- **Vanilla:** Balgruuf sends you straight to Farengar to retrieve the Dragonstone from Bleak Falls Barrow. Rewards: leveled armor, permission to buy Breezehome, and a word of Unrelenting Force [3].
- **In LoreRim:** Delay Bleak Falls Barrow - Voiced overrides `MQ103` [18]. The quest no longer follows straight from Before the Storm. You come back later and ask Farengar the new topic "Have you learned anything about the Dragons? Do you need any help?" [16]. The plugin's dialogue conditions only check quest stages, so how the "few nights" delay is enforced was not determined [16]. The Gate to Sovngarde wiki, a different list, says its version holds the quest back "until level 14". That figure is **unverified for LoreRim** [17].

### Dragon Rising (MQ104)
- **Vanilla:** You kill Mirmulnir at the Western Watchtower and hear the Greybeards' call. Rewards: Thaneship of Whiterun, the housecarl Lydia and the **Axe of Whiterun** [4].
- **In LoreRim:**
  - Unique Thane Weapons overrides `MQ104` [18]. Its plugin carries the **Blade of Whiterun** (upgradable at the Skyforge to the **Gilded Blade of Whiterun**). The mod replaces the generic thane weapon with that unique blade [19].
  - Requiem also overrides `MQ104` [18]. An old Requiem changelog entry (1.6 or earlier) says Balgruuf hands you potions against the first dragon. Whether that is still the current edit was not verified (low confidence) [20].

### Diplomatic Immunity (MQ201)
- **Vanilla:** Delphine gets you invited to Elenwen's reception at the Thalmor Embassy. Malborn smuggles in your gear, you cause a distraction, you find the Esbern dossier, and you escape through Reeking Cave [5].
- **In LoreRim:** Storm the Thalmor Embassy overrides `MQ201` [18]. You can skip the party and go straight to the Embassy by force [8][21]. The original party route still works [21]. Elenwen is disabled on the storm route because the main quest needs her later [21]. A character who is not the Dragonborn can enter the Embassy *before* Diplomatic Immunity and kill everyone, including Elenwen, but this **breaks the main quest** [21]. After escaping, you can skip Delphine and go straight to Riften, as in vanilla [21]. Details: [storm-the-thalmor-embassy.md](../mod-added/storm-the-thalmor-embassy.md).

### A Cornered Rat (MQ202)
- **Vanilla:** Depending on your Thieves Guild standing, the journal points you to Brynjolf or tells you to search the Ratway for Esbern [6].
- **In LoreRim:** Requiem overrides `MQ202` [18]. Since Requiem 2.0.0, the quest points you "towards the local inkeeper instead of the Thieves Guild ... to prevent characters from being forced into the Thieves Guild" [20].

### The Throat of the World (MQ204) and Elder Knowledge (MQ205)
- **Vanilla:** Paarthurnax gives you a lesson, then you recover an Elder Scroll [1].
- **In LoreRim:**
  - Only bug fixes apply (USSEP) [18].
  - **Defeat the Dragon Cult** adds a new quest, **Destroy the Dragon Cult** [22][8]. After Paarthurnax tells you to find an Elder Scroll, Esbern sends you a note. The objectives are to defeat Hevnoraak, Krosis, Morokei, Nahkriin, Otar the Mad, Rahgot, Vokun and Volsung, and to "Investigate Labyrinthian" [22]. The mod itself is pure roleplay, and you can ignore it [22]. See [destroy-the-dragon-cult.md](../mod-added/destroy-the-dragon-cult.md).
  - **Cult of the World Eater** gives this hunt real stakes. Alduin gains +3500 health, 6 health per second of regeneration, and magic resistance raised to 90%. Each named Dragon Priest you kill removes part of that [23]:

    | Dragon Priest (location) | Buff removed |
    |---|---|
    | Morokei (Labyrinthian) | 20% magic resistance |
    | Vokun (High Gate Ruins) | 20% magic resistance |
    | Rahgot (Forelhost) | 1500 health |
    | Volsung (Volskygge) | 1500 health |
    | Nahkriin (Skuldafn) | 500 health |
    | Krosis (Shearpoint) | 2 health/s regeneration |
    | Hevnoraak (Valthume) | 2 health/s regeneration |
    | Otar the Mad (Ragnvald) | 2 health/s regeneration |

    Nahkriin is in Skuldafn, so you can only remove his buff after the first Alduin fight [23]. A book next to Dragonbane in Sky Haven Temple hints at the mechanic [23].

### Paarthurnax (MQPaarthurnax)
- **Vanilla:** After Alduin's Bane, the Blades demand that you kill Paarthurnax. If you refuse, they stop helping you. If you kill him, the Greybeards turn hostile in dialogue and stop their services [7].
- **In LoreRim:** Paarthurnax - Quest Expansion overrides `MQPaarthurnax` [18]. It adds about 60 new voiced lines built from vanilla audio, with more context for both sides [24].
  - **Spare him:** You make Delphine recite the Blades' oath and accept your decision, then you have to convince Esbern. Shouting may be needed, or he may convince you. Sparing him keeps both the Blades and the Greybeards available [24].
  - **Kill him:** It becomes a boss fight against Paarthurnax and two other dragons, with combat dialogue [24].
  - The LoreRim site describes this as an option "where you no longer need to kill Paarthurnax" [8].
  - The Enigma voice patch (Sound files only) and the Gore follower patch also ship for this quest [25].

### The Fallen (MQ301), The World-Eater's Eyrie (MQ303), Sovngarde (MQ304)
- **Vanilla:** You trap Odahviing, fight through Skuldafn, and defeat Alduin in Sovngarde [1].
- **In LoreRim:** These records only get bug fixes and cleaning [18]. The combat changes come from other mods:
  - **Cult of the World Eater** buffs Alduin (see Elder Knowledge above) [23].
  - **Requiem 2.0.1** changelog: Alduin has "no magic resistance and no more fire dragon traits, but 33% resistance against all elements". He also no longer regenerates health specially in Sovngarde while not under Dragonrend [20]. See Contradictions in LoreRim notes.

### Blades side quests (Sky Haven Temple)
- **Vanilla:** These are the Blades radiant and reward quests: Rebuilding the Blades, Dragon Hunting, Dragonslayer's Blessing and Dragon Research [18].
- **In LoreRim:**
  - Paarthurnax - Quest Expansion and Dragon Hunting override them [18].
  - After **Rebuilding the Blades**, Esbern gives the *Blessing of the Blades*: weapon enchantments are 25% stronger against dragons for 8 hours [26].
  - **Dragon Hunting** can be repeated every 24 in-game hours [26].
  - **Dragon Research** now needs Dragon Blood, Dragon Bile and Dragon Rheum to make *Dragon Infusion*, which reduces damage from dragons by 10% [26].
  - Dragons drop new alchemy ingredients. The body-part ingredients can only be looted while you carry **Kahvozein's Fang** [26].
  - Farengar buys dragon parts through the "Buy Dragon Parts" quest, and Quantity Trade adds a slider to that trade [26][27].
  - **A Forgotten Blade** (Redeeming Fultheim) becomes available after Alduin's Wall [28]. See [redeeming-fultheim.md](../mod-added/redeeming-fultheim.md).

## Locations
- **Helgen** — AP rebuilds it as a living town with new paths and new NPCs in the empty houses. It stays that way until the intro destroys it [10].
- **The Resting Pilgrim** — Helgen's inn, where you start the main quest [10][8].

## Rewards & notable items
- **Blade of Whiterun** / **Gilded Blade of Whiterun** replaces the vanilla Axe of Whiterun as the Dragon Rising thane weapon [19][4].
- **Blessing of the Blades** and **Dragon Infusion** are the reworked Blades rewards [26].

## LoreRim notes
- **Bug fixes only:** USSEP, Official Master Files - Cleaned Plugins and LoreRim's output plugins override MQ101 to MQ304. These are fixes, not design changes [18].
- **Vittoria's wedding:** Vittorias Alternate Wedding overrides the `MQ201Party` helper quest [18].
- **Requiem Thu'um perks:** Since Requiem 2.0.0, "The Way of the Voice perks no longer require meditating on word of power but become available as you progress the main quest" [20].
- **Timing is Everything (TIE):** TIE overrides `WE34` "Thalmor vs Player"; Requiem also overrides that record, and what its edit changes was not checked [18]. Its Settings Loader defaults are:
  - Thalmor squad minimum level 8, with the quest requirement off.
  - Dragon attacks at least 3 days apart, at 100% chance.
  
  The "LoreRim - MCM and INI Settings" mod contains no TIE override, so these defaults apply (medium confidence) [29].
- **Optional AP files:**
  - "No Markers" stops the start room from showing its quest objective [10].
  - "Messenger" ships the "Messenger Spawn" file. Its script only disables a shrine exterior and the start cell when you enter its trigger, and its effect on gameplay was not documented [10].
- **Delayed Quest Starts mods in this unit:** The CC Fishing, House of Horrors, Mind of Madness, Taste of Death and Forsworn Conspiracy versions do **not** touch the main quest. For example, The Taste of Death needs level 40 and House of Horrors level 35 [30]. See [daedric-quests.md](daedric-quests.md), [side-quests-and-misc.md](side-quests-and-misc.md) and [creation-club.md](creation-club.md).
- **Dragonborn DLC:** Its start is tied to The Way of the Voice and Miraak's cultists. Its Dragon Priest buffs (Cult of the True Dragonborn) are covered in [dragonborn.md](dragonborn.md) [8].
- **Contradictions:**
  - **Alduin's magic resistance:** Cult of the World Eater assumes Alduin's base is 50% [23], but Requiem's changelog says Alduin has none [20]. His actual total in LoreRim was not verified.
  - **Bleak Falls Barrow delay:** The Gate to Sovngarde wiki gives level 14 [17], while the installed plugin only shows a "few nights" line [16].
