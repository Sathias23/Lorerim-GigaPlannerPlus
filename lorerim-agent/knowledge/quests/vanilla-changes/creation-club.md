# Creation Club Quests in LoreRim

LoreRim requires the full Anniversary Edition, so all Creation Club (CC) content is present, but the list ships a separator of CC overhaul mods that remove, relocate, delay or gate many CC quests [1][3]. In the vanilla game most CC content starts with a courier letter or a note, or as soon as you get close; LoreRim's site sums up the vanilla style as "here, its all in this note" [1]. This file covers each CC quest LoreRim changes and the mod that changes it. Bigger replacements have their own files (see Related).

## Starting in LoreRim
- **Removed or folded into other content (LoreRim site):** the Alternative Armors quests are disabled and their armors are given to selected NPCs. Umbra comes from another quest mod. *The Arms of Chaos* is removed. Sunder and Wraithguard come from *The Tools of Kagrenac*. *Stendarr's Hammer*'s quest is removed and the hammer is on Vigilant Tyranus. Chrysamere's quest is removed and the weapon is on the Ustengraav boss, now a ghost called The Lost Paladin. The *Nordic Jewelry* quest is disabled and the jewelry is craftable with the Craftsmanship perk. Ma'dran wears the Ring of Masser [1]. The install shows `Requiem - Creation Club.esp` overriding the Alternative Armors start quests (`ccBGSSSE050`–`064_MiscQuest`) [4]. The Requiem CC patch author says that plugin "disabled a number of CC Quests", and LoreRim does not ship the optional "CC Quests Re-Enabled" files [20][3]. Which plugin removes Chrysamere, Arms of Chaos and Nordic Jewelry was not found in the quest-override data (see Gaps in the digest).
- **New start gates:** see each quest below. Default values come from the mod pages. The console globals they list (e.g. `CCFarmingLevelReq`) were not found in LoreRim's settings mod, so assume the defaults [3][15].

## Quests

### The Cause (ccBGSSSE067_Quest)
- **Vanilla:** starts at level 46, when a courier delivers "Stranger's Plea". The Mythic Dawn is rebuilding an Oblivion Gate, and the follow-up quest is *The Consequences* [21].
- **In LoreRim:** *The Cause Tweaks - Alternate Start* removes the courier. You start the quest by finding a note on the dead Vigilant on the table at the back of the destroyed **Hall of the Vigilant**. Several notes and journals are reworded so your character need not be the Dragonborn [5]. The plugin's note from Skorvild asks you to meet at the Shrine to Stendarr between Fort Greymoor and Rorikstead [5]. The Hall is destroyed by vampires at level 10 or when *Dawnguard* starts [33]. Whether the level-46 gate still applies to the note was not verified [5].
- **Close the Gate** (*The Cause Tweaks - Close The Gate*): the Deadlands gate can now be closed. Kill the two dremora guards and take their weapons, then remove both sigil stones. You are then sent back to Tamriel for good, so loot the area first. The second stone gives a **Transcendent Sigil Stone**, which you use for either a perk point or a permanent enchanting bonus (needs Enchanting 75) [5][1]. If you close the gate without killing the bosses (console `set __ccBGSSSE067_VarAllowClosingAlways to 1`), *The Consequences* is completed but some of its objectives stay unfilled [5].
- **Land of Razors - The Deadlands Redone:** adds ruins, clutter and many more atronachs and dremora to the Deadlands, plus overhauled Valkyn arenas and some new loot [6].
- **Requiem balance (Trad patch):** the Mythic Dawn are strong spellcasters with deadly daedric poisons, and there is an extra daedric crafting manual. The patch also describes an early start from the *Arms of Chaos* summoning circle, but LoreRim removes Arms of Chaos (see Contradictions) [20][1].

### Battle of the Champions (ccFFBSSE001_Quest) / Corpse Check (ccFFBSSE001_CorpseCheck)
- **Vanilla:** starts from a note at The Drunken Huntsman, or by courier once you join a side. You duel the other side's champion near Whiterun and win the Imperial Dragon or Storm-Bear set [24].
- **In LoreRim:** *Civil War Champions - Reduced Cut* removes the duel. A new misc quest, **Civil War Champion Armor** (`CWCRQuest`), has one objective: "Pick up champion armor from your commanding officer". It starts soon after **Battle for Solitude** or **Battle for Windhelm** begins. If it doesn't fire, use `setstage cwcrquest 10` [7][1]. The Trad patch makes Storm-Bear light armor and requires the legendary smithing perk to temper either set [20].

### In the Shadows (ccBGSSSE038_Quest)
- **Vanilla:** ask Whiterun's steward for work and he hands you a note about an assassination plot. The reward is the Bow of Shadows plus gold [23].
- **In LoreRim:** *Bow of Shadows - Reduced Cut* disables the quest. The bow is static loot in the **Severin Family Safe in Severin Manor** (Raven Rock; the mod page calls the container the "Severin Family Chest"), as the prize for beating a Morag Tong assassin. The safe needs Mirri Severin's key, which is easiest to get during *Served Cold*. The bow now counts as a Daedric weapon with Daedric bow speed [8][1].

### A Soul Divided (ccBGSSSE013_AquisitionQuest)
- **Vanilla:** starts by itself when a ghostly apparition in the Ratway leads you toward the Guardian Vault. The reward is Dawnfang and Duskfang [22].
- **In LoreRim:** *CC Dawnfang and Duskfang - No Forced Quest* moves the starting note and activator to a wooden pillar in the canal outside the Ratway. The ghost and its trigger stay disabled until you use that activator, so you can enter the Ratway without starting the quest [9][3].

### Angler Acquaintances (ccBGSSSE001_Start_MQ2), Catch of the Day (ccBGSSSE001_Fish_MQ1), In A Pinch (ccBGSSSE001_Crab_MQ1)
- **Vanilla:** *Angler Acquaintances* starts the first time you visit the Riften Fishery and sends you to Viriya (*In A Pinch*) and Swims-In-Deep-Water (*Catch of the Day*) [29].
- **In LoreRim:** *Delayed Quest Starts - CC Fishing* stops the start on arrival. First catch at least one fish, then talk to **Keerava** at the Bee and Barb ("I'd like to learn about fishing. Could you help me?"). She sends you to the fishery [10]. *Simple Fishing Overhaul* adds third-person fishing and new quest dialogue lines [1][4]. Fish tanks for Myrwatch, Hendraheim, Wintersand Manor and Goldenhills need *Catch of the Day* done [14][13][16][15].

### My Pet Mudcrab, My Pet Nix-Hound, Let Sleeping Wolves Lie, Pets of Skyrim
- **In LoreRim:** *CC Pets - Hide Quest Logs* (the optional log-hiding file, not the full disable) runs these quests without journal entries, and you can still complete them [11][1]. A companion plugin adds a level-requirement property to *Let Sleeping Wolves Lie*; its value was not read [4]. The LoreRim site says CC pet collars and bags now need the first Smithing perk. The mod that does this was not identified [1].

### Hendraheim (ccEEJSSE004_Quest)
- **Vanilla:** a "Warrior's Challenge" courier letter arrives at level 10. You defeat Eydvina Shield-Hearth and take the hall for free [25].
- **In LoreRim:** *Creation Club Home Requirements* holds the summons back until you finish **The Silver Hand** and join the Circle [12][2]. *Hendraheim - Tweaks and Enhancements* buffs Eydvina. After the fight you loot a proof of worth from her body and **buy the hall from Markarth's steward (Raerek or Reburrus Quintilius) for 25,000** (global `cceejsse004_HousePrice`). It also adds a carpenter's workbench, a hirable steward, servants, and a 15-follower dormitory [13]. *Hendraheim TnE* loads after the Home Requirements plugin and overrides the same quest record without listing it as a master, and the CCHR author says TnE needs a separate compatibility patch that LoreRim does not ship [3][4][12]. So the Silver Hand gate is **(disputed)**: the LoreRim site states it, but the install's load order may override it; not tested in-game [2][3].

### Guests for Dinner (ccEEJSSE005_Quest)
- **Vanilla:** a "Dinner Invitation" arrives by courier at level 12. Bloodchill Manor is in Bloodchill Cavern [31].
- **In LoreRim:** you get the invitation only after completing the Dawnguard quest **Bloodline** [12][2].

### Dreams of the Dead (ccRMSSSE001_Quest)
- **Vanilla:** starts when you enter Gallows Hall on the north shore of Mara's Eye Pond [32].
- **In LoreRim:** Gallows Hall stays locked until you finish the **Conjuration Ritual Spell** quest [12][2].

### Myrwatch (ccEEJSSE002_MageTowerQuest) + MyrwatchTNE_Quest
- **Vanilla:** starts on approach east of Morthal. You cast a fire spell at the seal and the tower is yours [26].
- **In LoreRim:** *Myrwatch - Tweaks and Enhancements* adds a misc quest (`MyrwatchTNE_Quest`; the record has no display name) with the objectives "Speak to Tolfdir" and "Purchase Myrwatch". A note in the new vestibule says Tolfdir has to judge you worthy. You qualify by completing **Shalidor's Maze** (Diadem of the Savant), finishing the **College of Winterhold** questline, or passing a very hard Persuasion check. Then you buy the tower for **20,000** [14]. The mod removes the staff enchanter, all-forge, unique displays and late-game loot. Crafting stations are rebuilt at a carpenter's workbench, and the staff enchanter needs *Reluctant Steward* [14]. The Trad patch adds a cursed spectre guarding the outside [20].

### Tundra Homestead (ccEEJSSE001_Quest) → The Wintersand Deception
- **Vanilla:** you buy it from Whiterun's steward for 7,500 [30].
- **In LoreRim:** *CC Tundra Homestead - Tweaks and Enhancements* turns it into **Wintersand Manor**, Nazeem and Ahlam's home, and it is not for sale at first [16]. It goes on sale a few days after both are dead or after you finish **The Wintersand Deception**. A note on the door then sends you to Whiterun's steward [16].
  - Start: read a Thalmor Dossier on the pair. It is in the Thalmor Embassy during *Diplomatic Immunity*, or carried by Agent Lorcalin after *Elisif's Tribute* [16].
  - Objectives: "Talk to the Jarl of Whiterun", then "Obtain additional evidence", then "Return to the Jarl of Whiterun" [16].
  - The evidence is a letter to Elenwen in a safe in a hidden Thalmor meeting room behind the wine cellar. You reach it by picking two Expert locks or lighting two hidden candles in the pool room, and Thalmor guard it. The room connects to **Whitestream Den** [16].
  - Reward: gold from the Jarl, and Nazeem and Ahlam flee [16].
  - A LoreRim xEdit patch (`Tundra TNE - LoreRim Patch.esp`) adds Requiem lockpicking control to the containers and doors [3].

### The Unquiet Dead (ccVSVSSE004_MainQuest)
- **Vanilla:** ask an innkeeper for work or go straight to Goldenhills Plantation. You lay Rin's family to rest and get the key, then *A Farmer's Life For Me* follows [28].
- **In LoreRim:** *CC Farming - Tweaks Enhancements and Quest Expansion* sets a level 15 requirement. Ask an innkeeper in **Rorikstead or Whiterun**: "Are there any problems around town that need handling?" [15][2]. Before the quest the family is alive: Urval sells crops, and Jonquil sells potions and trains Alchemy up to 50 [15]. The quest gains haunted objects and extra steps to find Rin [15]. Afterward you hand in the bounty at Whiterun's steward and **buy the plantation for 10,000** [15]. Farmhands cost 1,000 each, planters can be upgraded, and you can hire guards and a carriage [15]. Up to five beggars can become farmhands. If Narfi becomes one, *Contract: Kill Narfi* does not start, or you can persuade Nazir (Speech 75) [15].

### A Dying Wish (ccKRTSSE001_zQuest)
- **Vanilla:** read "Mysterious Altar" at Dead Man's Drink in Falkreath, then choose a cup at Roadside Ruins. The "Nothing" path leads to Giant's Tooth [27].
- **In LoreRim:** *Bittercup - Tweaks and Enhancements* finishes Giant's Tooth island and adds an accessible upper level to The Pit. Enchanted and high-tier gear lying around is replaced with unenchanted leveled items, and the Bittercup is worth 1000 [17].

### Relics of the Crusader (ccMTYSSE001_DES_RelicsoftheCrusader)
- **In LoreRim:** replaced by *Knight of the North*. Each relic has a journal or note next to it that starts the quest, and the author suggests starting near the Tower Stone. Infamy is replaced by an **Honor** system [18][1]. See `mod-added/knight-of-the-north.md`.

### Gift of Kynareth (ccBGSSSE021_LordsMailQuest)
- **In LoreRim:** *Morihaus' Refuge - No Bruma Version* removes the Redguard-thieves quest. The Lord's Mail is worn by the boss of a new ruin dungeon [19][1]. See `mod-added/morihaus-refuge.md`.

### Saints & Seducers quests, Through a Glass, Darkly, The Edge of Ruin, Put A Fork In It
- **In LoreRim:** these are folded into *Skyrim Extended Cut - Saints and Seducers*, which needs level 20 and *The Mind of Madness* [1][4]. See `mod-added/saints-and-seducers-extended-cut.md`.

### Requiem-adjusted CC quests
*More than you can Chew*, *Night Hunter*, *Crypt of the Heart*, *Interception* and *Unholy Vigil* are overridden by `Requiem - Creation Club.esp`. *The Lost Library*, *Tilted Scales* and *Missing Merchant* are overridden by the Trad Requiem patch [4]. Trad rebalances enemies and loot: Daedric Mail costs 36,000–10,000, and the Umbra boss is stronger [20]. It was not verified which of these quests stay playable.

## Locations
- **Wintersand Manor / Whitestream Den**: the former Tundra Homestead east of Whiterun, and a mudcrab cave reached through its Thalmor room [16][30].
- **Giant's Tooth**: the Bittercup island worldspace, made fully accessible [17].
- **Severin Manor** (Raven Rock): where the Bow of Shadows now is, in the Severin Family Safe [8].
- **Morihaus' Refuge**: the new Lord's Mail dungeon [19].

## LoreRim notes
- **Fixes, not design changes:** the Unofficial CC patch fixes *Ghosts of the Tribunal*, *Legends Lost*, *A Soul Divided*, *Vile Whispers*, *Put A Fork In It*, *Horse Whisperer*, *Beyond the Grave*, *Crypt of the Heart*, *Fortunate Son*, *Forgotten Seasons*, *Pets of Skyrim* and *Corpse Check*. Restored Helmets touches *Missing Merchant* and *Once a Hunter*. Myrwatch House Fix and its USCCCP patches, CC Myrwatch Bug fix, Goldenhills Reset, the CC Farming No Respawn patch, Aspens to Pines and the CFTO Bittercup ferry patch are cell, ferry and stability fixes [4][3].
- Flavor: *Voiced Narrative* voices CC NPCs for Farming, Fishing, Bloodchill Manor, Redguard Elite Armaments, The Gray Cowl Returns and Ghosts of the Tribunal [1][3]. Guard Dialogue Overhaul's CC patch lets guards comment on CC gear [3].
- Verifier correction: the Bow of Shadows container was first written as the "Severin Family Chest" (the mod page's wording); the plugin record names it "Severin Family Safe" [8].
- The LoreRim Player Homes page still says Tundra Homestead is bought from the steward, but the installed THTE requires getting rid of Nazeem and Ahlam first [2][16].
