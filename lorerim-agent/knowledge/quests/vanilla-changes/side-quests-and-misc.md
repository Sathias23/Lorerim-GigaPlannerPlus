# Vanilla Side Quests & Miscellaneous Quests — LoreRim Changes

Skyrim's side quests are the town and dungeon stories outside the guilds and main quest: Blood on the Ice, The Forsworn Conspiracy, The Golden Claw, Lights Out!, The Heart of Dibella, and others [1]. LoreRim's own site says vanilla quests "are very linear with no real choices" and that LoreRim "fixes this greatly expanding the choices the player can make" [14]. The changes come from many small mods, each targeting one quest. This file covers side quests, misc quests and the Blades misc quests. Guild, Daedric, DLC, Thane and Creation Club quests have their own files (see Related).

## Starting in LoreRim

- **The Forsworn Conspiracy is delayed to level 20.** *Delayed Quest Starts - Forsworn Conspiracy* stops the Markarth market attack until you reach the required level and ask **Kibell**, Markarth's carriage driver, "What do you know about Markarth?" and then "Bent a few folks' arms the wrong way?" [16]. After that, entering Markarth between 8 am and 8 pm triggers the attack scene [16]. The mod's default level is 40 (`ANDR_MS01_LevelReq`), but LoreRim's `LoreRim - Global Modifiers.esp` loads later and sets it to **20** [16][17]. In vanilla, the attack plays as soon as you enter the market [5].
- **Rumors no longer force quests into your journal.** *The Choice is Yours* stops random greetings, comments, rumors and forced encounters from starting quests. A quest begins only when you agree to help [19]. In LoreRim it covers Missing In Action, Rise in the East, Laid to Rest, Supply and Demand, Soljund's Sinkhole, Wizard Duel, A Lovely Letter, The Raid and Buy Dwarven artifact, among others [19]. Map markers to quest givers no longer appear before you accept, so you have to listen to the NPCs [19].
- **Level gates from Timing is Everything.** LoreRim's Timing is Everything settings put The Wolf Queen Awakened at level 10 and Unfathomable Depths at level 14 [18]. These match vanilla (UESP: level 10 and level 14) [2][3]. The Ebony Warrior is set to **level 40**, against 80 in vanilla, in both the settings file and `LoreRim - Global Modifiers.esp` [4][17][18]. *Mr. Ebony... Get Lost* also lets you decline that duel [14]; see [dragonborn.md](dragonborn.md).
- **Seeking A Cure (vampirism cure) no longer starts from inn rumors.** You either talk to Urag gro-Shub at the College of Winterhold or go straight to Falion's house in Morthal [31].

## Quests

### The Forsworn Conspiracy (MS01)
- **Vanilla:** A Forsworn witchblade attacks Margret in Markarth's market, and Eltrys's note pulls you into Thonar Silver-Blood's conspiracy. The quest ends in Cidhna Mine [5].
- **In LoreRim:** Starts at level 20 via Kibell, as described above [16][17]. Before the attack, Eltrys and Weylin can be found around town, but Margret has not arrived yet [16]. *The Choice is Yours* adds that you "must meet at night to advance quest" [19]. USSEP fixes also apply [34].

### The Golden Claw (MS13)
- **Vanilla:** Lucan Valerius (Riverwood Trader) asks you to recover the claw from Bleak Falls Barrow. The reward is leveled gold of 400–1,200 [8].
- **In LoreRim:** *Silence is Golden* lets you lie to Lucan about finding the claw. The quest still completes, but you get no reward except keeping the claw, and Lucan is not your friend [22]. *Buyable Golden Claw* lets you buy the claw after returning it, from Lucan or from Camilla if he is dead, for 500–1,500 gold depending on level [22]. *Golden Claw - More Choices* is the compatibility patch between those two mods [22]. The LoreRim site lists all three as "Decide whether to keep the Golden Claw" [14].

### A Lovely Letter (FreeformRiverwood01)
- **Vanilla:** Sven or Faendal asks you to deliver a forged letter to Camilla. The reward is 25 gold and that man as a follower, and the other man is alienated [9].
- **In LoreRim:** *A Lovely Letter Alternate Routes* lets you refuse both men. The start dialogue is hidden if Camilla is your lover. If you talk to both and pickpocket back the first note, you can expose both of them [20]. The LoreRim site describes this as gaining "Camilla's favour for yourself" [14]. *The Choice is Yours* also edits this quest [19]. *More to Say* lets you repair your relationship with Sven or Faendal afterwards. Its follow-up misc quest **The Heart Will Go On** ("Deliver Sven's ballad to Camilla") unlocks after The Golden Claw and A Lovely Letter [21].

### Lights Out! (MS07)
- **Vanilla:** Jaree-Ra pays you to douse the Solitude Lighthouse so the Icerunner wrecks. The quest ends with a fight against Deeja at the wreck and against Jaree-Ra at Broken Oar Grotto [6].
- **In LoreRim:** *Save the Icerunner - Lights Out Alternate Routes* adds routes [23]:
  - The **Icerunner returns** if Jaree-Ra or Deeja dies before you hear the offer (the quest never starts), if Jaree-Ra dies before you put out the fire (the quest fails), if you convince Jaree-Ra to give up, or if the guards jail him [23].
  - To unlock these routes, find evidence and use it against Jaree-Ra or report it to Captain Aldis. Ahtar the headsman has a quest that points you to Broken Oar Grotto [23].
  - A route that still crashes the ship makes Jaree-Ra and Deeja friendly. Jaree-Ra can then be a follower (Rogue, max level 50), or either sibling can become your spouse [23].
  - Istar Cairn-Breaker replaces Aldis if the Stormcloaks hold Haafingar [23].

### Missing In Action (MS09), Rise in the East (MS10), Laid to Rest (MS14)
- **Vanilla:** Missing In Action is the Gray-Mane rescue in Whiterun. Rise in the East is the East Empire Company's pirate job in Windhelm. Laid to Rest is the Morthal house-fire investigation [1].
- **In LoreRim:** *The Choice is Yours* gates all three behind you agreeing to help [19]. In Missing In Action, you must also visit the Gray-Mane home at night to advance the quest [19]. Laid to Rest has a second role in LoreRim: completing it is the prerequisite for the Dawnguard recruiter ("Sensible Quest Prerequisites") [15]. See [dawnguard.md](dawnguard.md).

### Telrav's Request (dunNilheimQST)
- **Vanilla:** Telrav asks for an escort, and at Nilheim his bandit gang ambushes you. There is no formal reward [13].
- **In LoreRim:** *Nilheim - Misc Quest Expansion* changes several things [25]:
  - Telrav gives his full pitch first. You can then help him, leave, or press him for more information.
  - Pressing him unlocks an intimidation check to get cut in for gold, or calling him a liar, which makes him attack.
  - The bandits turn hostile when you approach the camp.
  - A journal by Telrav describing the "operation" is on a table.
  - Killing the bandits or finding the journal first opens new dialogue.
- *No Infighting Bandits* fixes the bandits turning on each other if you kill Telrav first [25].

### Infiltration (dunTrevasWatchQST)
- **Vanilla:** Stalleo asks you to enter Treva's Watch by the back tunnel and pull the gate lever. The reward is Spell Tome: Detect Life [11].
- **In LoreRim:** *Infiltration - Quest Expansion* is voiced, with spliced lines and a new voice actor [26]:
  - You hear out both sides (Stalleo and the bandit Brurid). You can side with either, try to save everyone, or kill everyone. The quest has "no dead ends" [26].
  - Stalleo can come inside with you and comment as you go [26].
  - You can no longer pull the lever from outside; you must enter by the back door [26].

### Search and Seizure (FreeformMarkarthM)
- **Vanilla:** Ondolemar sends you to find Ogmund's Amulet of Talos as proof of Talos worship [27][34].
- **In LoreRim:** *Search and Seizure - Quest Expansion* makes locating the contraband optional and adds endings [27]:
  - Warn Ogmund and lie to the Thalmor.
  - Warn him and report to the Thalmor.
  - Warn him and turn in the contraband.
  - Turn in the contraband (the vanilla ending).
- Killing the NPCs still fails the quest [27].

### The Heart of Dibella (T01)
- **Vanilla:** You are caught in the Temple of Dibella's Inner Sanctum, and Mother Hamal sends you to rescue Fjotra from the Forsworn at Broken Tower Redoubt. The reward is Agent of Dibella (+10% damage against the opposite sex) [10].
- **In LoreRim:** *The Heart of Dibella - Quest Expansion* adds an optional start [28]:
  - **Become Thane of the Reach and sleep.** A courier then brings Hamal's letter, so you don't have to break in.
  - Alternatively, help at least five people in the Reach.
- Other changes [28]:
  - If you sided with the Forsworn in No One Escapes Cidhna Mine and Madanach is alive, he can free Fjotra without bloodshed.
  - You can tell Hamal you won't get involved. This fails the quest and clears it from your journal.
  - A voiced talk with the priestess lets you change the blessing to same sex, both sexes (5% each), or no one.
  - Empty journal stages are filled in.

### Caught Red Handed (FreeformRiften11) + A Strong Nord Woman
- **Vanilla:** Svana Far-Shield has you collect three Marks of Dibella from Bolli, Indaryn and Hofgrir to shame Haelga. The rewards are a scroll and enchanted heavy armor [12].
- **In LoreRim:** *Caught Red Handed - Quest Expansion* lets you refuse to shame Haelga and get on her good side instead [29]:
  - Haelga's "lessons" are available by Speech check or as an Agent of Dibella. They give an "Experienced Lover's Comfort" bonus and a craftable Mark of Dibella necklace with 20% better prices [29].
  - The mod also adds the misc quest **A Strong Nord Woman**: "Talk to Tythis" → "Win the brawl against Tythis" or "Threaten Tythis" → "Talk to Svana" [29].
- Requiem also overrides this quest record [34]. See [caught-red-handed.md](../mod-added/caught-red-handed.md).

### Toying With The Dead (FreeformRiften21)
- **Vanilla:** A Thieves Guild misc quest. Vekel wants Arondil's journals from Yngvild [30][34].
- **In LoreRim:** *Toying With The Dead - minor Quest Expansion* moves it to its own journal entry as a side quest [30]:
  - An optional objective lets you report the Dawnstar disappearances to the Jarl of the Pale (Skald or Brina Merilis). The reward is gold and progress toward Thane [30].
  - Do this **before** handing the journals to Vekel, because you need them as proof [30].

### Seeking A Cure (VC01, vanilla "Rising at Dawn")
- **Vanilla:** "Rising at Dawn": as a vampire, inn rumors send you to Falion in Morthal. He cures you at dawn for a filled black soul gem, and the quest is repeatable [7].
- **In LoreRim:** *Seeking The Cure* renames the quest **Seeking A Cure** and makes vampirism effectively incurable [31][34]:
  - **Falion's ritual now fails.** The quest is non-repeatable [31].
  - It starts via Urag gro-Shub or Falion's house [31].
  - Falion researches for three days and no longer sells a Black Soul Gem; there are no markers for finding one [31].
  - You can optionally kill Falion afterward [31].
  - Falion carries an "Experimental Potion" for debugging [31].
- The COTN Morthal patch makes the quest trigger inside Cities of the North's version of Falion's house. If it doesn't trigger, let Falion talk to you first [31]. The LoreRim site confirms the ritual fails [15]. See [seeking-the-cure.md](../mod-added/seeking-the-cure.md).

### Collecting the Edda (BardsCollegePoeticEdda)
- **Vanilla:** An unfinished cut quest ("Collect parts of the Poetic Edda from Bards you meet") [32][34].
- **In LoreRim:** *Collecting the Edda* restores it [32]:
  - After Tending the Flames, Giraud Gemane gives the quest. Talk to any five bards across Skyrim for their Edda pieces [32].
  - The rewards are leveled gold and a Speech bonus [32].
- *Bards College Excavation* adds a repeatable **Clear Dead Men's Respite** misc quest after Tending the Flames [33]. See [bards-college-excavation.md](../mod-added/bards-college-excavation.md).

### Blades misc quests: Rebuilding the Blades, Dragon Hunting, Dragon Research
- **In LoreRim:** *Dragon Hunting* changes the three Blades quests [33]:
  - After Rebuilding the Blades, Esbern can give the **Blessing of the Blades** (+25% weapon enchantments against dragons for 8 hours) [33].
  - **Dragon Hunting** can be repeated every 24 in-game hours [33].
  - **Dragon Research** now needs Dragon Blood, Dragon Bile and Dragon Rheum for the Dragon Infusion (10% less damage from dragons) [33].
- *Paarthurnax - Quest Expansion* also edits these records, alongside its spare-or-kill rework of Paarthurnax [33][15]. See [main-quest-and-alternate-start.md](main-quest-and-alternate-start.md) and [dragon-hunting.md](../mod-added/dragon-hunting.md).

### Smaller changes
- **In My Time Of Need (MS08):** *Saadia to Iman* renames Saadia to "Iman" once she reveals her identity, and updates the later journal entries [24].
- **Extracting an Argonian (DarkwaterCrossingDerkeethusRescueQuest; the record has no in-game name, "Extracting an Argonian" is the UESP title [34]):** *Finding Derkeethus* makes several changes [24]:
  - It moves Derkeethus's cell key out of his own cell into the enchanter room.
  - It moves the quest out of the Misc tab.
  - It adds dialogue to refuse the quest or ask for a reward.
  - It can add a marker for Darkwater Pass.
- **A Bad Trade (FreeformWinterholdA):** Overridden by *More to Say*, which adds Birna's follow-up **Birna's Shipment** [21].
- **Siege on the Dragon Cult (dunForelhostQST):** *Immersive Dialogue Expansion - Thalmor* expands the Forelhost Thalmor impostor plot "with new paths and endings" [24].
- **The White Phial:** *Immersive Dying Nurelion* replaces the "asleep and dying" message box with groans and dialogue [24].

## Locations
- **Broken Oar Grotto** (Solitude coast). This is the key to Lights Out!'s alternate routes; Ahtar's quest points you there [23].
- **Dead Men's Respite** (Hjaalmarch). After Tending the Flames, a Bards College excavation camp with Rothen and Birinna appears here [33].

## Rewards & notable items
- Notable new rewards: keeping or buying back the Golden Claw [22]; Jaree-Ra as a follower, or Jaree-Ra or Deeja as a spouse [23]; Haelga's Mark of Dibella necklace [29]; reworked Blades blessings [33]; the Edda's gold and Speech bonus [32].

## LoreRim notes
- **Fixes, not design changes:** Many quests here are overridden only by the Unofficial Skyrim Special Edition Patch, the Unofficial Skyrim Modder's Patch or "Official Master Files - Cleaned Plugins" [34]. These include The Man Who Cried Wolf / The Wolf Queen Awakened, Unfathomable Depths, Blood on the Ice, No One Escapes Cidhna Mine, The White Phial, Repairing the Phial, Tending the Flames, The Book of Love, Forbidden Legend, The Lost Expedition, Kyne's Sacred Trials and most Freeform misc quests. *JS Instruments of Skyrim SE* touches the Bards College instrument quests (it is a mesh and texture replacer), and *ArteFakes* touches Gharol's Message [34].
- **Requiem** overrides the Bounty quests (BQ01–BQ04), the Treasure Hunter objectives (WE100Obj*), Climb the Steps, Stoking the Flames, Pilgrimage and Caught Red Handed [34]. The install evidence does not show what these edits change (unverified); see [requiem-quests.md](../mod-added/requiem-quests.md).
- **Blood on the Ice** has no delayed start in LoreRim. Only five Delayed Quest Starts modules ship: Forsworn Conspiracy, House of Horrors, Mind of Madness, Taste of Death and CC Fishing. The Blood on the Ice delay from the same author is absent [16].
- See also [vittorias-alternate-wedding.md](../mod-added/vittorias-alternate-wedding.md), [book-of-love-fastreds-awakening.md](../mod-added/book-of-love-fastreds-awakening.md) and [sissels-book.md](../mod-added/sissels-book.md).
