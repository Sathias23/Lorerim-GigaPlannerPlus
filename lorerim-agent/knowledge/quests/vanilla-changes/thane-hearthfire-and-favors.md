# Thaneships, Hearthfire Homes and Favors (LoreRim changes)

In vanilla Skyrim you become a thane by befriending a Jarl, helping the hold's people (five in holds with a major city, three elsewhere) and, in Whiterun, Riften, Windhelm, Solitude and Markarth, buying a house; the reward is the title, a thane weapon and usually a housecarl [1]. Hearthfire adds three buildable homes on land bought in Falkreath Hold, Hjaalmarch and the Pale, starting from a courier letter at level 9 [5][6]. Radiant "favors" raise an NPC's disposition and count toward the "help the people" step [7]. LoreRim keeps all of these routes; its mods change how many favors can run at once, what the thane rewards are, who the housecarls are before you are named thane, what counts as "helping", and how Hearthfire building and bounties are balanced [10][11][13][14][15][16].

## Starting in LoreRim

- **No new gates on thaneship.** None of LoreRim's quest-gating mods (the "Sensible … Prerequisite" and "Delayed Quest Starts" mods) target the thane or Hearthfire quests; the thane quests are overridden only by bug fixes, Requiem and Unique Thane Weapons [10][18].
- **Level gates are the vanilla ones, set through Timing is Everything.** Timing is Everything SE can change the Hearthfire start and the "Kill the Giant" level requirement, which controls when you can buy land in the Pale [12]. Its default settings are the vanilla values [12]: Hearthfire minimum level 9, Kill the Giant 22, Dungeon Delving 20, Kill the Vampire 10 (`settings.ini` in the Settings Loader) [12]. LoreRim's "LoreRim - MCM and INI Settings" mod ships no `TimingIsEverything.ini` override, so these defaults apply unless you change them in the MCM (medium confidence) [12][18]. They match UESP's vanilla numbers: letter at level 9, Kill the Vampire level 10, Dungeon Delving (Jarl - Hagravens) level 20, Kill the Giant (Jarl) level 22 [6][8][9][5].
- **What counts as helping the hold.** Vanilla favors count, except in the Rift, which uses its own counter of Riften quests [7]. In LoreRim, NPCs you complete Missives jobs for also count toward "people helped" for thaneships [15]. (That mod was written for "Harder Thaneships", but LoreRim does not ship Harder Quests, so the vanilla 5/3 thresholds stand — medium confidence [15][18].)
- **Quests start only when you accept them.** LoreRim ships The Choice is Yours (it stops greetings and rumors from forcing quests into your journal) and the Favor Quests Separated patch for it [19][11].
- **LoreRim site wording.** LoreRim's Player Homes guide says Honeyside, Vlindrel Hall, Proudspire Manor and Hjerim are bought "after becoming Thane", Breezehome after "Bleak Falls Barrow", Lakeview Manor "after helping the Jarl (you will recieve a letter from a courier)", and Windstad Manor and Heljarchen Hall after becoming Thane of Hjaalmarch or the Pale [17]. See **LoreRim notes** for where the plugin records order this differently.

## Quests

### Thane of Whiterun (Favor253)
- **Vanilla:** Balgruuf makes you thane after Dragon Rising. If Vignar Gray-Mane becomes Jarl (Stormcloak "Battle for Whiterun"), you regain the title by helping five people and buying Breezehome (objective "Purchase a house in Whiterun") [4][10]. The reward is a leveled thane weapon and Lydia [1][4].
- **In LoreRim:** Unique Thane Weapons (loads last of the overriding mods) swaps in fixed unique weapons [13][18]. **Blade of Whiterun** is a greatsword that can be reforged into a one-handed sword at the Skyforge (Steel smithing perk) and upgraded to the **Gilded Blade of Whiterun** (Orcish smithing perk). It adds 15/30 fire damage and uses no enchantment charge. **Axe of Whiterun** is "the weapon you get when you re-gain thaneship of Whiterun" [13]. The plugin also edits Dragon Rising, so the Blade comes from the Balgruuf thaneship (medium confidence) [13]. With Housecarls Pre-Thaneship, Lydia already lives her own life around Whiterun (Dragonsreach, the market, the Bannered Mare, the Skyforge) before you are named thane [14].

### I Done Got Thaned! (FreeformRiftenThane)
- **Vanilla:** Speak to the Jarl of Riften, then "Purchase Honeyside from Riften's Steward", then "Return to the Jarl of Riften" [10]. Prerequisites are the Jarl's three skooma quests plus three Riften miscellaneous quests [3]. The housecarl is Iona. In vanilla the "Blade of the Rift" is bugged and comes out as a generic iron weapon (fixed by the Unofficial Patch) [3].
- **In LoreRim:** Unique Thane Weapons makes the reward **Black Blade of the Rift**, a short sword with fast attacks and 3 poison damage per second for 15 s [13]. Requiem also patches this quest; its changelog says it "grants the correct version of the Blade of the Rift" (3.1.1) [16]. Iona gets a pre-thane schedule in Riften (Bee and Barb, docks, market, temple, Mistveil Keep, Honeyside) [14].

### Thane of Eastmarch (Favor254)
- **Vanilla:** Given by Ulfric or Brunwulf, whoever is Jarl. Requires the civil-war prerequisite ("Blood on the Ice" for Stormcloaks or "Battle for Windhelm" for Imperials), five people helped and the purchase of Hjerim. Rewards are Calder and the Axe of Eastmarch [2][10].
- **In LoreRim:** The reward is the **Ebony Warhammer of Eastmarch**, the only ebony-tier thane weapon: 50% chance per attribute to absorb 25 Health/Stamina/Magicka [13]. Requiem also overrides the record [10]. Calder exists before thaneship (Housecarls Pre-Thaneship) [14]. The LoreRim site lists "Blood on the Ice" for Hjerim [17].

### Thane of Haafingar (Favor252)
- **Vanilla:** Befriend Elisif ("Elisif's Tribute"), help five people, and buy Proudspire Manor ("Purchase a house in Solitude"). Jordis the Sword-Maiden is the housecarl [1][10].
- **In LoreRim:** The reward is the **Sword of Haafingar**, a glass-tier greatsword that can be reforged to one-handed. It deals 15 shock damage plus 10 sun damage, triple sun damage against undead [13]. Requiem also overrides the record [10]. Jordis has a pre-thane life [14].

### Thane of the Reach (Favor250)
- **Vanilla:** If Igmund is Jarl, his favor "Dungeon Delving (Jarl - Hagravens)" (level 20) unlocks buying Vlindrel Hall and leads to Thane of the Reach [9]. Objective: "Purchase a house in Markarth" [10]. Argis the Bulwark is the housecarl [1].
- **In LoreRim:** The reward is the **Dwarven Axe of the Reach**, a glass-tier battleaxe whose attacks stagger, with attack speed lowered to warhammer speed [13]. Requiem also overrides the record [10]. Argis has a pre-thane life [14].

### Thane of Falkreath (Favor258)
- **Vanilla:** Jarl Siddgeir's favors ("Rare Gifts" and "Kill the Bandit Leader") lead to the title. Rewards are Rayya and Lakeview Manor land; there is no city house to buy (objective "Return to <QuestGiver>" only) [1][10].
- **In LoreRim:** The reward is the **Hunter Axe of Falkreath**, a two-handed axe (reforgeable to a war axe) with +30 damage to animals [13]. Rayya appears around Falkreath (Dead Man's Drink, outside the Jarl's hall) before thaneship [14].

### Thane of Hjaalmarch (Favor255)
- **Vanilla:** Completing "Laid to Rest" for Jarl Idgrod leads to the title and to Windstad Manor land. Valdimar is the housecarl [1][5].
- **In LoreRim:** The reward is the **Silver Warscythe of Hjaalmarch**, a glass-tier silver battleaxe that absorbs 20 Stamina [13]. Requiem also overrides the record [10]. Laid to Rest is also LoreRim's gate for the Dawnguard recruiter (Sensible Dawnguard Prerequisite) [20]; see vanilla-changes/dawnguard.md.

### Thane of the Pale (Favor256)
- **Vanilla:** After "Waking Nightmare", the Jarl's favor "Kill the Giant (Jarl)" (level 22) unlocks Heljarchen Hall land and the title. Gregor is the housecarl [5][1].
- **In LoreRim:** The reward is the **Evening Star of the Pale**, a fast glass-tier warhammer that causes bleeding [13]. Requiem also overrides the record [10].

### Thane of Winterhold (Favor257)
- **Vanilla:** Earned through a Jarl dungeon-retrieval favor (UESP: "Find the Helm of Winterhold"). There is no house and no housecarl [1].
- **In LoreRim:** The reward is the **Blade of Winterhold**, a glass-tier sword (reforgeable to a greatsword) with a 50% chance each of 20 fire, frost or shock damage [13]. Requiem also overrides the record [10].

### Build Your Own Home (Hearthfire)
- **Vanilla:** At level 9 a courier brings a letter, from the steward if you are friends with the Jarl of Falkreath or Hjaalmarch, or from the Jarl of the Pale; otherwise it comes from Falkreath [6]. Each plot costs 5,000 gold [6]. Falkreath requires "Kill the Bandit Leader (Jarl)" while Siddgeir is Jarl; Hjaalmarch requires "Laid to Rest" while Idgrod is Jarl; the Pale requires "Kill the Giant (Jarl)" [5].
- **In LoreRim:** The level-9 trigger is Timing is Everything's default (see Starting) [12]. Requiem changes building [16]:
  - Hearthfire is "fully integrated"; the houses are available at low levels, but you still owe the Jarls favors (2.0.0).
  - Each Hearthfire house has at least three stone quarries nearby, yielding 32 stone, and general merchants in major cities sell clay and quarried stone (2.0.2).
  - "Building a Hearthfire house doesn't pass time" (5.2.0). This supersedes the 2.0.0 note that building takes time.

  Hearthfires Houses Building Fix restores correct preview models, labels exterior parts "exterior", and stops you building second-floor walls before first-floor walls [21]. Pre-thane versions of the three Hearthfire housecarls are also added [14].

### Favor quests: Delivery (Favor001), A Few Words with You (Favor013), Some Light Theft (Favor018), Kill the Bandit Leader (Favor104), Rare Gifts (Favor110), Dungeon Delving (Bandits) (Favor204), Dungeon Delving (Caves) (Favor205)
- **Vanilla:** Each is a single shared quest, so only one instance of each type can be active anywhere in Skyrim at a time [7][11].
- **In LoreRim:** Favor Quests Separated splits them into 32 independent quests, so they can all run at once [11]. The location-specific copies in `Favor Quests Seperated.esp` keep the vanilla names [11]:
  - Delivery: Windhelm, Angas Mill, Markarth, Morthal, Darkwater Crossing, Falkreath.
  - A Few Words with You: Whiterun, Windhelm, Winterhold, Kynesgrove, Solitude, Markarth.
  - Some Light Theft: Falkreath, Winterhold, Windhelm.
  - Kill the Bandit Leader: Solitude, Darkwater Crossing, Windhelm, Skaal Village.
  - Rare Gifts: Solitude, Morthal, Dawnstar, Falkreath, Windhelm, Whiterun.
  - Dungeon Delving (Bandits): Whiterun, Windhelm.
  - Dungeon Delving (Caves): Dawnstar, Solitude, Windhelm, Kynesgrove, Falkreath.

  The author's example: Amren's family sword (Whiterun) and Shahvee's Amulet of Zenithar (Windhelm) can now be active together [11]. Objectives are unchanged, for example "Steal the <QuestItem> for <QuestGiver>" and "Bring one <QuestItem> to <QuestGiver>" [11].

### Jarl favors: Kill the Vampire (Favor109), Kill the Giant (Jarl) (Favor153), Dungeon Delving (Jarl - Hagravens) (Favor157), Dungeon Delving (Jarl - Caves) (Favor158)
- **Vanilla:** Kill the Vampire is given by Sybille Stentor (Blue Palace) at level 10 and counts toward Thane of Haafingar [8]. Kill the Giant (Jarl) is the Pale's level-22 favor [5]. Dungeon Delving (Jarl - Hagravens) is Igmund's level-20 favor [9].
- **In LoreRim:** Timing is Everything overrides Favor109, Favor153 and Favor157 so their level gates come from its MCM; the defaults are 10, 22 and 20 (vanilla) [12][10]. Requiem also overrides Favor157 and loads after Timing is Everything [10][18]. Favor158 is touched only by the cleaned master files [10].

### Bounty quests ("Bounty: Bandit Boss", etc.)
- **Vanilla:** Jarls and stewards hand out bounties on bandits, Forsworn, giants and dragons [11].
- **In LoreRim:** Requiem overrides the vanilla bounty quests (BQ01–BQ04) [10]. Its changelog says giant bounties pay 1000 gold (3.2.0); the 2.0.2 change that paid a large part of bounty rewards in gems was reverted in 3.2.0 ("quest rewards yielding a mix of gold and gems have been reverted to pure gold rewards") [16]. Favor Quests Separated contains per-hold bounty variants, such as "Bounty: Draugr", "Bounty: Warlock", "Bounty: Spriggan", "Bounty: Falmer", "Bounty: Hagraven" and "Bounty: Vampire" [11]. The author says those only activate with the optional "Bounty Quests" file [11]. LoreRim's Favor Quests Separated folder contains only the main plugin, so the per-hold bounty variants are most likely **dormant** and bounties follow vanilla + Requiem (medium confidence) [11][18].

## Locations
- **Breezehome, Honeyside, Hjerim, Proudspire Manor, Vlindrel Hall** — the five thane city houses. See the LoreRim site for sellers (Proventus Avenicci, Anuriel, Jorleif, Falk Firebeard, Raerek/Reburrus Quintilius) [17].
- **Lakeview Manor, Windstad Manor, Heljarchen Hall** — Hearthfire plots in Falkreath Hold, Hjaalmarch and the Pale [5][17]. Their areas are covered in areas/falkreath-hold.md, areas/hjaalmarch-and-morthal.md and areas/the-pale-and-dawnstar.md.

## Rewards & notable items
- The Unique Thane Weapons set is listed under each thane quest above. No weapon exceeds ebony tier, and the reforgeable ones need the Steel smithing perk [13].
- Requiem: housecarls carry a helmet and wear Varied Guard Armor designs (4.0.0). The Jarl's Longhouses in Dawnstar, Falkreath, Morthal and Winterhold have master-locked treasure chests in the Jarl's room (4.0.0) [16].

## LoreRim notes
- **Bug fixes (not design changes):** the Unofficial Skyrim Special Edition Patch and Official Master Files - Cleaned Plugins override the thane quests, several favors and many Hearthfire records (BYOHHouse*, BYOHRelationshipAdoption*) [10].
- **Hearthfire family fixes:** Adoption Spouse and Moving Fixes repairs half-adoption soft-locks and family moves to Creation Club/AE houses, retroactively [21]. Prince and The Pauper Refine also edits the adoption records [10]. Bluesky Hall and Fort Valus Allow Adoptions makes those homes adoption-capable [18]. Voiced Hearthfires Stewards and Missing Voices in Hearthfire Added Back add steward/child lines for Khajiit, Argonian, female Dark Elf and female Orc voices [21].
- **Verifier correction (2026-10-02):** an earlier draft said Requiem pays a large part of bounty rewards in gems (changelog 2.0.2); the installed `Changelog.md` shows that was reverted to pure gold in 3.2.0 [16].
- **Patches:** Favor Quests Separated ships with its The Choice Is Yours patch and an NGCDT dialogue patch [11][18].
- **Contradiction — thane-then-buy vs buy-to-become-thane:** the LoreRim site says the city houses are bought "after becoming Thane" [17]. The plugin records make the purchase an objective inside the thane quests ("Purchase a house in Whiterun/Solitude/Markarth/Windhelm", "Purchase Honeyside from Riften's Steward"), so in practice you buy the house to finish becoming thane [10]. For Lakeview Manor the site says "after helping the Jarl" while UESP names "Kill the Bandit Leader (Jarl)" [17][5].
- **Contradiction — Whiterun weapon names:** UESP gives the Vignar regain quest the "Blade of Whiterun" [4]. Unique Thane Weapons says the Axe of Whiterun is the regain weapon and the Blade is the early one [13]. In LoreRim, follow the Unique Thane Weapons naming.
- **Unit note:** the mod "In My Time Of Need Early" was filed with this unit but affects the Saadia quest "In My Time Of Need", not thaneship. LoreRim installs its "Before the Storm" version, so that quest starts after Before the Storm instead of Dragon Rising [20]. See vanilla-changes/side-quests-and-misc.md.
- **Creation Club homes** (Hendraheim, Bloodchill Manor, Gallows Hall) are gated by Creation Club Home Requirements; see vanilla-changes/creation-club.md [20].
